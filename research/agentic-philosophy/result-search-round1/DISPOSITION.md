## What the modal route now contributes

The source excludes histories consisting wholly of choice points. The
comb exposes the distinction between this and dense choice: a proper
dense branch set leaves non-choice moments while distinguishing every
pair of trunk moments by their enabling transitions. Exact computation
gives singleton presents on that history.

The coordinator generalized the calculation to arbitrary branch set D:
for s<t, CC(s)=CC(t) iff D∩[s,t)=∅. Within the nonempty proper-D comb
family, all histories have nontrivial co-presentness iff D is not dense.
This is an exact family-level criterion, not a general BST repair theorem.

One original error and one review error had the same source: omitting
earlier continued transitions from offshoot CC by changing an existential
outside-history witness into a universal demand. The explicit h₀/h₁
counterexample corrected both. Agreement was not used to certify truth.
