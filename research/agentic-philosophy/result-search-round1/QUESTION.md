# Round1: what warrants an extended present?

31 October 2026. Source-first question card. No preferred temporal ontology.

**Target:** identify a defensible argument, counterexample or conceptual
revision about when a physically or modally individuated temporal extent
deserves an ontological reading as a present. The route's exact conclusion
must be stated before its derivation. A full temporal ontology is not the
minimum deliverable, and mathematical novelty is not mandatory.

**Shared constraints:** mathematical representation is not concrete existence;
determination is not constitution; genuine process unity does not by itself
assert simultaneous/current concreteness of every temporal part; an inclusion
requirement does not entail exclusion of other events. Countermodels to
entailment do not settle an abductive preference. Respect system-relative
views and do not turn them into a single global inventory by changing quantifiers.

**Nearest sources:** Brading2015 §5 pp17–18, public journal-layout copy in
the [existing primary register](../../article-admissibility/primary-review/SOURCES.md);
Guarino–Guizzardi2024 §§2–3, DOI10.1007/s11229-024-04505-2; Müller2019/2020
§§2.2–2.3,3–4, DOI10.1007/s10701-019-00267-x. These are different approaches,
not interchangeable theories. Exact selected-reading records are in
[sources.jsonl](sources.jsonl). The nearest-third-predecessor comparison is
route specific; record unread sources as gaps rather than verified predecessors.

**Route A — occurrence:** supply one independently motivated defense of the
claim that an ongoing process's elapsed constitution is concrete from b.
Distinguish the premise from genuine unity and the three predicates named
in the main plan. Test Guarino–Guizzardi's flight/constitution account before
claiming that a rival must abandon process unity. Use a second process case.
Alternatively give a genuinely new defeating case and its consequence.

**Route B — modal nontriviality:** inspect the claim in Müller §2.3 that a
non-choice moment ensures some non-singleton dynamic present, and Theorem1's
featureless restriction. Candidate stress test: a real-line trunk with one
deterministic offshoot at every rational trunk point. Maximal histories are
the trunk and the once-diverging paths. Test whether its irrational trunk
points are non-choice while distinct trunk points have different sets of
containing histories. Check every relevant BT/BST axiom, empty-chain convention,
Weiner's postulate where claimed, and any implicit manifold restrictions.
Do not call it a counterexample to a relativistic theorem without that audit.

**Coordinator — entity and semantic correspondence:** inspect the old pilot,

This is not a second worker attempt at either mathematical proof or P2 defense.

**Return:** one exact candidate, decisive test, nearest-predecessor comparison,
strongest rival, independent consequence and falsifier. At most one revision
