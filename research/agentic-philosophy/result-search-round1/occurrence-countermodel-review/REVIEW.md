# Independent review: regular-open occurrence countermodel

Checked 31 October 2026. Primary source read at full-text level: Guarino & Guizzardi, “Processes as variable embodiments,” version of record (20 March 2024), DOI 10.1007/s11229-024-04505-2, §§2–3.2, A1–A12, V1′–V3′, and footnotes 10–17. Locators and source limits are in `sources.jsonl`.

## Verdict

The regular-open construction is a credible model of the displayed first-order constitution fragment under its stated sorted, category-relative mereology. Its key counterexample works: at the later interval the same process has a cumulative manifestation; an earlier event is its part; the process is interval-existing; yet the new inventory predicate excludes that earlier event. A1–A12 and V1′–V3′, as displayed, contain no predicate for “concrete from the current standpoint” and therefore do not entail P4 or the conclusion. Event-part inheritance P5 can remain true. This is a useful counterinterpretation of entailment, not a completed alternative metaphysics or a source-based denial that the earlier event exists.

The source-faithful scope must be stated narrowly. The paper describes its account as descriptive ontology, says process kinds cognitively carve processes from events, and presents §§4–5 unity and individuation principles as crucial to understanding process kinds. The model identifies a token across two times by stipulation and does not supply one of those unity/individuation principles, the paper’s taxonomy, or a physical criterion for selecting manifestations. It thus demonstrates logical independence from the displayed A/V apparatus, not a rival satisfying every commitment of the whole paper or a proof that an ongoing token can be metaphysically unified on the proposed presentist reading.

Recommendation: stop this occurrence family as a positive novelty/result candidate, consistent with the coordinator’s provisional stop. Retain the bounded formal-fragment result and the source/interpretation distinction as a correction. Do not represent the result as an ontology-level countermodel or as independently establishing presentism.

## Clause audit and corrections

- **A1–A5, A8, V1′, V3′:** The construction’s functional K, one-way typed process-to-event constitution, disjoint sorts, and existence iff manifestation are compatible with the displayed clauses. V3′ can be satisfied by the stated extensional identity criterion for the manifestation profiles. These facts do not supply a principle of unity beyond that criterion.
- **A6:** Allen interval semantics is used correctly for the cases described. With I₀=[0,1], I₁=[1,2], and event hulls ending at or before the assessment interval’s left endpoint, the event either Meets or is Before the interval. Events are open sets while Allen relations apply to their temporal hulls; this is a representation choice which should be said explicitly. No point-time shortcut is needed.
- **A7:** For the repeated token, the hulls of Y₀ and Y₁ have the same start and strictly increasing ends, so the event hull for Y₀ Starts the hull for Y₁ under the usual strict Allen definition. Y₀⊂Y₁ gives event parthood. This checks the asserted pair. State explicitly that A7’s antecedent is satisfied only for the identified token’s two manifestations; the other process tokens have one manifestation each.
- **A9–A10:** The process-part/event-part correspondence is correctly witnessed by the process indexed by the corresponding nonzero regular-open subevent. For the two-time token’s earlier manifestation, this ensures every subevent has a corresponding temporary process part at that time. It does not make the process parts identical to event parts; the model correctly keeps the sorts disjoint, as A8 requires.
- **A11:** The proposed witness H(S)=S∩(c,d), c=(inf S+sup S)/2, is nonempty and regular open for nonzero regular-open S. Its hull has the same right endpoint and a strictly later left endpoint than S’s hull, hence strictly Finishes S’s hull. This realizes each *specified* local-property correspondence. The paper’s A11 is a schema for each local temporary property, with a matching timeless event property and biconditional; the model should explicitly interpret the property families as those matched by H (and define their extensions accordingly), rather than imply that one witness settles arbitrary predicates added to an expanded language. Since the source itself treats the front window and granularity as property-dependent, H is a formal witness function, not a general physical account of process fronts.
- **A12:** Assigning each paired global property matching truth values on p and its manifestation is the right schema interpretation. A threshold property varying between Y₀ and Y₁ exhibits genuine change within the formal model. Avoid saying this proves a physical cumulative property or an independent account of score/production; property semantics are part of the constructed interpretation.
- **V2′:** Inheriting temporal location from each manifestation is coherent if the model takes the event hull as location. It does not entail that the contents of the hull are concrete-from the assessment interval. Location and concrete inventory are distinct predicates here.

### CEM and typed scope

The model takes the event mereology to be the nonzero regular-open sets ordered by inclusion, and the temporary process mereology at each interval to be an isomorphic copy. Regularized joins provide fusions; extensionality and supplementation hold within each sort/slice. This supports CEM in the model’s typed reading. It does **not** instantiate a single untyped global CEM over the disjoint union of processes and events, with cross-category fusions. The article says it adopts CEM and keeps constitution separate from parthood; A8’s explicit predicates and A9–A10 support a typed reading for the displayed structure, but the paper does not, in these clauses, spell out a universal all-category fusion theory. Therefore label category-relative CEM as a modeling choice, not an unambiguous source theorem. If a critic adds cross-category fusion objects and a global CEM, that is an expanded theory to model separately; it does not by itself entail P4.

## Inventory expansion and what it proves

The C interpretation is intentionally independent of the source reduct. The example sets process C equal to V1′ interval-existence and event C equal to membership in a selected front region. At I₁ this yields C(p,1), P(e_Y₀,e_Y₁), and not C(e_Y₀,1), while retaining P5 (closure of C among event parts). That is an explicit relative countermodel to P4 plus the derived claim in this formalization. It also demonstrates that preserving P5 alone cannot repair the inference: with P4 denied, the conclusion does not follow.

The fresh-predicate argument has a stronger but purely semantic form: for any model of a theory T that leaves C unconstrained, C can be expanded in more than one way without changing the truth of T. This establishes non-definability/non-entailment of a claim involving C from T alone. It is conditional on a model of T and on C being genuinely absent/unconstrained. It does not show that every substantively specified “concrete inventory” has both expansions, or that the interpretation corresponds to a viable metaphysics. In the explicit example, calling the front-window predicate the present concrete inventory is a stipulation. Process-C follows interval existence, while event-C does not; that is the intended counterinterpretation, but the source gives no independent criterion endorsing it.

The expansion also does not settle the rival’s costs. A presentist may object that a current process cannot be constituted by a noncurrent event, or that its current location/history or global properties require stronger cross-temporal realism. Those are substantive bridge/factivity/unity principles absent from A1–A12 and V1′–V3′ as quoted. Conversely, the countermodel cannot claim those principles are false. It shows only that they are additional premises.

## Exact repairs to the handback claim

1. Replace “model of the source apparatus” with “model of the displayed A1–A12/V1′–V3′ fragment under sorted, category-relative CEM.”
2. Say that the source axioms do not interpret C or entail P4; do not say that the source endorses the constructed current-inventory interpretation.
3. Add the two limitations to the conclusion: token unity across I₀/I₁ is stipulated at this fragmentary level, and the model does not meet or refute additional unity/individuation commitments from §§4–5.
4. Keep event-P5 closure explicit and note that the counterexample denies P4 only. This is enough to invalidate the inference while preserving P5, but it leaves P4’s independent warrant as the live philosophical question.
5. Treat A11 as a matched property schema and identify H as a formal terminal-sub-event witness only. Treat A12’s truth-value assignments as model semantics, not a physical grounding account.
6. Describe the result as an independence/counterinterpretation result relative to an unconstrained C predicate, conditional on the regular-open model. Make no claim about every theory of process constitution, full presentism, event annihilation, historical priority, or novelty.

## Bounded surviving statement

There is a two-interval, sorted regular-open model of the displayed constitution and manifestation clauses (with the property schemas interpreted by matched property families) in which one process token has an accumulated later manifestation, every event part remains event-concrete whenever its event whole is event-concrete, and an added interval-relative inventory predicate includes the process but excludes an earlier event part. Thus the displayed A/V clauses plus event-parthood closure alone do not entail occurrence-to-current-concreteness; P4 or another bridge that rules out this interpretation is required. This is a finite-time model with an infinite event domain, conditional on the stated typed CEM and manifestation semantics. It is not a full metaphysical presentist model, a universal theorem about constitutive ontologies, or evidence that the source authors accept the added C interpretation.
