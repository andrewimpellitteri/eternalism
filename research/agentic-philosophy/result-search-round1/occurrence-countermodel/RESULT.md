# Occurrence route: an explicit rival and a stopping verdict

31 October 2026. Coordinator test of GLM submission
`f98641ce90108e83198937041e2b59b8e2136c94`. This is the final adversarial
test of the old pilot's P2 family, not a newly commissioned recovery.

**Claim under test:** every elapsed event constituting an ongoing process
is concrete from the current standpoint. The worker derives this given
constitution-to-current-concreteness (P4) and inheritance to event parts
(P5). That conditional inference is valid. The question is whether the
source apparatus independently requires those premises.

**Audited disposition:** the formal bridge is not forced by the displayed
constitution fragment under the stated sorted mereology. The model
below keeps one process through changing manifestations, accumulation and
the event/process parthood correspondence, while an earlier phase is outside
the added current inventory. It preserves current-inventory closure under
event parthood and rejects P4. It is a counterinterpretation of the proposed
inference, not a completed presentist metaphysics or a new theorem about
every constitutive ontology. The independent
[review](../occurrence-countermodel-review/REVIEW.md) checks the construction
and preserves these limits.

## 1. Source contract and interpretation boundary

Direct primary reading: Guarino–Guizzardi2024,
[§§3.1–3.2](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-024-04505-2),
A1–A12, V1′–V3′, footnotes10–17 and the flight discussion in §2.
Constitution is functional, typed and asymmetric; manifestations accumulate;
temporary process parts correspond to timeless event parts. The source
does not require the constituent's temporal presence at the assessment
interval. Its separate process-existence postulate is retained below.

We distinguish three notions:

- Quantification over an event and its untensed parthood/constitution facts.
- The source's process existence at an assessment interval, E(p,j).
- The additional inventory predicate C(z,j): concrete from standpoint j.

The third is the disputed interpretation, not a source synonym supplied by
the first two. Treating every quantified event as already concrete from j
would settle the target by a bridge premise. Conversely, permitting past
relata in the semantics does not prove that a presentist can dispense with
all of its usual problems about reference and cross-temporal relations.

## 2. An infinite, atomless event structure

Let Ω=(−1,1), and B=RO(Ω), the Boolean algebra of regular open subsets of Ω.
Its zero is ∅; work with nonzero elements as events e_S. Event parthood is

    P(e_S,e_R) iff S⊆R.

Boolean joins are regularizations of unions, not necessarily raw unions.
This gives classical extensional mereology, including supplementation and
arbitrary nonempty fusion, in the event sort. Every event has bounded
temporal hull ℓ(e_S)=[inf S,sup S]. This is an event model, not a model of
an airplane or of relativistic spacetime.

Use two atomic assessment intervals, I₀=[0,1] and I₁=[1,2]. The use of
intervals rather than points is deliberate. Set Y₀=(−1,0), Y₁=Ω. Their
events are the accumulated manifestations immediately preceding the
respective assessment intervals. The A6 alternatives allowing Before or
Meets are used literally; no point-time replacement of Allen relations is
needed.

## 3. Processes, constitution, existence and temporary parts

For each j∈{0,1} and each nonzero S∈RO(Y_j), introduce a process p_{j,S},
except identify p_{0,Y₀} and p_{1,Y₁} as the **same process p**. No other
identifications are made. Put

    K(p_{j,S},e_S,I_j),

and no other K facts. At each interval define E(x,j) iff x has a K
manifestation there. The main p exists at both intervals with different
manifestations. The remaining processes exist at one interval each. The
model does not assert their persistence at unmodelled times. The main
token's cross-time identity is stipulated at this fragmentary level. We
have not supplied a physical sustaining mechanism or reconstructed the
additional unity and individuation commitments in §§4–5. Thus this is
not the promised full metaphysical rival with all of those commitments
independently vindicated; it is the completed formal test of the worker's
claimed derivation from the displayed constitution apparatus.

At I_j, define temporary process parthood by

    P_t(p_{j,S},p_{j,R},I_j) iff S⊆R.

Thus at each interval the active process mereology is isomorphic to
RO(Y_j) minus zero. Event and process sorts are disjoint. No temporary
part is common to a process and its constituting event. A8 is respected;
manifestations are not smuggled in as process parts.

Set the process's location at I_j equal to ℓ(e_S). This satisfies V2′.
Manifestation graphs distinguish every pair of processes: the main p has
two entries; every other process has its own single entry. This satisfies
V3′ as well as V1′.

**Mereological scope:** CEM here is category-relative, for timeless event
parthood and the temporary active-process parthood actually used in the
source formulas. No untyped axiom demanding fusions across every ontological
category is assumed. The source does not spell out that larger theory.
The relative expansion result in §6 does not depend on this choice.

## 4. Check the source's temporal and structural clauses

| Clauses | Verification |
|---|---|
| A1–A5; asymmetry and anti-transitivity | Each process has exactly one manifestation where active. K always runs from Process to Event, never back or through another Process. Every process has a manifestation. |
| A6 | For K at I_j, sup S≤j. If sup S<j, the event is Before I_j; otherwise it Meets I_j. This holds for every subevent, not only the main manifestation. |
| A7 | Only p is active at both intervals. Y₀⊂Y₁ and their hulls have the same start with strictly increasing ends. Thus parthood and strict Allen Starts both hold. |
| A8 | Temporary process parts are processes; a constituent is an event. There is no common temporary part. |
| A9 | For e_S≤e_R constituting p_{j,R}, use the existing p_{j,S} as the required temporary part. |
| A10 | A temporary part p_{j,S} of p_{j,R} has e_S as its manifestation, and e_S≤e_R. |
| V1′–V3′ | Existence is manifestation-having; locations are inherited as specified; distinct process manifestation graphs are unequal. |

A11 needs a genuine terminal subevent, not a finite atomic shortcut. For
nonempty S, write a=inf S, d=sup S and c=(a+d)/2. The event with region

    H(S)=S∩(c,d)

is nonempty and regular open, has the same supremum and a strictly later
infimum than S, and therefore strictly Finishes e_S. Give each designated
local event property λ′ a process counterpart by
λ(p_{j,S},I_j)=λ′(e_{H(S)}). This witnesses A11. These are specified
property families; no second-order claim is made about every arbitrary
predicate one might add.

For each designated global event property g′, put
g(p_{j,S},I_j)=g′(e_S). This is A12. Nonconstant instances exist: for
example, a length threshold between |Y₀|=1 and |Y₁|=2 changes truth value
for the same p. The process and its properties have not been replaced by
one unchanging snapshot.

This verifies the displayed constitution fragment, including A11–A12 for
the designated properties. It does not reconstruct the taxonomy in §§4–5,
Fine's original theory, all DOLCE axioms, or a physical dynamics.

## 5. Add a front inventory: deny P4 while keeping event inheritance

Let F₀=Y₀ and F₁=(0,1), the latest portion of the respective manifestations.
Interpret the new predicate on events as

    C(e_S,j) iff S⊆F_j,

and on processes as C(x,j) iff E(x,j). The inventory thus includes the
same main process at each interval and its selected front events. This
is a possible regimentation of C for testing entailment, not an assertion
that these windows are the physically or metaphysically correct present.

At I₁, choose the elapsed phase z=e_{Y₀}. Then

    K(p,e_{Y₁},I₁),   P(z,e_{Y₁}),   C(p,1),   not C(z,1).

The worker's conclusion C fails. P4 fails because the accumulated
manifestation e_{Y₁} is not in the current inventory. P5, for event wholes,
**holds**: if R⊆F_j and S⊆R, then S⊆F_j. Thus the rival need not selectively
drop ordinary event parthood closure. Process and event mereologies,
manifestation graphs and changing properties are exactly those above;
only the extra inventory interpretation was added.

This does permit a current process to be constituted by an event not
current in the C sense. The worker calls that a cost; denying its
possibility is precisely a version of P4, not a contradiction derived
from A1–A12. Likewise V2′ supplies a location, not the further inference
that everything in that temporal hull is concrete from the assessment.

## 6. Preservation beyond the constructed fragment is relative

There is a simple broader logical result. Let T be any consistent
formalization of the source vocabulary, including further axioms about
constitution, parthood, properties or unity, but no bridge involving C.
Take a model M of T with a past phase z in a manifestation at j. Expanding
M by a new C interpretation leaves every T sentence true. In particular
one can retain C(p,j) while excluding z; one can also expand the same
reduct with z included.

This is a relative independence result, conditional on such an M. It does
not independently prove consistency of all possible source commitments.
If a fuller theory already defines C, or fixes its meaning by an additional
existence or relational-factivity axiom, that bridge must be included and
tested; the expansion observation alone does not answer it. In particular,
a decorative fresh predicate is not by itself a successful metaphysics.

The explicit structure above does more than the expansion observation:
it exhibits a changing process and parthood correspondence, with P5 for
events retained, in the actual constitution fragment. Neither result
proves a universal impossibility for constitutive arguments.

## 7. Second case, rival costs and final disposition

For the worker's fermentation case, distinguish accumulated product from
the production events. A stock can obey m₁=m₀+r₁ while only its latest
production event belongs to the chosen front inventory. Set m₀=1,r₁=2,
m₁=3 in arbitrary mass units. The stock equation does not add a bridge
from the present product to current concreteness of its earlier production
event. This is a counterinterpretation of that inference, not an empirical
model of fermentation or a claim that product mass grounds every truth
about the process. No conservation principle turns products into events.

The proposed falsifier must not require the rival to accept all of the
worker's alleged costs as established. P4 and P5 together already entail
the conclusion; a purported countermodel retaining both cannot defeat
that valid conditional. The relevant question is whether one premise can
be withheld without violating an independently accepted commitment. Here
P4 is withheld; a process is not thereby reduced to a fiction, and P5
need not be surrendered.

**Stop this P2 family as the current positive result candidate.** Its one
recovery has not supplied an independently warranted occurrence-to-inventory
bridge. Retain the source distinction and counterinterpretation as review
work, with no historical-originality or ontology verdict. Do not issue a
referee packet or another renamed recovery. The modal route is a separate
candidate with its own source and axiom tests.
