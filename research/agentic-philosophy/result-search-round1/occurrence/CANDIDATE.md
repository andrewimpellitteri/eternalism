## 1. Target entity fixed (apparatus vs operation vs token)

All claims below are about the **individual ongoing process token**: flight
AZ123 Munich→Verona on 1 January 2022 *while ongoing* (Guarino–Guizzardi's own
example), and batch fermentation B *while fermenting* (§6 below). The
**apparatus** (aircraft; vat with culture) is a persisting endurant whose
persistence raises separate questions and is not the target. The
**operation** (the scheduled route; the fermentation kind) is a process kind,
which Guarino–Guizzardi call "a cognitive construction … processes are
therefore cognitively carved out of events" — the kind is not the target
either. Confusions between these three are excluded by fiat here.

## 2. The three predicates, separated before use

| Predicate | Reading | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **Constitutes**(x,y,t) [their K] | process x is constituted by event y during atomic interval t | G&G §3.1, fn 11; A1, A5–A7 |
| **Present-at**(t,z) | z overlaps or finishes at t; front-presence; DOLCE idiom "partially present" | G&G §2 ("no longer present"), fn 11; DOLCE 2023 passage |
| **Concrete-from**(b,z) | z belongs to the concrete inventory as of standpoint b (this project's Q-form; Brading's "exists only presently" over a dynamically delimited region) | Brading 2015 §5 pp. 17–18 |

The pilot's P2 fused the second and third ("elapsed extent concretely
*present* from b"). Guarino–Guizzardi's footnote 11 is a direct
source-level refutation of that fusion: "we do not require y to be present
during the interval t (Masolo et al., 2003, p. 34)" — constitution holds
while presence fails. So the fused reading of P2 is dead **at the source**,
and no recovery of this family may defend it. What remains defensible is the
split claim **C** below: concrete-from-b without presence-at-b.

## 3. The exact inference (original form, preserved)

Common ground — the rival's own published apparatus, granted without
conversion to any temporal ontology: A1–A7, A9, V1', V2', classical
extensional mereology (CEM), footnote 11, and their flight clauses.

- **P1** (constitution clause). At b, the ongoing token x has manifestation
  y_b with K(x, y_b, b), where "when a process is ongoing, its manifestation
  at a given time is the history of what happened until that time, i.e., the
  current life of the process (Stout, 2003)" (§3.1, A5–A6; y_b Finishes at b).
- **P2** (accumulation clause). Each elapsed phase z of x is a timeless part
  of y_b: A7 ("the life of a process accumulates as the process goes on,
  because the life y of a process x at t must be a starting part of the life
  y′ of the same process at t′"), and A9 transfers z into the token's
  temporary structure at b: ∃w (P(w,x,b) ∧ K(w,z,b)) — the Alps crossing is
  "a manifestation of a part of" the flight *at t₃*.
- **P3** (token existence). x exists at b (V1': "it exists at time t iff it
  has a manifestation at t").

Independent premises (motivated without assuming C, eternalism, or
presentism):

- **P4** (constitution factivity at the standpoint). If x exists at b and
  K(x, y_b, b), then y_b is concrete-from-b.
  Independent reasons: (i) constitution theories of the Baker line — which
  G&G follow when they reject Fine's V4* and adopt A8 — are factive about
  both relata at the constitution time; (ii) G&G's own V1' defines token
  existence *by* manifestation-having, so the manifestation must have being
  whenever the token does; (iii) fn 11 declines only a **presence**
  requirement, not an existence requirement — their framework and the DOLCE
  idiom it inherits explicitly separate being from presence (perdurants are
  "partially present", not absent).
- **P5** (parthood inheritance of concrete being). If a concrete whole is
  concrete-from-b, its parts — in the timeless-parthood sense that
  structures that whole (their event parthood P(z,y) in A7/A9) — are
  concrete-from-b.
  Independent reasons: (i) CEM, which G&G explicitly adopt ("abandoning
  Fine's hylomorphic mereology in favor of classical mereology"), is factive
  about parthood: an existing whole has existing parts; concrete-from-b is
  the standpoint-relative counterpart of existence for concrete wholes;
  (ii) their own accretion picture: processes are "dynamic accumulations of
  events", temporal parts that "add to the temporal parts of the flight as
  long as it goes on", "like the parts of a snowball … which gradually
  become inaccessible as they are covered by new parts" — inaccessible, not
  annihilated; (iii) anti-brute-cohesion: without P5, the accumulated whole
  y_b (which Finishes at b, hence is present at its front) would contain
  sub-wholes (the earlier life y_b1, each phase z) that are not
  concrete-from-b, making the token's accumulated being at b brute at b —
  for accretion unities this inverts the explanatory order, since the
  accumulated extent is nothing over and above the accumulated phases.

**C.** Every elapsed phase of x — every phase that temporally constitutes x
at b — is concrete-from-b.

Derivation: P3+P4 give Concrete-from(b, y_b); P2 gives P(z, y_b) for each
elapsed z; P5 transfers. ∎

Explicit non-claims (per the shared constraints): C is **not** exhaustiveness
(nothing is asserted about non-constituting events, the apparatus, other
systems, or the future); C is **not** presence (the fused P2 stays refuted by
fn 11); C is **not** derived from unity alone — a rival who reads unity as
principle-driven carving without accumulated concrete constitution blocks
P2's application (rival R3 below); an inclusion claim does not exclude other
events; no system-relative region is promoted to a global inventory.

## 4. Strongest rival (source-based; directly read)

- **R1 — fusion rejection (already conceded).** Keeps process unity intact
  (V1', A5–A7) and denies only that constitution brings presence. Footnote 11
  plus the flight prose ("the crossing of the Alps … that is no longer
  present, while the descent to Verona is a new subprocess that is just in
  the inception stage") show the rival can do this *within* its published
  apparatus. **No rival must deny unity to reject the fused P2.** This route
  is closed; it was the pilot's error.
- **R2 — tenseless trivialization (grants C, drains it).** Their conclusions
  "stay neutral … sticking to the classic notion of events as perdurants":
  every elapsed phase then exists tenselessly, C is true but does no
  restrictive work — the completed history satisfies Concrete-from(b,·) too,
  so C delimits nothing. This is this project's known
  inclusion-without-exclusion point
  ([CONSTITUTIVE_GROUNDING_TEST.md](../../CONSTITUTIVE_GROUNDING_TEST.md),
  "Exact inclusion test"), not a new discovery here. The candidate's value
  is not exclusion but *location*: it shows the concrete-from-b leg of the
  occurrence bridge is forced on the apparatus (R2 and R3's non-denial both
  grant it), while the presence leg is dead at source. The abductive choice
  between a block completion and a growing completion is left open by the
  paper itself (fn 10: "we do not commit on the existence of future events";
  fn 15 mentions "the so-called growing block metaphor … introduced in a
  different context" only for cross-world modal behavior).
- **R3 — front-only deflation (the only genuine denial).** "Concrete-from-b"
  is front-existence plus records; the accumulated life at b is
  representation ("a cognitive construction … carved out of events", §3.1);
  all global pro-tem properties (A12; "the current score of a football
  game") are grounded in present front-states, logs, and products; no
  elapsed constituting event is concrete-from-b. Costs, stated precisely:
  (i) it must withhold P5 from the manifestation while retaining CEM
  elsewhere — a selective factivity needing independent motivation;
  (ii) A7's cross-time parthood then relates items in different concrete
  inventories, so accumulation becomes purely representational, and the
  flight's own "We have just crossed the Alps" (§2, said of "the present
  flight") must be carried by logs rather than constitution;
  (iii) combined with V2' ("its temporal location is that of its
  manifestation"), the token would be an existing-at-b process whose
  temporal location — the whole accumulated interval — is not
  concrete-from-b: a located-but-not-concrete inventory tension inside their
  own postulates; (iv) it sits ill with fn 15's own picture, "at a certain
  time, the past is frozen" (for processes), on which the token's frozen
  past is not nothing-from-b.
  None of R1–R3 requires abandoning process unity; R3 pays named costs;
  R2 grants C as true-but-nonrestrictive.

## 5. Second case (non-flight; sedimented accumulation)

Batch fermentation token B (vat ≠ operation-kind ≠ token). At b the global
pro-tem property is "B has produced 3.2 g ethanol and is still fermenting"
(A12 pattern). The elapsed production phases are **sedimented**: by mass
conservation the product is materially part of B's present front-state.
Test: R3 grounds the 3.2 g in present products; the inheritance reading
grounds it in the accumulated production events, which *are* B's
constitution at b (manifestation = "the history of what happened until that
time"). Both groundings succeed as truth-groundings; the discriminator is
the constitution clause. Outcome: the sedimented case shows the disagreement
is not an artifact of representational accumulation (flight logs), because
here any b-inventory that includes the token's concrete front-state but
excludes the accumulated production phases must still include the sedimented
products — narrowing R3's exclusion to the **events themselves**, at cost
(ii) above: denying that the token's constitution is anything over and above
products + records, i.e., the unity of the ongoing fermentation becomes a
bookkeeping unity. This does **not** refute R3 (products ≠ events; the
exclusion remains consistent); it localizes its price. Category caution
kept: the union of elapsed token-paths is not "the interval"; products are
not the production events.

## 6. Falsifier (cheapest decisive test)

at b, **and** some elapsed constituting phase is not concrete-from-b —
without accepting R3's named costs (i)–(iv). Formal shape: (a) a factive K
over events; (b) an existence-at-standpoint predicate on events that
excludes a timeless part of the constituting manifestation; (c) grounding of
the token's A12 global properties at b that quantifies over no elapsed
constituting event. If such a model is written down, C fails on this
apparatus class and the occurrence bridge's live leg collapses to R2's
trivialization. This is a pen-and-paper model check; no computation is
needed or used.
