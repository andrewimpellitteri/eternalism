## Retain

The rational comb satisfies the journal's Definition6 under its necessary
nonempty-chain convention. Its maximal directed histories and history sets
are as stated. The non-featurelessness calculation and direct trunk-CC
calculation survive. The journal's every-history nontriviality conclusion
fails on the trunk. This is a substantive external inferential target,
unlike another rephrasing of our own occurrence bridge.

The [clean proof](RESULT.md) gives each infimum/supremum and chain-PCP case,
repairs the offshoot calculation, and states a sharp parameterized result
within this comb family. No finite grid is used as a substitute for density.

## Correct or withhold

- **Offshoot CC is incomplete.** It contains all continued transitions at
  earlier branch points as well as the departure transition. The first
  native review inherited this error and was asked to correct it using the
  explicit h₀/h₁ witness. The error changes neither tail constancy nor
  the journal counterexample. Review agreement was not used as a proof.
- **PCP differences need both pieces.** For d<e, h_e\h_d contains a trunk
  segment and an offshoot. The common maximum remains below all of it.
- **“Minimal sparseness repair” is not proved.** Nor is the proposed
  physical motivation an established empirical premise. Non-density is
  necessary and sufficient for trunk non-identity in this comb family;
  no general BST sufficiency or minimality follows.
- **No small-perturbation theorem.** The two branch distributions give
  different class sizes; an “arbitrarily small redistribution” claim
  requires a specified metric and perturbation notion.
- **Do not overstate the BT target.** The rational comb has non-singleton
  classes on offshoots, so the source's literal existential BT sentence
  remains true. Footnote26 gives a sufficient identity scenario; our result
  shows that scenario is not necessary, not that its sufficiency fails.
- **Later-book scope is only selectively checked.** The cached corrected
  proof has456 PDF pages, not445 as the handback claims. Its copyright is
  2022; the official online publication is2021. Theorem10.1 is explicitly
  BST_NF; the direct comb is excluded by Fact3.17. No transformed-NF
  construction, full-book absence claim or silent-correction history is
  adopted without its own proof and source audit.
- **Priority and truth have different falsifiers.** A prior publication
  would defeat originality, not make this counterexample mathematically
  false. No broad claim that no erratum exists is warranted by bounded
  title/abstract searches.
