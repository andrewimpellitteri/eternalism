# Two reformulations for observer access to the atomless matched pair

Status: bounded proposal generation, 22 October 2026. These are distinct candidate routes, not results, novelty claims, physical predictions, or evidence for a temporal ontology. The frozen question is:

> Under precisely specified observer access, can the matched-cylinder atomless pair be operationally distinguished?

The pair has equal magnitudes on every individual finite labelled cylinder, but the completed intrinsic event E2 = “exactly two minimal elements” has different rank-one quantum-measure values. Its total-variation measures are atomless. The construction is scalar and has norm-only cylinder covariance; it supplies no detector or physical instrument. Coordinator's L2-density / Hilbert-readout route is outside these proposals.

## Avenue 1 — finite-memory observers as stopping rules

**Exact target.** Define an observer's readout to be a measurable function of the finite prefix visible at a stopping time T, where T is a stopping time for the filtration F_n generated by the first n birth masks. Specify a memory cap K by requiring that the readout state takes at most K values and updates recursively from the previous state and the next mask. Ask whether a fixed finite K and a stopping rule with finite expected duration can separate the models by a declared operational statistic, while the same statistic cannot be recovered from matched single-cylinder magnitudes alone. Keep the statistic explicit: either ordinary event frequency under a separately specified probability law, or the complex amplitude of a finite union. Do not identify these two readouts.

**Unusual reformulation.** Treat “when the observer stops looking” as part of the observable, rather than treating every finite stage as an arbitrary cutoff. E2 is an event on completed infinite histories: since M_n is monotone, it is characterized by M_n eventually stabilizing at two. This does not assert that E2 belongs to the formal tail sigma algebra; membership can depend on early minimal births. An observer can use a sequential certificate such as “two minima have appeared and no third has appeared by T,” but this is only a finite-prefix approximation, never a certificate that no later minimum will occur. The interesting target is therefore a bounded-memory stopping scheme whose stopped readout law differs, not a claim that the observer decides E2 in finite time.

**Smallest discriminating test.** First use the exact stage-three fact already in the construction: M_3=2 consists of four labelled cylinders, (0,1,0), (0,0,1), (0,0,2), and (0,0,3). Their coherent amplitude sum is 1/2 in A and 1/2-i/5 in B, giving squared moduli 1/4 and 29/100; each corresponding individual cylinder magnitude agrees across the pair. This is distinct from the separate two-cylinder cp union, whose values are mu_A=1/16 and mu_B=9/400. Then use the supplied tail estimate

    |nu_j(E2)-a_(j,N)| <= 16/[7 L^(N-1)(L-1)]

At N=3, tau=16/[7*1000^2*999] < 1/100000000. The completed-event amplitudes therefore lie within tau of 1/2 and 1/2-i/5. Their modulus centers differ by sqrt(29)/10-1/2 > 3/100, so even after allowing tau error on each modulus the intervals are disjoint. This confirms that the completed-event value gap is already certified using the earliest available stage-three approximation; it does not build an instrument. For a classical nonnegative readout, the negative control is that individual cylinder magnitudes alone cannot encode the phase that distinguishes the coherent union.

**Strongest kill.** On every run where T is finite, the readout depends on a finite prefix; this does not mean every admissible observer has finite expected stopping time. The existing equality of individual cylinder magnitudes does not imply equality of coherent finite-union values, and the four-cylinder M_3=2 coherent witness already separates the pair, so a stopping-time restatement may merely repackage the known finite-union distinction. Conversely, if “operational” means a classical observer sampling histories under |nu| or another positive measure, the complex amplitudes do not define that sampling law, and the proposed discrimination has no physical meaning until the law and instrument are supplied. A finite stopping rule cannot decide E2 exactly; any claim that it can is killed by continuations that add a third minimal element after the stopping prefix.

**Required bridge premise.** A physical observer is represented by a specified filtration, bounded-memory state update, stopping rule, and calibrated readout; the selected readout statistic corresponds to an actually available instrument. This is a physical/instrument premise. Any move from distinguishability to passage, presentism, eternalism, determinism, or an arrow of time would require an additional philosophical bridge premise and is not licensed by this proposal.

## Avenue 2 — accessible events as relabeling-invariant questions

**Exact target.** Replace “observer sees a labelled cylinder” with an explicit accessible-event algebra on completed histories. Start with events invariant under all natural relabelings of the same completed past-finite order; then ask whether E2 belongs to that algebra and whether its two model values remain different when the event is evaluated on the completed-history sigma algebra. Separate this diagonal membership question from whether an observer can access the off-diagonal coherent operator needed to read a rank-one quantum measure.

**Unusual reformulation.** Make the event language the object under test. The question becomes: which invariant questions can an observer ask without naming a birth order, and does that event language distinguish the pair? This reframes “covariance” as a restriction on admissible predicates, not as a claim that a representation change is automatically gauge. A strong candidate is a completed-history invariant defined from the order itself—E2, “the completed order has exactly two minimal elements”—rather than “the first N births have two minima.” Compare its membership projector with any coherent filter separately.

**Smallest discriminating test.** Use the exact four-element witness already in the audit: predecessor-mask sequences (0,0,3,0) and (0,0,0,3) are isomorphic enumerations of V plus an isolated element, yet the first-three-birth phase-ray event gives opposite answers; append the same infinite chain of universal tops. This is a negative control: finite isomorphism invariance does not make the prefix lift a completed-history invariant. Then check the intrinsic E2 predicate directly on an isomorphic pair of enumerations and verify equal membership. Finally ask whether its value difference is an accessible observable: for the known finite class-pinching channel, the coherent rank-one target across multiple classes is outside the adjoint image, while the diagonal membership projector can remain accessible. No full instrument claim follows from that algebraic test.

**Strongest kill.** E2's completed-order membership may be invariant and have unequal values under the two scalar complex measures, but the existing observer-access audit establishes that membership access does not provide access to the coherent target. If operational statistics are required to be positive probabilities generated by a physical instrument, the model has not defined one. Also, relabeling invariance alone does not select a unique physical symmetry group or observer algebra; stipulating the invariant event language may encode the desired answer. If the proposal silently upgrades finite isomorphism invariance to completed-history covariance, the explicit four-element continuation kills it.

**Required bridge premise.** Completed-order relabelings represent physically indistinguishable descriptions, and the observer's accessible event algebra contains the named invariant predicate. A further physical premise is needed to realize its event statistic as an instrument on the relevant quantum measure. Neither premise entails any temporal ontology. In particular, covariance or representation invariance is not by itself an ontological equivalence or gauge identification.

**Bounded next action.** Build a tiny finite certificate with the two four-element enumerations and their common infinite-top continuation, plus one explicit relabeling-invariance check for E2 membership on finite extensions. Record separately (i) event invariance, (ii) equality or inequality of the supplied complex measure values, and (iii) instrument access. Stop if (iii) remains unspecified; do not infer measurement from covariance or launch a general causal-set observer construction.

## Disposition of the two ideas

The first changes the observer's temporal access protocol (finite memory and stopping); the second changes the event ontology of access (completed-order invariant predicates and symmetry). Their cheap checks expose different failure modes. Both may collapse to the already-known distinction between finite coherent unions and single-cylinder magnitudes. They are preserved as proposals precisely because the physical instrument bridge is missing; neither is a result, novelty claim, or empirical distinction.
