# Frozen pilot question

Under a **named observer-access model**, what can and cannot distinguish the
two atomless matched-cylinder scalar growth models?

Fixed input: the exact pair, common real-percolation reference P0, all-stage
measure extension and E2 gap in `research/longshots/bc-matched-diffuse/RESULT.md`;
the matching-variation theorem in its `VARIATION_DATA.md`; and the earlier
membership/coherent-filter distinction in the observer-access audit. These
are mathematical project inputs, not a physical instrument.

Allowed search changes: representation of the measures; an auxiliary state or
operator; finite memory/stopping protocol; a precisely declared accessible
algebra. State every added assumption. Preserve the original pair and identify
changes to readout or the scientific question explicitly.

Useful result: a tested mathematical representation with a sharp access
boundary, a concrete obstruction, or an explicitly unresolved instrument
requirement. Do not equate a nonadditive quantum-measure value with a normalized
detector probability. A formal completed-event projector does not prove that
a finite physical observer can implement it.

Separate questions: whether the access model is physically realized, whether
its symmetry requirements are appropriate, and whether any result selects a
temporal ontology. No pass on the mathematical part answers those questions.
No claim of novelty follows from this pilot.

Budget: at most two rounds, at most three active researchers including the
coordinator, small standard-library exact checks, no installs or new external
runs. Preserve rejected shortcuts and at least two different mechanisms.
