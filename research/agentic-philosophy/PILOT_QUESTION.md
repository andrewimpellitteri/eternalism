31 October 2026. Prospective question card for one bounded research attempt.

method. The [older admissibility comparison](../article-admissibility/PROTOCOL.md)

## Question and common ground

Can a physically individuated sustaining activity justify the concrete
temporal extent of a system, beyond establishing how the system behaves?

Allow the law-constitutive theory genuine dynamical unity. The rival may
accept that unity, process, laws and successful physical explanations while
denying that a sustaining region exhausts concrete existence. A mathematical
representation does not itself make its domain concrete. Keep Brading's
system-relative proposal distinct from a global simultaneous present.

## Required advance

Produce one explicit philosophical argument for a constitutive-to-existence
bridge, with independent reasons for its controversial premise. State exactly
what the strongest rival has a reason to revise. A useful result may favor
either side or find a previously unnoticed obstruction, but must not merely
repeat that more premises are needed. A conditional argument with a defended
premise can be valuable despite disagreement; unanimity is not required.

## Execution and stopping rule

State the decisive inference in full. Give the best rival response and one
independent test case; permit at most one revision answering that response.
Keep the original argument and change log. Changing the question loses the
original success claim. An unfamiliar term or routine calculation is insufficient.

Known, openly labelled controls: a state and law can determine an interval
activity without the instant realizing it; this is valid. Inferring that
the instantaneous data therefore constitute the activity is invalid.
Recognizing these controls is a prerequisite, not a discovery.

No numerical grid, installation or child agent is needed. Deliver the bounded
argument or exact failure, nearest-precedent comparison, public provenance,
and what was learned beyond the supplied constraints. A fresh-context review
will inspect it; this is not a blinded capability experiment.
