# Review of the first GLM bridge attempt

31 October 2026. Coordinator inspection of clean isolated submission `ccb40b5`,
task `ontology-constitutive-bridge-pilot`. The original four lane files and
root handback remain in its worktree. Status stays **review**; no wholesale
acceptance or source-depth upgrade. This review changes the next search design.

## 1. P2 is an additional premise, not the stipulated common ground

The brief grants a genuine unity sustained by a diachronic activity. It
does not grant the worker's P2, which says an ongoing token's elapsed extent
is concretely present from b, or its composite-presence principle. Calling
these consequences of the stipulation does not defend them. A rival can
accept the activity as genuinely constitutive while contesting the assessment-
relative existence claim. The two positions are not contradictory without
the very bridge being tested.

The independent category argument may be defended as philosophy. It has not
shown why identifying one continuing process requires every elapsed part
to be present together. The source lead Guarino–Guizzardi2024 §§2–3 now has
selected direct coordinator reading: it distinguishes an ongoing process's
temporal constitution from earlier phases described as no longer present.
That is a serious alternative to examine, not a complete reconstruction of
its temporal ontology or an automatic refutation of P2.

## 2. The physical cessation test fails as stated

RESULT §3 says switching the boundary input to zero from b leaves no
carriage in progress and ends the transmission system. Let the input be1
before b and0 after. The very response formula cited in the submission gives

    y(b+s)=1−s/τ,    0<s<τ.

This exact formula uses an ideal step boundary and its weak transport
solution. With a continuous nonnegative switch starting at b, the response
is still at least1−s/τ for0<s<τ, from the unchanged earlier input alone.
The failure of immediate cessation therefore persists in the continuous
input class used by the benchmark.

The channel continues to carry its earlier input until the transit time has
elapsed. Afterward the unloaded apparatus can still transmit a newly applied
signal. Cessation of a particular transmission is not destruction of the
channel as an apparatus. One may choose the ongoing operation as the target
entity, but that is a different individuation and needs to remain fixed.
Also q(b,·) is a modeled physical field configuration, not intrinsically
mere bookkeeping. Declaring that it encodes but sustains nothing requires
an argument; the capacity of the unloaded apparatus also involves L,c and
its boundary law, not just its current q.

There is a second mismatch: the union of tokens' elapsed paths is a
spatiotemporal region. Its projection on source time is an interval; it is
not literally that interval. Input/output equivalence does not identify
physical carrier tokens across every realization. Exact kernel support
concerns a dependence protocol; its measure-zero endpoints cannot silently
be discarded in a claim about concrete inventory.

## 3. The general impossibility and global-collapse claims are unproved

R2 claims that no system-relative constitutive argument can settle
exhaustiveness without a global doctrine. The restricted domain-insensitivity
example proves only what its weak schema leaves undetermined. It quantifies
over neither every possible constitutive principle nor every account of
system-relative existence. A new negative/maximality premise might be poor
or good; it cannot be excluded by the weaker example alone.

Likewise ∀S: E_S(b)=R_S(b) does not imply that there is one system-independent
E(b) equal to every R_S(b). Applying a rule to all systems does not remove
its system index. The local-to-global collapse asserted in §§4,7 therefore
does not follow. No coherent complete ontology is inferred from this logical
observation either.

The Kepler case also does not establish AC's universal restriction that only
completion-structured activities can have determinate extents. A single
case supplies no universal result. A binding time is not furnished by the
exact eternally circular solution; adding a formation history changes the
physical setup and must be specified.

## 4. Retained scope and consequence for the process

The submission preserves one argument and revision, explicitly distinguishes
inherited sources, and eventually drops its unsupported exhaustive conclusion.
Its conditional inclusion claim remains conditional on P2 and the added
composite-presence principle. The claimed forced revisions by rival families
are not established. No philosophical breakthrough or historical novelty follows.

The next round therefore searches for independently warranted premises and
their cheapest real tests. It does not commission more applications of P2,
accept a stipulated explanandum as a fact, or promote a narrow countermodel
into a universal impossibility. Source-based conceptual criticism and a
targeted check of a published theorem are both admissible result routes.
