# The constitutive-bridge pilot: one executed argument, one rival, one revision

31 October 2026. Isolated worker submission for independent review, task

not a novelty claim, ontology verdict, or breakthrough. Everything here is

Question (from `PILOT_QUESTION.md`): Can a physically individuated sustaining
activity justify the concrete temporal extent of a system, beyond establishing
how the system behaves?

## 1. Common ground, physical case, and labeled controls

**Stipulated common ground.** S is genuinely sustained in its unity by an
activity A. The rival accepts unity, process, laws, and successful physical
explanations, while denying that a sustaining region exhausts concrete
existence. A mathematical representation does not itself make its domain
concrete.

**Primary physical case** (taken from the existing coordinator benchmark,
`RESPONSE_MEMORY_BENCHMARK.md` §6; the physics is inherited, not re-derived
here): a boundary-driven transport channel, length L, transport speed c,
transit time τ = L/c; boundary input f; output
y(t) = τ⁻¹ ∫₀^τ f(t−s) ds. The response kernel is strictly positive on the
open window (0, τ) and has exact cutoff τ; the exact support and truncation
error are invariant under internal realizations preserving inputs, outputs,
clock, and gain (benchmark §7, after Boyd–Chua §8.2). At an assessment time b
the interior field q(b,·) is an instantaneous Markov state that encodes the
input history, and the output at b depends on exactly the input over
[b−τ, b].

**Activity tokens.** For a signal element injected at time s, its carriage
token C_s occupies [s, s+τ]. At b the in-transit tokens are {C_s : s ∈
[b−τ, b]}; the union of their in-progress portions is the window
W_b = (b−τ, b] (endpoints carry no weight). Completed tokens are the exited
carriages C_s with s+τ < b. The channel's instantaneous disposition is q(b,·).

**Labeled controls** (stated as controls, per PILOT_QUESTION; recognized,
not rediscovered):

- *Valid control:* the state at b and the law determine the window and every
  token verdict. Determination of an interval activity by instantaneous data
  is genuine physics.
- *Invalid control:* inferring from that determination that the instantaneous
  data therefore realize or constitute the activity. This slide is not
  committed anywhere below; the presence claim is inferred from a different
  premise (P2), and §9 gives a counterexample separating P2 from determination.

## 2. Attempt 1: the strong bridge (original argument, preserved verbatim)

Definitions.

- *Sustainer*: an activity whose operation constitutes the system's
  characteristic organization, such that its cessation (admitted alternative
  held fixed, §3) ends the system's unity as this system without destroying
  matter or background laws. Contrast classes, each distinct: *enabling
  conditions* (required for A but not S's organization: power, absence of
  disturbance), *preparation events* (how A came to run), *encodings*
  (q(b,·) represents the history without organizing anything), *completed
  tokens* (exited carriages: they sustain no longer).
- *Operative extent of A at b*: the union of the in-progress portions of
  A's tokens at b. For the channel: W_b.
- *Concrete present of S at b*: the extent of S's presence **as this
  composite system** — its unified presence — at the assessment standpoint b.
  (Not the presence of S's matter: pipe material exists at b throughout the
  apparatus.)

Premises.

- **P1 (restricted contemporaneity of sustainers).** The unifier of a
  presently-unified composite is operative at the assessment time: what
  sustains S's unity at b is the activity in progress at b, and S's unity at
  b consists in that activity's being operative at b.
- **P2 (occurrence-presence).** A process token in progress at b is concretely
  present at b in its in-progress extent: its already-elapsed portion is
  present as the elapsed extent of the one occurring thing, not as a completed
  past item and not as absent.
- **P3 (presence-constitution, biconditional — Attempt 1 form).** For a
  composite S sustained by A: E is S's concrete present at b **iff** E is the
  operative extent of A at b.
  - *Forward direction:* the operative unifier's presence realizes S's
    presence as this composite (the composite has no further presence-bearer;
    its unified being-here is the unifier's operative being-here — this is the
    stipulated law-constitutive commitment applied to presence).
  - *Backward direction:* nothing outside the operative extent is
    present-for-S at b: completed tokens, enabling conditions, preparation
    events, and encodings sustain nothing and are not present-for-S.

Inference.

    G1: A sustains S's unity                     (stipulated common ground)
    G2: operative extent of A at b is W_b        (channel physics, inherited)
    P1, P2, P3  ⊢  S's concrete present at b = W_b, width τ.

Attempt 1's conclusion: the channel has a physically located, system-relative,
concrete present of width τ — fixed by apparatus physics, not by a
metaphysician's choice of look-back — with no global simultaneous present and
no transitivity assumption across systems.

## 3. Independent defense of the pivotal premises

The defenses must not assume the conclusion. P2 and P3-backward are where the
argument lives; they are defended before any rival is heard.

**P1 defense.**

1. *Definitional core, not ontological posit.* "Sustains" is fixed by the
   role test in §2: the activity whose operation constitutes the system's
   organization and whose cessation ends the system's unity by internal
   cessation. This is a role in the shared physical description. The oxygen/
   enabling contrast is respected: an enabler's cessation also ends the
   system, but it is not described as the system's organization; the
   organizing activity is individuated by the system's own input/output
   organization.
2. *Non-vacuity (the CONSTITUTION_IS_NOT_PREDICTION §8 obligation, met).* An
   admitted alternative is specified: drive the boundary with no signal from
   b onward (f ≡ 0), medium and background laws intact. Then no carriage is in
   progress and the channel ceases to be a transmission system. The test is
   therefore not vacuously true (the §8 worry about empty alternative classes).
   The removal is a change of boundary input within the same model family, not
   an ontological deletion of an occurrence.
3. *Scope restriction honoring the C&R constraint.* P1 concerns only the
   *unity-ground*. It is analytic about "operative unifier" that it is
   operative at the assessment time. Grounds of present *facts* (records,
   positions, output values) may be past and cross-temporal; nothing here
   requires grounds generally to exist at the standpoint. The later-grounding
   reply (C&R) is thus preempted rather than ignored.

**P2 defense.**

1. *Individuation-tracking.* The item the physics individuates at b is the one
   in-transit carriage token (representation-invariantly: same input/output
   organization, exact support). An instantaneous-only presence semantics
   splits that occurring thing into presence-slices: the thing now mid-channel
   would be concrete only at its b-slice, and its being the token that entered
   at s would be a tenseless fact about a structure none of whose concrete
   present occupants sustains anything. That package (classical perdurance)
   is coherent, but it deflates the stipulated achievement: no slice sustains;
   sustaining is diachronic. Given the pilot's stipulation — genuine dynamical
   unity by the activity, which the designated rival accepts — P2 is the
   minimal way to keep that diachronic achievement in the concrete inventory.
   Denying P2 while retaining genuine operative sustaining is not a stable
   combination; this is the dilemma recorded as R4a in §7.
2. *Category argument.* Concrete presence has two modes: state-presence
   (an object's being-there at a time) and occurrence-presence (a process's
   ongoing). For a process token, occurring is the primary mode: a process
   neither occurring nor just-recorded is absent, not present. The elapsed
   portion of an occurring token is present because it is not a separate item:
   there is one token, and its elapsed extent is how much of it has occurred.
   Answering "what of the token is present?" with "an instant of it"
   misclassifies an occurrence as a stage-collection. This category view is
   adjacent to occupied process-ontology territory (§8); no originality is
   claimed for it, only its application as this bridge's pivotal premise.
3. *Independence from the conclusion.* P2 concerns the lower bound only. It
   does not say that nothing outside the window is concrete; that is
   P3-backward, which the rival will attack and the revision will retract. So
   P2 is not defended circularly by assuming exhaustiveness.

**P3-forward defense.** From the stipulation, S's being-one-here-now is
A's being operative here-now (the composite has no further presence-bearer).
By P2 the unifier is concretely present over its in-progress extent. By the
composite-presence principle — a composite is present *as this composite*
only where it is one as this composite, and it is one as this composite
exactly where its unifier is operative (stipulated) — S's concrete present
includes the operative window. A objector who grants P2 and the stipulation
but confines S's own presence to {b} separates the presence of the unity
achievement from the presence of the system it constitutes; that separation
contradicts the composite-presence principle, which is the stipulated
commitment under the pilot's design.

## 4. Strongest same-physics rival: the concrete-token realist (CTR)

The designated rival per PILOT_QUESTION. The CTR accepts every physical and
constitutive claim: the channel, the carriage tokens, exact support τ, P1's
role test and dissolution test, the stipulated sustaining unity, P2
(trivially — occurrences are concrete for the CTR), and P3-forward.

**CTR response to Attempt 1.**

(i) *P3-backward is a global doctrine in local clothing.* To say that nothing
outside W_b is present-for-S at b requires that completed tokens (exited
carriages) and all earlier events of S are not concrete as of b. Whether past
occurrences are concrete as of b is precisely the global dispute between
presentism, growing inventories, and event realism. No fact about S's
internal structure settles it: within S's description at b, completed phases
appear only as records (the interior field and register values), and records
underdetermine the metaphysics — the phase may be absent-with-record
(presentist reading) or present-as-occurred (growing/eternalist reading).
This is the project's domain-insensitivity result
(`EXPLANATORY_ROLE_TEST.md` §2) applied here: the restricted explanatory
schema is satisfied under all three inventory policies.

(ii) *The C&R warning bites on the exhaustiveness direction.* "The completed
carriage is no longer operative, hence not present" assumes that presence
requires grounds or occupants at the standpoint. Cross-temporal grounding is
legitimate; completed carriages can still ground present facts and, on the
growing/eternalist policies, remain concrete. P1's restricted contemporaneity
was about the unifier (definitional); extending it to *all* of S's history is
a different, undefended, and necessarily global premise.

(iii) *Generalization collapse.* If P3-backward were asserted for every
system, the resulting doctrine — each system's concrete inventory is its
operative window — just is a global presentism about composites. That
collapses the system-relative program into the global doctrine it was
designed to bypass (PILOT_QUESTION: keep Brading's program distinct from
global presentism).

(iv) *What the CTR concedes.* P2 + P3-forward force: the window is present,
width τ, physically located — and this is *not* parasitic on a global
simultaneous present. The CTR therefore cannot dismiss the physical-location
program's finding as contentless; something concrete-present with determinate
physically-fixed extent has been located.

**CTR verdict on Attempt 1:** the exhaustiveness half (P3-backward) is
unsupported; the argument establishes only P3-forward, which the CTR already
accepts. If the CTR can retain everything (and it can, on premises), the
claimed advance must be narrowed per BREAKTHROUGH_TEST item 4.

## 5. Independent second case: the Kepler pair (run before the revision)

The case is chosen to test the unrevised argument on a system whose unity
mechanism differs and which was not used to motivate any premise. It is the
pair flagged in `CONSTITUTIVE_SUBMISSION_REVIEW.md` §3: two bodies in circular
Kepler orbit; internal forces do no work (perpendicular to velocities), yet
momentum redirection is essential to the bound configuration — an ordinary
dynamically maintained pair that a gross-work criterion would wrongly exclude.

- Sustaining activity A_K: the continuous mutual interaction (momentum
  redistribution). P1's role test applies: remove the interaction (admitted
  alternative: release into free escape, background laws fixed) and the pair
  dissolves as a bound pair. The interaction is organizing — it is the pair's
  mutual organization — not enabling (background: stable star, absence of a
  third body; these are enablers).
- P2 applies: the interaction is in progress at b, hence present-for-the-pair.
- P3-forward yields: the pair's concrete present at b includes the operative
  extent of A_K.
- **The obstruction the second case exposes:** A_K has no completion token.
  It has run since binding, so its operative extent at b is (t_bind, b] —
  history-relative, unsteady, and not fixed by apparatus physics in the way
  τ is. Completed orbits are completed tokens but are *not* the operative
  unifier, so the argument correctly refuses to let periodicity pick a width:
  one orbital period is not the pair's present. Attempt 1's biconditional
  would therefore force either a history-relative width (time since binding)
  for such systems or silence. The channel gets a steady width τ from a
  completion-structured token; the pair gets none.

So the move is not vacuous — applied to a second case it gives a restrictive,
limiting verdict — and it reveals a scope condition that Attempt 1 never
stated: determinate widths come only from completion-structured sustaining
tokens.

## 6. The one revision (change log preserved)

**Change log.**

1. *Attempt 1* (§2, preserved above): P3 as biconditional; conclusion:
   concrete present = operative window, width τ.
2. *Rival response* (§4): P3-backward unsupported; requires a global
   doctrine about past occurrences; generalizes to a collapse of the
   system-relative program into global presentism.
3. *Second case* (§5): no completion token for the Kepler pair; unsteady
   width; scope condition discovered.
4. *Revision (the single permitted revision)*: retract P3-backward; replace
   P3 by **P3⁻ (forward-only presence)**: the operative extent of A at b is
   included in S's concrete present at b. Add the **Apparatus Condition
   (AC)**: determinate width claims require completion-structured sustaining
   tokens; for completion-free sustainers the account yields presence with
   history-relative width, and any sharper claim needs an independently
   motivated individuation (scale or protocol), which is further work.
   *Rejected alternatives for the revision:* (a) importing a global presentist
   premise — forbidden, and it would collapse the program (CTR point iii);
   (b) defining "concrete present" as "operative extent" — the
   restriction-by-definition shortcut, refused; (c) identifying S's present
   with the whole token or the whole history — strictly stronger than
   P3-backward and fails the same objection; (d) making the constitution-relay
   (whirlpool-style matter relay) the primary bridge — it tracks which matter
   is currently part of S, not presence-extent, and was set aside as
   illustration only.

No further revision is permitted by the brief; the residual objection (§7) is
recorded as answered-to-a-point, not eliminated.

## 7. Final result and rival-revision verdicts

**R1 (positive bridge, conditional on P2).** A physically located,
system-relative concrete present of a composite is justified as *at least*
the operative window of its sustaining activity. The channel's concrete
present at b includes (b−τ, b], width τ, fixed by representation-invariant
input/output physics. The presence-width explanandum — why is S's present
this wide? — is answered: because presence attaches (P2) to the occurring
sustainer, whose operative extent the apparatus fixes (G2).

**R2 (structural obstruction, the main negative result).** The exhaustiveness
half — S's concrete present is *exactly* the operative window — is not
deliverable by any system-relative constitutive argument. Settling the
concrete status of completed phases of S requires a doctrine about past
occurrences in general; that is the global ontology dispute, and no fact
internal to S's dynamics or organization decides it (records underdetermine;
all three inventory policies satisfy the restricted explanatory schema).
Hence: either the system-relative program lives with a lower-bound presence
claim, or it collapses into global presentism. This derives, rather than
stipulates, why Brading's system-relative program must be kept distinct from
global presentism — and it is a different, sharper diagnosis than the supplied
"the constitutive criterion is missing": a concrete criterion (P1–P3⁻) has
been supplied, and the remaining gap is located at one specific joint
(operative extent vs. concrete-as-of-standpoint inventory) with a structural
reason it cannot be closed locally.

**R3 (scope).** By AC: determinate widths only where sustaining tokens are
completion-structured (channel: yes). Completion-free sustainers (Kepler
pair) get presence with history-relative width; the argument refuses
gerrymandered widths (orbit cycles are not presents).

**R4 (what each rival family has a reason to revise — on explanatory
grounds, not definitions).**

- *R4a — instantaneous-stage presence theorist.* The state at b encodes the
  window without constituting it (honored control); and the stipulated
  sustaining unity is diachronic. Retaining both instant-only presence and
  genuine operative sustaining is unstable: accept P2 (window-inclusive
  presence of occurring sustainers) or explicitly deflate the stipulated
  unity into tenseless organization — exiting the pilot's common ground. The
  cost of deflation: the dissolution test loses its grip on concrete presence
  (stopping the sustainer would not affect the concrete stage), so the
  physical individuation of systems would no longer be borne by anything
  present.
- *R4b — CTR (designated rival).* May retain the fuller inventory. But must
  revise the running-together of presence and inventory: presence is
  physically located, system-relative, width-τ structure that any ontology
  must accommodate *as presence*, not merely as dynamics. Refusal costs the
  presence-width explanandum (R1): from the inventory alone the CTR cannot
  say why the system's present has this width; accepting P2 + P3⁻ inherits
  the answer, and the extra inventory then does no presence-explanatory work
  (it may do other work — cross-temporal explanation of records — which is
  granted).
- *R4c — growing theorist.* The window argument is neutral physics-side
  support: a sliding, physically located present-window with fixed width and
  no global simultaneity. Nothing here favors or defeats accumulation; noted
  so the bridge is not misread as presentist.

**Conditional status.** Everything turns on P2. A reviewer who rejects the
pilot's common-ground stipulation (holding that "genuine sustaining unity"
already begs the constitutive question) will find the bridge conditional in
exactly that premise — which is the pilot's design. The bridge does not
advance the constitutive step itself; it executes the constitutive-to-existence
step under the stipulation and maps precisely where it stops.

## 9. Shortcut checks and exposure tests

- *Prediction-to-constitution:* no predictions used; G2 is input/output
  physics about extent, and presence is inferred from P2, not from
  predictive sufficiency.
- *Temporal-grounding-to-current-existence:* P1 is restricted to the unity-
  ground and is definitional about "operative unifier"; grounds of present
  facts remain free to be cross-temporal (C&R honored explicitly, §3).
- *Support-to-unity:* unity is stipulated, never inferred from response
  support; the benchmark supplies only the extent G2.
- *Restriction-by-definition:* "present" is not defined as "operative"; the
  ascription is argued (P2 + composite-presence principle), and the rival's
  contrary presence semantics is met with the presence-width explanandum
  (R4b), not by stipulation. The place where restriction-by-definition would
  close the gap — exhaustiveness — is left open and reported (R2).
- *State-encodes-without-constituting control:* honored; the presence claim
  never runs through the encoding. Separation witness: a free particle's
  state at b determines its whole trajectory, but there is no sustaining
  activity, so P2 does not apply and no presence window follows — P2 is not
  determination.
- *Present disposition vs completed token activity:* disposition = q(b,·)
  (encodes, sustains nothing); completed tokens = exited carriages (sustain
  no longer; their concrete-status-as-of-b is the open global question R2);
  in-progress tokens = the window (present per P2). The weak-coupling note's
  question — what distinguishes continuing existence from the completion of
  one sustaining activity — is answered locally: continuing existence is the
  carriage regime's continuing; completed tokens never constitute the
  present; widths come only from in-progress, completion-structured tokens.
- *Vacuity test:* not every width can be declared present — the Kepler case
  yields no determinate width, and completed tokens/enablers are excluded
  despite passing counterfactual-dependence tests.
- *Known test:* priority comparisons in §8; no claim that window physics,
  process categories, or interval-present phrasing are new.
- *Invalid test:* if P2 could be derived from G2, the argument would collapse
  into the invalid control; the free-particle witness above blocks that.
- *Physical-irrelevance test:* the width is read off without metaphysical
  add-ons (representation-invariant exact support).
