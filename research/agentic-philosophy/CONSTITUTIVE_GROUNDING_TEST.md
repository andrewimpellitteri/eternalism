# Worked attempt: from constitutive activity to concrete existence

31 October 2026. Coordinator application of the
[question card](PILOT_QUESTION.md), written before inspecting the new GLM
pilot's research output. One candidate, one revision. This is a public
argument artifact, not private reasoning or a blinded experiment. No novelty
or complete reconstruction of a named philosopher is claimed.

## The claim to earn

A genuine system's unity is realized by an activity extending through a
region R. Does this give a reason to restrict concrete existence to R,
rather than merely a reason to recognize a concrete process occupying R?

For comparison, let H label the represented history and b the assessment
standpoint. E_b is the subset interpreted as concrete from b in the model
comparison, not an ontologically neutral definition of existence for every
theory. R is nonempty and proper in H. H's role in the metalanguage does
not assert that all of its represented events exist from b.

Two targets must be separated: recognizing concrete parts of the activity,
and excluding other events from concrete reality. For a system-relative
proposal, also distinguish the parts of this particular system-token from
all events that are real from its standpoint. A bounded process can have
exactly its own parts while events outside it are also concrete.

## Candidate 1: grounding must be concrete

1. S exists as a genuine unity at b.
2. S's unity at b is constituted by an actually realized activity R,
   rather than by the mathematical description or prediction of R.
3. Whatever actually constitutes a concretely existing unity must itself
   be concrete from the assessment standpoint at which that unity exists.
4. Therefore all the occurrences realizing R are concrete from b.
5. Since R is the extent constituting S, R is the exhaustive concrete
   present for S; the larger completed history is ontologically dispensable.

The initial reason for premise3 is factivity: an unreal realization seems
unable to constitute a real unity. The appeal is substantive. A proposed
constitution whose realizing occurrences never occur would not be an actual
realization. But that reason licenses their occurrence; it does not yet
license their existence *from the original standpoint*. A theory may use
cross-temporal constitutive or grounding operators. Whether that is a good
theory needs argument, not substitution of an ordinary currently quantified
relation. C&R's later-grounding position is a known warning about this step,
not an established account of physical constitution.

The strongest event-realist response grants premises1–4. All the activity's
events are concrete, as are other actual occurrences. It therefore accepts
the claimed process constitution without accepting step5. That inference
does not follow even if premise3 is granted.

### Exact inclusion test

Retain the same physical activity, unity and constitutive facts. Compare

    E_b^A=R,      E_b^B=H.

Both satisfy R⊆E_b. Thus any argument whose only inventory requirement
is that constituting occurrences are concrete cannot distinguish them.
The inclusion conclusion gives a lower bound, not an exhaustive upper bound.

This is a restricted countermodel to the displayed inference, not a theorem
that every temporal ontology admits both expansions. Further axioms about
grounding, maximality, parthood, tense or reference might block one expansion.
If they do, identify their independent rationale. A nonmonotone criterion of
unity also needs examination rather than being assumed unchanged.

For a local reading, E_b^B may be replaced by a larger admitted domain R′
containing R. The same logical point holds. But if the claimed conclusion
concerns only S-token's parthood, the expansion test is not a counterexample
to that mereology: outside events need not become parts of S.

## Independent case: disposition versus a completed return

Consider the effective driven oscillator q″+q=u. This is a mathematical
test of two property descriptions, not a proposed fundamental unity model.
Compare q_A(t)=cos(t),u_A=0 with

    q_B(t)=cos(t)+εχ(t),
    u_B(t)=ε[χ″(t)+χ(t)],

where χ is smooth, zero for t≤π/4 and one for t≥π/2, with bounded χ′.
Take ε>0 sufficiently small that ε||χ′||∞<sin(π/4), and ε<1.
For example, with ρ(s)=0 for s≤0 and exp(−1/s) for s>0, use
χ(t)=ρ(t−π/4)/[ρ(t−π/4)+ρ(π/2−t)]. Its denominator is always positive,
and the constant exterior pieces make χ′ bounded, so both ε requirements
can be met at once.

The histories and all derivatives agree near b=0. Their equation form is
the same; the later external forcing differs. The global driving data are
not held fixed. Neither determinism nor uniqueness is challenged.

On the transition interval q_B′=−sin(t)+εχ′(t)<0. Outside that interval
q_B′=−sin(t). Hence before or at 2π the only positive-time possible full
returns of q_B to (q,q′)=(1,0) are at π or2π. Their positions are −1+ε
and1+ε, so neither is a return. A has its first full return at2π.

The presently specified oscillator law and state are shared, while the
completed-return property differs. This does not establish a current
disposition difference: a disposition may be masked by later forcing.
Nor does it show that a completed cycle is presently instantiated in A.
That latter claim would supply the contested temporal bridge, not follow
from the return calculation. An instantaneous theorist may treat the
completed return as a temporally extrinsic fact; an event realist may
accept it as a concrete process. Neither must deny the calculation.

Thus a revision cannot silently replace “has an oscillatory disposition”
with “has already/actually realized a complete token cycle”. It also cannot
promote this controlled effective example to a closed relativistic universe.

## One revision: make the activity the object's parts

Avoid the assessment-sensitive grounding premise. Propose instead:

> A dynamically individuated system-token just is the concrete organization
> of carrier occurrences in its sustaining activity R. Those occurrences
> are its parts; no further history is needed to make this token that unity.

This makes process constitution explicit. It has a reason beyond prediction:
the organizing activity, rather than a data encoding, realizes the claimed
relations between carriers. Whether the specified activity is sufficient
for genuine unity remains a physical/philosophical question.

The strongest rival can nevertheless accept this finite process-token,
its parts and organization, alongside other concrete processes and later
activity. Not needing later events as parts of this token does not show
they are nonexistent. Identifying a continuing object's entire identity
with the token would add a further persistence thesis. Even that would not
exclude distinct later tokens or other concrete systems.

Accordingly the revision earns, at most, a process-mereological proposal.
It does not earn the original inventory restriction. Calling its support
the system's “present” would not answer the rival. No second revision is
introduced by weakening the target after this failure.

This defeats the displayed entailment, not every explanatory argument for
the revised ontology. To compare explanatory preferences, investigate why
each inventory is warranted and what each explains. Mere compatibility of
the rival with the activity does not establish equal explanatory merit.
The [assessment method's false-negative risks](BREAKTHROUGH_TEST.md)
apply here as much as to the worker proposal.

its controversial grounding premise. The revision removes one problem but
