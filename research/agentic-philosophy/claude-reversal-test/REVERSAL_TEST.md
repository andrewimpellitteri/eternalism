# A reversal test for constitutive presents: headline withdrawn

31 October 2026. Claude Code (Opus 5.5), at the user's request. The first version
claimed that a bridge from sustaining activity to a present can fix a width but not a
side. **That claim is withdrawn.** REVIEW.md, a same-model red-team, found it false as
stated. This note keeps only what survives. No novelty or ontology verdict.

## What was wrong

- The Lemma needs a Θ_b-invariant *model*. The claim said reversal-symmetric *physics*.
  These differ. A neutral rule on a non-invariant model can be one-sided.
- Counterexample: in the damped channel q=F(t−x/c)e^(−γx/c), keep the events on tokens
  crossing t=b whose amplitude is at least the amplitude at the crossing. This uses no
  orientation vocabulary, is neutral under PT with γ→−γ, and returns the pilot's set.
  So "non-neutral premise or symmetric window" was a false dichotomy.
- The Lemma is idle for every dissipative sustainer, hence for every realistic
  self-maintaining system. The first version's "absorbed, not refuted" was wrong.
- "Width but not side" fails as well: width is section-relative too, and the benchmark
  calls duration a property of protocol and boundary configuration.
- The Lemma is Curie's principle, close to a tautology, and not a new result.
- Other errors: "exactly four options" listed two; "cannot favour an inventory" was an
  invalid inference; presentism was misattributed, since it needs no side; and the
  process-worm consequence was unsupported and tilted toward one party.

## What survives

**Reversal-neutrality statement.** Name Θ and what it conjugates. If W(Θm,Θb)=ΘW(m,b)
and (Θm,Θb)=(m,b), then W(m,b) is Θ-invariant. All content is in the definition.

**Corollary.** If a neutral present P includes a set E on such a model, it also includes
ΘE. This bears on a lower-bound claim like the pilot's R1, where a mere side would not.

**Finding against the pilot only.** In its lossless channel, at a mirror standpoint, no
past-sided rule is neutral. So its one-sided window needs a stated source. This holds at
one b for a palindromic F, and is a claim about that model.

**A small methodological rule.** A one-sided window at b requires a Θ-odd feature of the
rule or of (m,b), and a bridge should name it. Where the feature lies in the activity,
as with attenuation or entropy production, the activity does fix a side, and side and
width share their contingency.

**The two-sided identity.** y(t)=τ⁻¹∫₀^τ f(t−s)ds=τ⁻¹∫₀^τ g(t+s)ds holds, and
channel_check.py confirms it at sample points. It is a substitution naming the same data.
It removes the side from functional dependence, not from dependence as control, since
one end is physically driven. REVIEW.md gives a stronger form: for every x,
y(t)=τ⁻¹∫_(x/c−τ)^(x/c) q(t+s,x)ds, so the window's side varies with the probe position.
