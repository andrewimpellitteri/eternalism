# Agent report: `ctp-fresh-proof-audit`

## Result

No falsifier was found for the iff claim on the labeled sigma-algebra
generated by doubly-principal cylinders, assuming the standard finite-complex-
measure convention. The primary-source definitions match the target domain.
The all-parameter nonextension proof can be shortened: enumerate every
naturally labeled order `G` at level `k`, cap it twice to obtain all
doubly-principal cylinders at level `k+2`, and use the exact level sum
`V_(k+2) = a Σ_G t^(u(G)+v(G)) r^u s^v ≥ a t^k F_k(s)`. Here
`F_k(s)=∏_{j=1}^k(1-s^j)≥c_s>0`. Thus the level sums diverge for `t>1`,
contradicting finite total variation. This shorter route was suggested in a
coordinator follow-up; I independently checked the derivation and finite
instances. The earlier principal-only Harris route is also valid, but
unnecessary.

This result concerns `\tilde{R}_p`; it does not settle extension on the
covariant intersection `R_p=\tilde{R}_p∩R`. The full-cylinder extension
theorems do not directly settle it: lifting a measure from a smaller
sigma-algebra preserves that measure on the smaller algebra, not arbitrary
prescribed values on all missing cylinder sets.

As a separate requested check, the conditional covariant rule in
`research/creative/covariant-boundary/BOUNDARY.md` is internally consistent.
Under that extra rule, the same exact doubly-principal level sum groups by
unlabeled shape and gives variation lower bound `|c(q)|V_(k+2)`, so it
contradicts finiteness for `|q|<1` off `[0,1]`. This remains conditional on
the additional post-event values; it does not transfer the bare labeled
theorem to `R_p`.

I also checked the stronger proposed factorization on **all principal**
unlabeled `H`, with `ν(P∞)=1`: the family of all `D_H` is laminar and covers
`P∞`, so κ=0 is impossible by finite-union inclusion-exclusion and
continuity from below. For κ≠0, the level-sum bound excludes nonclassical
`p`; `p=0` is separately impossible because all rank-at-least-2 amplitudes
vanish while those events cover each `D_singleton`. Classical CTP gives
existence for `0<p≤1`. This stronger classification fails if factorization
is imposed only on doubly-principal `H`; an ordinal sum of repeated
two-antichain-plus-post blocks supplies a Dirac counterexample to the κ=0
exclusion.
