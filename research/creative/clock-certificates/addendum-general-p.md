# General-parameter skeptic addendum: doubly-principal variation bound

**Status:** conditional proof audit of a proposed all-parameter extension
obstruction. The conclusion is specific to the CTP cylinder pre-measure on
\(\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\), generated by doubly-principal cylinders. It does not
imply non-extension on the covariant intersection \(\mathcal R_p\).

## 1. Claim under review

For every \(p\notin[0,1]\), set
\[
 a=|p|,\quad b=|1-p|,\quad t=a+b>1,\quad x=a/t,\quad y=b/t.
\]
Then \(x,y\in(0,1)\), \(x+y=1\). The proposed argument gives, for a
finite principal-block size \(k\), total seam-inclusive absolute weight of
doubly-principal blocks
\[
 W_k\ \geq\ a t^k F_\infty(y)^2,
 \qquad F_\infty(y):=\prod_{j=1}^\infty(1-y^j)>0.
\]
Since \(a>0\), \(t>1\), and \(F_\infty(y)>0\), this exceeds one for some
finite \(k\). Repeating that finite family yields unbounded variation and
therefore no finite countably additive complex-measure extension to
\(\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\).

## 2. Three closest primary predecessors

1. Dowker, Johnston, and Surya, [*On extending the Quantum Measure*,
   arXiv:1007.2725v1 (2010), Secs. 2 and 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2725).
   They show bounded variation is necessary for the relevant complex/vector
   pre-measure to extend and establish non-extension for complex percolation on
   the full cylinder sigma algebra except at \(p\in[0,1]\). Their
   domain includes the doubly-principal cylinders, but a measure on the smaller
   generated sigma algebra need not extend to their larger sigma algebra, so
   their theorem alone does not settle this restricted-domain question.
   **Read depth:** full-text for the bounded-variation argument and stated
   result; proof dependencies were not fully audited.
2. Surya and Zalel, [*A Criterion for Covariance in Complex Sequential Growth
   Models*, arXiv:2003.11311 (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.11311).
   They derive extension criteria for complex sequential-growth models and
   discuss complex percolation. The present argument instead lower-bounds
   variation directly on the family of doubly-principal generators. The
   criterion's hypotheses and cited measure-theoretic dependencies have not
   been reconstructed independently here. **Read depth:** full-text at the
   relevant result/discussion.
3. Dowker and Zalel, [*Observables for cyclic causal set cosmologies*,
   arXiv:2212.01149v2 (17 July 2023), Sec. V, Theorems 14–15 and Eqs. (30)–(31)]
   (https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.01149).
   They define \(\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\) through doubly-principal cylinders and
   report that their extrema-based criteria are inconclusive for complex CTP:
   valency-one nodes defeat the minimum-zeta test, while the maximum criterion
   does not establish extension. The proposed block argument targets that
   precise algebra and would settle it for all \(p\notin[0,1]\), if the proof
   and domain match hold. Their \(\mathcal R_p=\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\cap\mathcal R\)
   identity prevents transferring this conclusion to \(\mathcal R_p\).
   **Read depth:** full-text at the cited section.

The novelty search in this addendum is limited to these primary predecessors
and targeted searches around their extension results. A complete forward
citation search has not been done; no priority claim follows.

## 3. Weight identity and lower bound

For a naturally labeled finite causet \(C\), let \(L(C)\) count links,
\(R(C)\) related pairs, and \(M(C)\) minimal elements. Write
\[
 A(C)=p^{L(C)}q^{\binom{|C|}{2}-R(C)},\quad q=1-p,
 \qquad B(C)=p^{M(C)}A(C).
\]
Here \(B\) is the amplitude factor contributed when \(C\) is appended above
an earlier principal prefix: the seam contributes exactly one link from the
prefix's unique maximum to each minimal element of \(C\). For the set of
naturally labeled principal causets of size \(k\), define
\[
 R_k(a,b)=\sum_{G\text{ principal},\ |G|=k}
 a^{L(G)+M(G)}b^{\binom{k}{2}-R(G)}
 =\sum_G|B(G)|.
\]
For each summand let
\(E(G)=L(G)+M(G)+\binom{k}{2}-R(G)\). The undirected Hasse diagram of a
finite poset with a unique maximum is connected, so \(L(G)\geq k-1\); also
\(M(G)\geq1\), and the gap exponent is nonnegative. Hence \(E(G)\geq k\), and
\[
 R_k(a,b)=\sum_G t^{E(G)}x^{L(G)+M(G)}y^{\binom{k}{2}-R(G)}
 \geq t^k R_k(x,y).
\]

The real sum \(R_k(x,y)\) has a probability interpretation. Run forward
Bernoulli transitive percolation on labels \(0,1,\ldots,k\): each newly born
vertex independently chooses each earlier vertex with probability \(x\),
then take transitive closure. The probability of a particular labeled
resulting order \(C\) is
\(x^{L(C)}y^{\binom{|C|}{2}-R(C)}\). To see why, at each birth the maximal
elements of the new vertex's ancestor set must be selected; all optional
selections among its other ancestors sum to \((x+y)^m=1\), while unrelated
earlier elements must be unselected. This gives one \(x\) per new link and
one \(y\) per unrelated pair, with no multiplicity factor.

Let \(E_0\) be the event that label 0 is an ancestor of every other label,
and \(E_k\) the event that every earlier label is an ancestor of label \(k\).
On \(E_0\cap E_k\), the induced order on \(1,\ldots,k\) is principal; and
each principal \(G\) yields exactly the full order \(0\oplus G\) in that
event. Its probability weight is precisely the corresponding summand in
\(R_k(x,y)\), since the root-to-block seam contributes \(M(G)\) links.
Therefore
\[
 R_k(x,y)=\Pr(E_0\cap E_k).
\]

Both events are increasing events in the independent edge indicators.
Harris's inequality gives
\(\Pr(E_0\cap E_k)\geq\Pr(E_0)\Pr(E_k)\). For \(E_0\), expose vertices
successively: conditional on the root already reaching all earlier vertices,
the new vertex is reached iff at least one of its \(j\) possible incoming
edges is selected, which has probability \(1-y^j\). Thus
\[
 \Pr(E_0)=\prod_{j=1}^k(1-y^j)=F_k(y).
\]
Reversing the order gives the same probability for \(E_k\). Consequently
\[
 R_k(x,y)\geq F_k(y)^2\geq F_\infty(y)^2>0.
\]
The infinite product is strictly positive because \(0<y<1\) and
\(\sum_{j\geq1}y^j<\infty\): after finitely many terms, use
\(\log(1-z)\geq-2z\) for \(0\leq z\leq1/2\) to bound the tail product
away from zero. Combining bounds,
\[
 R_k(a,b)\geq t^k F_\infty(y)^2.
\]

Now cap every such principal \(G\) by a new universal maximum to obtain
\(H=G\oplus\{u\}\). This adds one link and \(k\) relations, so
\(A(H)=pA(G)\), \(M(H)=M(G)\), and \(B(H)=pB(G)\). Each \(H\) is doubly
principal, and their aggregate seam-inclusive block weight satisfies
\[
 W_k:=\sum_H|B(H)|=aR_k(a,b)\geq a t^k F_\infty(y)^2.
\]
For example, any integer \(k\) with
\[
 k>\frac{\log(1/(aF_\infty(y)^2))}{\log t}
\]
ensures the displayed lower bound is greater than one (if the right side is
negative, any positive \(k\) suffices). This is an existence bound; it does
not assert a practical small block size.

## 4. Repetition and extension obstruction

Fix a finite \(k\) with \(W_k>1\), and take a one-element initial order
\(C_0\). For every tuple \((H_1,\ldots,H_m)\), form
\(C_m=C_0\oplus H_1\oplus\cdots\oplus H_m\), with fixed consecutive label
intervals for the blocks. Each endpoint is principal. It is doubly principal
because deleting its final universal top leaves the ordinal sum ending in
\(G_m\), which is principal. Thus each endpoint cylinder belongs to the
generating family of \(\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\).

At each seam, cross-block relations are all present, so there is no cross
factor of \(q\), and the cross-links are exactly from the previous prefix's
unique maximum to the new block's minimal elements. The intrinsic endpoint
amplitude is therefore
\[
 A(C_m)=A(C_0)\prod_{j=1}^m B(H_j),\qquad |A(C_0)|=1.
\]
At a fixed checkpoint size, different tuples give different labeled orders,
since restriction to each fixed block label interval recovers that block.
Their cylinder sets are pairwise disjoint. The sum of absolute values on
these finite disjoint families is
\[
 \sum_{(H_1,\ldots,H_m)}|A(C_m)|=W_k^m\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
This is unbounded total variation on the generated algebra. Any countably
additive complex measure on the sigma algebra has bounded total variation;
therefore the CTP assignments cannot extend to a finite complex measure on
\(\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\).

Normalization and cancellation do not defeat this argument. The transition
sum rule sums complex child amplitudes over all children; the variation bound
uses absolute values on a finite disjoint subfamily. The \(G\mapsto H\) cap is
injective after restricting to the first \(k\) labels, and all labeled
principal endpoints are counted once.

## 5. Falsifiers and scope checks

The proof depends on these precise facts: (a) the extension sought assigns
\(A(C)\) to every doubly-principal cylinder; (b) the real transitive-percolation
probability of a labeled order is the displayed link/unrelated-pair weight;
(c) the two endpoint-connectivity events are increasing under the same
independent-edge product measure; (d) principal posets have connected Hasse
graphs; and (e) the repeated endpoints remain in the generating algebra and
are distinct labeled cylinders. The definitions in the cited papers support
(a) and (e); the arguments above prove (b)–(d). A mismatch in the intended
pre-measure/domain would invalidate the conclusion.

The conclusion is only for \(\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\). The paper's covariant
algebra is \(\mathcal R_p=\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\cap\mathcal R\), a smaller
sigma algebra. Non-extension on a larger algebra does not rule out extension
on a smaller subalgebra. The argument makes no claim about \(\mathcal R_p\),
other growth models, or temporal ontology. For \(p\in[0,1]\), the known real
probability measure on the full cylinder sigma algebra supplies an extension;
the present argument intentionally uses strict \(t>1\) outside this range.

## 6. Disposition and novelty boundary

**Mathematical status:** the inequalities and endpoint probability identity
are proved on the stated finite Bernoulli DAG model. Harris's inequality is
applied to two increasing events under one product measure. The positive
infinite-product bound justifies choosing some finite \(k\); it does not give
an efficient numerical value for \(k\). The full repeated-family no-extension
claim is conditional only on matching the paper's extension problem to these
prescribed cylinder values.

**Potential contribution:** a direct all-\(p\) bounded-variation obstruction
on \(\tilde{\mathcal R}_p\), where Dowker–Zalel's 2023 analysis is
inconclusive. This is stronger in parameter range than the earlier fixed
\(p\) certificate, while using a different route: normalize the modulus
weights into a real product measure, lower-bound a two-sided connectivity
event, then use \(t^k\) growth.

**Not established:** novelty/global priority is unverified; there is no result
for the covariant intersection \(\mathcal R_p\), no theorem beyond complex
transitive percolation on this algebra, and no temporal-ontological inference.
