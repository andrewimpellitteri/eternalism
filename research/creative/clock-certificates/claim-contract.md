# Clock-certificate lane: defeated hypotheses and remaining target

**Status (2026-10-19): no positive novelty claim selected.** This is a scoped
failure result from a targeted primary-source audit, not evidence that the
subject has no open problems. Read depth is stated per source. No ontology
conclusion follows from any result below.

## Hypotheses considered

### H1 — A finite periodic clock with a non-commuting interaction cannot have exact autonomous relational evolution

**Proposed form.** Let a finite clock and system have a periodic group action,
and let the clock-system interaction fail to commute with the clock generator.
Conjecture that no covariant instrument can produce an exact relational
description generated by one time-independent system Hamiltonian.

**Disposition: reject by finite counterexample to the broad form.** In the
finite Abelian model of Ludescher et al., Example 32, take \(n=3,K=1\). The
interaction is \(V=(X_1-X_2)^2\). It commutes with the *global* translation
generator \(P_1+P_2\), as required for their Z3 constraint, but it does not
commute with the local first-particle clock generator \(P_1\): shifting only
particle 1 changes the difference coordinate and hence V. Nevertheless, the
authors exhibit a covariant center-of-mass instrument whose reduced system
state advances by the fixed unitary in Eq. 60. Thus noncommutation with a
chosen subsystem clock Hamiltonian alone does not prohibit exact autonomous
relational evolution for another declared instrument/frame. The same example
shows the local first-particle reduction has a step-dependent generator. The
remaining narrow question would have to fix the local frame and specify a
noncommuting coupling and physical reduction domain; it is not a general
no-go, and the current evidence does not establish a novel theorem there.

### H2 — Non-ideal finite-clock outcome overlap breaks the Dirac/PW/Heisenberg trinity

**Proposed form.** A finite covariant clock POVM with nonorthogonal reading
states makes the three relational reductions disagree in physical
probabilities, even when all use the same effects and physical inner product.

**Disposition: reject as already occupied at the stated level.** The 2019
trinity constructs relational Dirac observables with covariant POVMs that
include non-ideal clocks and proves equivalence of the Dirac, Page–Wootters,
and relational-Heisenberg descriptions under its non-interacting
clock/system constraint. The 2024 periodic-clock paper extends the trinity to
periodic clocks, including non-ideal covariant U(1) POVMs. The 2026
instrument-QRF paper proves reversibility of its covariant rank-one instrument
perspectives. A counterexample would therefore have to change a named premise
(e.g. the interaction, a non-isometric instrument, an operational quotient, or
the accessible subspace) and show a different allowed probability. Merely
changing normalization or representation is not enough.

### H3 — Autonomy versus information leakage gives a new finite-clock tradeoff

**Proposed form.** In a finite clock instrument, the failure of a relational
reduction to be a fixed unitary is quantitatively equivalent to information
leaked to an inaccessible complement, with a sharp finite-dimensional bound.

**Disposition: do not select yet.** This is the only direction with a possible
mathematical delta, but its obvious forms translate into established
covariant quantum error correction and Knill–Laflamme conditions. Hayden et al.
prove a finite-dimensional no-go for perfect erasure correction under Lie-group
covariance and construct finite-group codes. Faist, Woods, Albert, Renes, Eisert,
and Preskill derive a
time-energy/QFI tradeoff for an idealized noisy communication model and connect
zero sensitivity loss to time-covariant codes. Neither source, on the checked
material, states the proposed relational-instrument equivalence. That gap is
not yet a result: a valid contract needs a defined channel, environment,
accessible algebra, clock resource measure, and proof that the proposed
quantity is not exactly an existing complementary-channel or approximate-QEC
criterion. No bound is claimed here.

### H4 — A finite-clock frame-switching echo detects information loss

**Proposed form.** Switch a physical state from clock frame A to B and back to
A using non-ideal covariant instruments; quantify when the round trip fails to
recover all operational statistics, including interventions made during B.

**Disposition: reject the broad form.** The basic reversibility question is
already directly occupied. Carette, Głowacki, and Loveridge derive operational
frame-change maps and show they are invertible exactly when the final frame is
localizable. Castro-Ruiz and Oreshkov obtain reversible transformations by
including an extra particle that carries reference-frame information.
Castro-Ruiz, Galley, and Loveridge's 2026 three-qubit Z2 comparison shows that
perspective-neutral, extra-particle, and operational approaches can give
distinct maps because their accessible observables and global-state
assumptions differ. These are direct defeats of a broad echo/reversibility
claim. If the perspective changes are the
isometries in the instrument-QRF framework and the state remains in their
declared images, A→B→A composes to the identity by construction. If a step uses
a non-isometric outcome map or discards its outcome, the experiment is a
channel with an environment; exact recovery reduces to ordinary sufficiency,
Petz recovery, or Knill–Laflamme conditions. Requiring all interventions to be
mapped consistently is essential, but by itself defines a process-tensor
comparison, not a new theorem. Only a specific interacting-clock constraint
that imposes a new obstruction or a saturable bound beyond these recovery
criteria could survive. No such constraint or witness was found in this pass.

## One exact failed-conjecture result

The candidate obstruction “finite periodic relational dynamics with
non-ideal clocks is not an exact trinity” is false under the standard
non-interacting premise. Chataignier, Höhn, Lock, and Mele, arXiv:2409.06479v3,
state in the abstract and establish in Sec. VI that the Dirac, PW, and
relational-Heisenberg constructions are equivalent for periodic clocks; Sec.
III models the clock by covariant U(1) POVMs including non-ideal clocks. Their
Sec. II.2 explicitly assumes the clock and system do not interact. Thus the
failure of the candidate is bounded to that premise set and must not be
misread as an interacting-clock theorem.

The closest finite interacting witness is Example 32 of Ludescher et al.,
arXiv:2607.24976v1, Appendix B.2. It sets

\[
H=P_1+P_2+K(X_1-X_2)^2,\qquad K\in\mathbb Z_n,
\]
on two \(\mathbb C^n\) factors. Choose \(n=3,K=1\), so
\(V=(X_1-X_2)^2\). In the position basis, \(V|0,0\rangle=0\) while
\(V|1,0\rangle=|1,0\rangle\); the first-particle cyclic shift therefore
does not commute with V, which entails \([V,P_1]\ne0\). Since V commutes
with \(P_1+P_2\), this is still a valid \(\mathbb Z_3\) action (Eq. 58). The physical
kernel is spanned by \(|d;Kd^2\rangle\) (Eqs. 59 and following). For the
first-particle clock, the reduced step is generated by an explicitly
step-dependent operator (Eqs. 60's preceding lines); for the non-local
center-of-mass clock, Eq. 60 gives the fixed unitary
\(\exp[-i(2\pi/n)K(X_1-X_2)^2]\). This is an authors' worked example, not a
new calculation here. The coupling commutes with the *global constraint's*
translation part but not the local first-particle clock generator P1.

## Six-part claim contract for H3 (deliberately not passed)

1. **Exact target.** Determine whether, for a specified finite covariant clock
   instrument and declared physical code/reachable domain, the defect from a
   state-independent unitary relational step is bounded below or above by the
   complementary channel's distinguishability of encoded clock states. No
   inequality or theorem is currently asserted.
2. **Nearest predecessors.** (i) Höhn, Smith, Lock, *The Trinity of Relational
   Quantum Dynamics*, arXiv:1912.00033v3, Secs. II and IV–VI: non-interacting
   clock/system constraint, covariant clock POVMs and reduction maps, with
   equivalence among three pictures. (ii) Chataignier et al., *Relational
   Dynamics with Periodic Clocks*, arXiv:2409.06479v3, Secs. III, V–VI: periodic
   U(1) clocks, non-ideal POVMs, and periodic trinity, again with no
   clock-system interactions. (iii) Ludescher et al., *Quantum Reference
   Frames beyond Subsystems*, arXiv:2607.24976v1, Def. 10/Lemmas 12–14 and
   Example 32: rank-one covariant instruments give isometric perspective maps;
   a finite-Abelian interacting example has frame-dependent autonomy. (iv)
   Hayden et al., *Error Correction of Quantum Reference Frame Information*,
   PRX Quantum 2, 010326 (2021), arXiv:1709.04471v3, Sec. IV and Appendices:
   exact covariant-erasure correction is impossible in finite dimension for
   Lie groups with infinitesimal generators, while finite groups admit finite
   codes. (v) Faist, Woods, Albert, Renes, Eisert, and Preskill, *Time-Energy
   Uncertainty Relation for Noisy Quantum Metrology*, PRX Quantum 4, 040336
   (2023), arXiv:2207.13707v2, main
   theorem and Sec. VII: an idealized state-transmission noise model relates
   probe time sensitivity to environment energy sensitivity and connects
   zero-loss conditions to time-covariant codes. The last two papers concern
   encoding/noise/metrology, not relational reduction equivalence.
3. **Possible delta.** Only a theorem translating a precisely defined
   relational-isometry defect into a complementary-channel quantity, with a
   sharp bound outside the above coding/metrology statements, could be a
   mathematical delta. This remains a conjectural research question, not a
   demonstrated gap.
4. **Smallest example and independent route.** Start with a finite clock
   instrument \(M_g\), physical code isometry \(V\), a specified noise or
   discarded subsystem channel \(\mathcal N\), and a declared observable
   algebra. Compute both (a) the relational step's state dependence and (b)
   complementary-channel distinguishability. Independently check the claimed
   relation by Choi matrices and compare it term-by-term with the
   Knill–Laflamme conditions. No example has been calculated because choosing
   these objects would silently choose the theorem's content.
5. **Adversarial falsifiers.** The claim dies as a repackaging if the defect is
   exactly a norm of \(V^\dagger E_i^\dagger E_jV-c_{ij}I\), or if a Stinespring
   dilation makes the proposed leakage quantity the standard complementary
   channel distance. It dies as physically irrelevant if all observable
   probabilities on the declared algebra agree despite a state-vector
   difference. It is preempted if a clock-specific covariance/QEC paper gives
   the same inequality after relabeling. Search targets still needed: exact
   clock-information, covariant instrument, autonomous QRF, and approximate
   QEC literature, including forward citations of the two PRX Quantum papers.
6. **Novelty and consequence.** Mathematical novelty: unestablished.
   Formalization novelty: none claimed. Physical novelty: none until the
   instrument is operationally realizable and the bound constrains measurable
   probabilities or accuracy. Philosophical consequence: none; autonomy,
   information leakage, temporal passage, determinism, and ontology are
   distinct questions.

## Limits

run, no theorem was reproduced, and no claim of priority is made. The next
