# Initial priority check: limited and incomplete

Checked 2026-10-19. This records discovery coverage, not a certificate of novelty.

The exact target is extension of **prescribed complex transitive-percolation
cylinder values** to the labelled algebra generated by doubly-principal
cylinders. Full-cylinder nonextension, existence of other measures on this
algebra, and extension on its covariant intersection are different claims.

Primary comparison:

- [Dowker–Zalel, arXiv:2212.01149v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.01149v2),
  Secs. III.3–III.4 and V, Eqs. (26), (29)–(31): coordinator directly checked
  the amplitude, generator definition, covariant intersection, and inconclusive
  doubly-reduced analysis. These sections state the problem but do not contain
  the repeated-block proof in `RESULT.md`.
- [Zalel, Covariant Growth Dynamics, arXiv:2302.10582](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.10582),
  relevant discussion of covtree and measure extension: checked for an existing
  solution. Probability-measure extensions there do not require the prescribed
  complex cylinder values used here. No proof of this target was located.
- The 2010 and 2020 full-algebra predecessors and read limits are recorded in
  the worker contract and both independent proof reviews.
