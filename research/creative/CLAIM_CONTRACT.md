# Current claim contract: complex-percolation extension boundary

Checked 2026-10-19. Candidate mathematical contribution, not a priority
certificate or selection of a temporal ontology. This contract supersedes the
method description in the first creative-pass contract; that earlier proof
remains valid.

1. **Exact question and claims.** For the prescribed scalar cylinder values
   \(A_p(C)=p^{L(C)}(1-p)^{\binom{|C|}{2}-R(C)}\), does a countably additive
   finite complex measure exist on the labelled sigma-algebra generated by
   doubly-principal cylinders? The [theorem draft](RESULT.md) gives **iff
   \(p\in[0,1]\)**. Its quantitative estimate is
   \(V_{k+2}\ge |p|(|p|+|1-p|)^k c_s\), with
   \(s=|1-p|/(|p|+|1-p|)\) and \(c_s=\prod_{j\ge1}(1-s^j)>0\)
   outside that interval. A [conditional covariant corollary](COVARIANT_RESULT.md)
   asks for unit measure on infinite-post histories and one common tail factor
   \(\nu(D_H)=\kappa N(H)A_p(H)\) for **all principal** post-past shapes.
   Under that added rule, existence holds **iff \(0<p\le1\)**. Covariance
   alone does not imply the rule.

3. **What changes and relevance.** The mathematical delta is an elementary
   estimate on the precise smaller labelled event algebra, plus the explicit
   conditional covariant boundary and its necessary all-principal scope.
   It closes one route to countably additive scalar-amplitude models while
   leaving other quantum measures, decoherence functionals and covariant value
   rules untouched. The number of agents or use of automation adds no novelty.

4. **Reproduction and independent verification.** Every doubly-principal
   \((k+2)\)-order is uniquely an arbitrary \(k\)-order capped twice.
   Covers increase by \(M(G)+1\), where \(M\) counts maxima; gaps stay fixed.
   Normalized real weights sum to the probability of a universal maximum,
   \(\prod_{j=1}^k(1-s^j)\). The
   [independent exact script](proof-audit/verify_small.py) checks the identities
   for \(k=0,\ldots,4\), rational parameters, exact symmetric-point sums, and
   finite future-post factors. The [fresh audit](proof-audit/AUDIT.md)
   reconstructs the all-level proof and challenges the covariant corollary.
   Finite checks corroborate the proof; they are not its quantifier extension.

5. **Defeaters and kill criteria.** A prior proof of this same prescribed-value
   classification defeats priority; a broad full-cylinder theorem does so only
   if it actually forces the missing cylinder values. A mismatch in the primary
   generator algebra, cover/gap exponents, endpoint probability, or disjointness
   defeats the mathematical proof. The covariant result requires the stated
   common factor and cyclic normalization; changing them changes the model.
   A concrete Dirac example in COVARIANT_RESULT.md defeats the weakened
   doubly-principal-only rule with zero factor. No claim of observable new
   physics survives without an operational model and empirical prediction.
   Forward-citation searches found no matching proof at their recorded depths,
   but inaccessible indexes and unscreened work remain. The conditional result
   received a later, [separate targeted search](priority-audit/POST_TAIL_PRIORITY.md).
