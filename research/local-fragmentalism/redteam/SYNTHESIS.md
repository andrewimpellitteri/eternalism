# Red-team synthesis

25 October 2026. Merges reports 01–07 (method: `METHOD.md`; groups 08–09 not
run). Organised by the claim attacked, not by school. Most worker sources are
from memory or discovery depth (web search was largely blocked), so every
attribution below is a lead until checked against a primary text. Rakić 1997
was checked (full text, repo root `bjps%2F48.2.257.pdf`).

## Verdict

No group found an error in the theorem. Every group found the *bridge* from
the theorem to the metaphysics overstated. The attacks converge on five
points, and one cheap restructuring answers most of them. The view survives
as a conditional thesis about grounding, not as a "resolution of the divide".

## Attacks by target

### A. Is it eternalism with tensed vocabulary? (O2, O12, R3) — serious
Raised independently by 01, 02, 04, 05 and 06.
- **Translation argument (02):** every P and R statement translates into
  tenseless causal-order facts plus a quantifier restricted to an index. If
  "real" means real full stop, the view is eternalism with restricted
  quantifiers; if it means only "real from b", it is indexed semantics, i.e.
  a perspectival B-theory. Deng, Price and Ismael accept R1–R3 and decline
  the gloss. The current O2 reply does not block this.
- **Inclusion is not passage (01, 04):** nested fragments are an order, not a
  happening; the totality in P7 is a static block (moving-spotlight,
  growing-block and Maudlin's objections).
- **"Present" equivocates (06):** the apex of a cone is not lived presence
  (Husserl, Heidegger, Merleau-Ponty, Ricoeur).
- **Direction is imported (02, 04):** the theorem is time-symmetric; the past
  lobe enters through (CB)/P2. "Directed becoming" (R3) is an input.

### B. P7 and the view from the whole (R6, R6′, O13) — serious
Raised by 05, 06 and 03.
- The theory quantifies over all of M, real from no event-standpoint; P7
  supplies that vantage but makes the eternalist totality the theory's home
  (05).
- Region standpoints (e.g. hypersurfaces) readmit Fine-style shared presents,
  contradicting R2 and O11(b) (05).
- A coherent totality makes the view Fine's *relativism*, not fragmentalism,
  and Hofweber–Lange returns as an explanatory-priority dilemma (03).
- Totalizing overview (Bergson, Levinas) (06).

### C. P5 (standpoint-invariant tenseless facts) — serious, formal
Raised by 05. No event is real from every standpoint, so "holds from every
standpoint" yields no absolute facts about events. Replace it with
persistence along ≤ plus agreement on overlaps. Fragments then grow as event
sets but not as sets of facts. There is also no semantics for future-tensed
or spacelike claims; a Belnap-style completion semantics is missing.

### D. Locality and causal sufficiency (P3, P3w, P6, O6) — serious
- **Bell (04, 07):** if dependence on spacelike events counts as causal
  influence, P6 with transitivity makes everything real from b. Tumulka's
  rGRWf is an event ontology with a nonlocal law and no foliation. Stapp
  reportedly left the Whiteheadian causal-past picture over Bell. P6 must be
  restricted to *signalling* influence, and that restriction must be argued.
- **Foliation presentism (01, 04):** in nearly FLRW cosmologies the metric
  defines a canonical foliation, so a shared now may cost no *added* structure,
  only non-locality. That weakens R8, R9 and the "diagnosis". (The paper's
  Proposition 5 already shows such rules violate (LC); the dispute is whether
  that is a cost.)
- **Off-shell (04):** R1, R4 and R9 hold for the kinematic category only;
  P3w's rationale is dynamical, the proof's use is kinematic.

### E. Novelty — narrower than stated
- **Whitehead** (07, 06): an occasion's "actual world" reportedly is its
  backward light cone (Stapp 1977; Hättich 2004; secondary). **Čapek**
  1961/1975: local becoming (unverified). If confirmed, the *content* of the
  fragment is not new.
- **Rakić 1997** (03; checked): event-relative transitive realization, with
  Stein cited. She argues it is not definable from Minkowski causal geometry
  and adds it as a primitive, i.e. she takes the added-structure horn. Her
  sea-fight case (an elsewhere event settled by a decision in b's past) is a
  direct challenge to R1. The Stein paper's description was corrected the
  same day.
- **Also cited as leads:** branching space-times (settledness = causal
  downset), Dowker/Sorkin causal-set becoming (the discrete case), Savitt 2005
  and 2006, Dieks 2006, Arthur 2006, Goldblatt 1980 (the modal logic of
  Minkowski causal order), Earman 2008 (unread; main risk for the theorem
  itself).
- **What no group found:** any derivation of the fragment's content from a
  locality theorem across curved spacetimes. That remains the defensible core.

### F. Smaller points (manageable)
- Persisting objects beyond J^-(b) (Sider, Hawley) (02).
- Why every event is a standpoint (Ismael) (02).
- Referring to the spacelike "Sun now" (01).
- Open future: "not real from b" does not make the future open (07, 01).
- Point events as basic units; the worldline corollary is available (06).
- Quantum field theory does not pick I^- over J^-: the timelike tube theorem
  suggests I^-, massless propagation J^- (04).

## Recommended restructuring (one edit, not yet made)

1. **Re-scope the headline:** local fragmentalism is a *grounding* thesis.
   Tensed facts are grounded in the causal past of a standpoint. Concede that
   it agrees with eternalism on every truth condition and every observation
   (answers A, partly D). Drop "resolves the divide", "uniqueness" and
   "directed becoming".
2. **Make the view without P7 the official one;** keep P7 only as an optional
   add-on and state the stating problem openly (answers B).
3. **Replace P5** by persistence along ≤ plus agreement on overlaps, and give
   an explicit object language. Hybrid S4 over the causal order is the
   general base; Goldblatt's S4.2 holds only for Minkowski spacetime and fails
   with particle horizons (NEW_PAPERS_CHECK.md) (answers C).
4. **Restrict P6 to signalling influence,** and say plainly that Bell-nonlocal
   dynamics would be a falsifier (answers D-Bell).
5. **Add a scope paragraph:** neutral on lived time, the open future, the arrow
   and duration (answers A-phenomenology, F-open future).
6. **Rewrite the novelty claim** as the locality derivation only, pending
   Whitehead, Čapek, Earman 2008, Lipman 2026 and Iaquinto–Torrengo ch. 4.
