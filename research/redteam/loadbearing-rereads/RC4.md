# RC-4 reread: record distinguishability in sequential growth

Coordinator draft, 2026-10-20, after `kb-csg-quantum-boundary` (merged
2026-10-20). Passages re-read from arXiv HTML fetched 2026-10-20. Not a novelty
claim.

## Verdict: SHRINK (classical finite-stage part KILLED)

**Killed.** The coordinator's 2026-10-19 derivation (finite stage, fixed
covariant growth: labelled outcome uniform over natural labellings, so birth
order is ancillary) is already published. Brightwell–Luczak 0901.0240v3, Sec. 1:
"conditional on the causal set at some stage k being equal to some unlabeled
k-element poset P, each linear extension of P is equally likely to have been the
order in which the elements were generated. They called this property 'general
covariance.'" It also follows from Rideout–Sorkin `Prob_N(C) = W(C) P(C)`.

**Classical remainder is a bridge question.** Covtree 1910.07292v2, Lemma 4.9
discussion: "for every walk on labeled poscau – whether it satisfies Discrete
General Covariance or not – there exists a covtree walk that produces the same
measure on R(S)." So records modelled as covariant (stem) events cannot detect
birth order or failure of discrete general covariance. What is left is not
mathematics: can a physically motivated record correspond to a label- or
stage-dependent event? Bento–Dowker–Zalel 2109.10749v1 keep covariant
convex-events as the observables even for non-DGC alternating models.

**Quantum remainder, in one sentence:** *in quantum sequential growth with
covariance imposed only on amplitudes' norms and path-dependent phases allowed
(Srivastava–Surya 2603.25503v1 introduce and set aside this option), do the
phases change the quantum measure of covariant events?* A second form: for
complex models whose measure does not extend, do covariant predictions depend on
the preferred ordering used for conditional convergence (Dowker–Johnston–Surya
1007.2725v1)?

## Claim-contract draft

- **Question.** In a two-generation quantum sequential growth model with
  norm-only covariance, does a path-dependent phase change the quantum measure of
  any covariant event?
- **Nearest predecessors.** Srivastava–Surya 2603.25503 (introduce the phase
  option, do not pursue it); Dowker–Johnston–Surya 1007.2725 (complex
  percolation, measure extension); Surya–Zalel 2003.11311 (extension criteria).
- **Delta.** Computing what the first predecessor sets aside. Possibly
  immediate if covariant events are insensitive to phases by construction.
- **Smallest example.** Stage-3 growth with two paths to the same unlabelled
  causet and a relative phase φ; compute the decoherence functional on the
  covariant events.
- **Falsifier.** Sorkin–Surya "Constructing covariant observables in complex
  percolation" (cited as in preparation; not found), or a short proof that norm
  covariance fixes the covariant quantum measure.
- **Novelty kind.** At most a small computation in a known framework. No bearing
  on temporal ontology without a bridge premise linking birth order to becoming.
