## Primary contract (defeater first)

### Strongest defeater

**Priority and novelty may be a corollary.** Dowker–Zalel 2212.01149v2 already
pose the exact extension question on doubly-reduced poscau and supply an
improved variation criterion for valency-one nodes; they explicitly note the
valency-one sum "in future it could be computed using techniques similar to
those used to obtain the probability of a post [17]." If their criterion plus
that post-probability technique yields the same lower bound, this is a short
corollary, not a new theorem. The candidate is elementary and the priority
search is incomplete: Semantic Scholar returned HTTP 429, OpenCitations HTTP
400, and 28 citing records for Surya–Zalel were not each screened
(`priority-audit/AGENT_REPORT.md`). No external referee or proof assistant has
checked it. **Second defeater: physical relevance.** Even if the theorem is
correct, it rules out one class of countably additive scalar complex measures on
one labelled algebra with prescribed values. It says nothing about other quantum
measures, decoherence functionals, dynamics, observables, or temporal ontology.

### 1. Exact question and theorem

Let `q = 1-p`, `p ∈ ℂ`. For a naturally labelled finite causet `C`, prescribe

`A_p(C) = p^{L(C)} q^{binom(|C|,2) - R(C)}`,

where `L` counts covers and `R` strict comparable pairs. `C` is principal if it
has a unique maximum; doubly principal if it and its restriction deleting the
last label are principal. Let `R̃_p` be the sigma-algebra generated by cylinders
of doubly-principal naturally labelled causets.

**Claim.** There is a countably additive, finite-valued complex measure on
`R̃_p` agreeing with `A_p(C)` on every doubly-principal cylinder **iff**
`p ∈ [0,1]`.

Scope note: this is the labelled generator algebra, not the covariant
intersection `R_p = R̃_p ∩ R`, and not a claim about Hilbert-valued or
decoherence-functional measures. The larger principal-cylinder algebra is
excluded by restriction.

### 2. Three nearest predecessors at assumption/result level

1. **Dowker–Zalel, arXiv:2212.01149v2, Secs. III.3–III.4 and V, Eqs. (23)–(26),
   (29)–(31), Theorems 14–15.** They define the doubly-reduced poscau, the
   doubly-principal stem sets, and finite-level variation criteria. Their
   application to Complex Transitive Percolation is **inconclusive**: the
   valency-one sum cannot be bounded, and they name its future computation.
   *Delta:* the candidate gives an explicit divergent lower bound on the sum
   over all doubly-principal cylinders at level `k+2`, resolving their named
   obstruction for every complex `p` outside `[0,1]`.
2. **Dowker–Johnston–Surya, arXiv:1007.2725v1, Secs. 2 and 4.** Complex/vector
   measure framework and full-labelled-algebra nonextension; post events need
   additional treatment. *Delta:* the candidate works on a strictly smaller
   labelled generator algebra, and a generic lift from a larger algebra does not
   preserve the missing prescribed cylinder values.
3. **Surya–Zalel, arXiv:2003.11311v1, Theorem 2.1 and the complex-percolation
   application.** Bounded-variation extension criteria on the full generated
   history sigma-algebra. *Delta:* the direct sum over doubly-principal
   cylinders avoids assuming that full-algebra failure descends to a smaller
   subalgebra; this is exactly the gap Dowker–Zalel flag.

Real-percolation post probabilities and the Euler-product tail factor are prior
work (Bombelli–Seggev–Watson 0809.2258v2, Sec. 2, Theorem 2.1); the candidate
uses them after modulus normalization.

### 3. What changes and why it matters

The mathematical delta is an elementary estimate plus the exact
doubly-principal classification, not a new probability method. It closes one
route to countably additive scalar-amplitude cyclic models while leaving other
quantum measures, decoherence functionals and covariant value rules untouched.
It has no physical or ontological consequence without a bridge model. The
number of agents, or use of automation, adds no novelty.

### 4. Smallest reproducible example and independent verification

The finite certificate at `p = (1+i)/2`, `q = (1-i)/2`:

`V_5 = 3/4 + 3√2/16`, `V_6 = 31/32 + 55√2/64`,

and the five-element block sum `W = 9/16 + 105/(128√2) > 71/64 > 1`. The
independent script is `research/creative/proof-audit/verify_small.py`
(exact edge-mask enumeration through `k=4`, rational parameters, symmetric
complex-point sums, finite post-future factors). The analytic route is the
two-cap bound

`V_{k+2} ≥ |p| (|p| + |1-p|)^k ∏_{j≥1}(1 - s^j)`, `s = |1-p|/(|p|+|1-p|)`,

with the Euler product positive for `s ∈ (0,1)`. Finite enumeration
corroborates; it does not prove the all-level quantifier.

### 5. Falsifiers and kill criteria

- A prior proof of the same prescribed-value classification (priority defeat).
- A proof that Dowker–Zalel's improved criterion already implies the bound
  (novelty defeat, derivation survives).
- A mismatch in the generator algebra, cover/gap exponents, the `p=0`/`p=1`
  endpoints, or the disjointness of cylinders (mathematical defeat).
- A finite certificate that contradicts the analytic bound.
- Any claim of physical relevance without an operational model and a measurable
  prediction. The conditional covariant result additionally requires the
  all-principal common factor `(T)` and cyclic normalization; changing either
  changes the model, and the doubly-principal-only weakened rule has a Dirac
  counterexample (`COVARIANT_RESULT.md`).

### 6. Novelty separation

- **Mathematical:** a candidate short extension-boundary result.
- **Formalization:** none; no proof assistant was used.
- **Physical:** a limitation of one scalar-measure framework under named
  assumptions, not a new dynamical theory or experimental prediction.
- **Philosophical:** none; no conclusion about ontology, passage, determinism,
  causal order, clock time or the thermodynamic arrow.

Global priority and external mathematical acceptance remain outstanding.
Selection authorizes reproduction and external review, not a priority claim.

## Fallback contract (defeater first)

### Strongest defeater

**Ludescher et al. 2607.24976 and Hausmann et al. 2308.10967 may already cover
the interacting finite-clock case.** Ludescher generalizes perspective-neutral
QRFs to covariant instruments and obtains exact Schrödinger dynamics in a
center-of-mass clock for interacting subsystems; Hausmann compares twirled and
purified-measurement formulations for finite-resource clocks. Neither was read
at the exact three-picture, non-commuting, finite `Z_N` scope. The novelty
audit also found the proposed class underspecified. The likely outcome is an
application, not a new theorem.

### Contract

- **Question.** In a finite `Z_N` clock + qubit model with
  `H_int = g σ_x ⊗ K`, `[K,H_C] ≠ 0`, a non-ideal covariant clock POVM, one
  constraint surface (pure physical state), and a fixed multi-time readout
  instrument: (a) do the Dirac, Page–Wootters and relational-Heisenberg
  reductions agree, and where do they first differ; (b) under linear
  conditioning, which memory criteria (semigroup, CP-divisibility, backflow,
  process tensor) fail, and do any two come apart?
- **Nearest predecessors.** Höhn–Smith–Lock 1912.00033 (non-interacting
  trinity); Chataignier–Höhn–Lock–Mele 2409.06479 (periodic, non-interacting);
  Smith–Ahmadi 1712.00081 (interacting, PW only, degenerate Example 3);
  Rijavec 2204.11740 (`V = X⊗h`); Ludescher et al. 2607.24976 (covariant
  instruments, to be read first); Chruściński–Kossakowski–Rivas 2011 and
  Milz et al. 2019 (criterion separations).
- **Delta.** Applying known reductions and criteria to an interacting finite
  clock that the predecessors exclude by assumption. At most a worked
  application.
- **Smallest example.** `Z_3` clock, qubit system, `H_int = g σ_x ⊗ K` with `K`
  a clock operator not commuting with `H_C` (e.g. a reading projector); compute
  the reading probabilities and `Φ_τ` exactly.
- **Falsifier.** A published computation of the three reductions or the four
  criteria for an interacting Page–Wootters/Smith–Ahmadi clock; or a proof that
  linear conditioning with any finite covariant clock yields `U ∘ D` even with
  interaction.
- **Novelty kind.** Application/classification of known criteria. Not a new
  criterion, not an ontology result.

## Why each rejected contract lost

All reasons are traceable to the read sources; the full ledger is `DECISION.md`.

- **RC-2** lost as a new-theorem claim: Clifton–Hogarth 1995 p.365 gives the
  uniqueness trichotomy, p.376 the Malament-type no-go, pp.376–380 the
  privileged-worldline countermodels. Only a formalization table remains, and
  formalization prior art was not searched.
- **RC-3** lost as stated: HSL 2007.00580 after Eq. (33) says sector coherence
  is not physically accessible and the external-frame route violates gauge
  invariance; Hosseini–Lock 2604.00092 gives the normalization obstruction and
  sector-wise partial-isometry remedy. Only the lane's unchecked reflection
  conjecture remains.
- **RC-4 classical** is killed by Rideout–Sorkin general covariance and
  Brightwell–Luczak 0901.0240v3 Sec. 1 / Theorem 4.2. The quantum phase and
  preferred-ordering remainders are small computations explicitly set aside by
  Srivastava–Surya 2603.25503 and left open by DJS 1007.2725; archived.
- **RC-5 generic separation** is killed by Chruściński–Kossakowski–Rivas 2011
  and Milz et al. 2019. Nandi–Petruccione 2606.08595 Eq. (10) is a second-order
  TCL kernel statement, not a map family. Only the application to RC-1 remains.
- **Broad A/B/C** are killed as standalone targets by the novelty audit and
  `STATUS_2026-10-19.md`: A's classical growth core is occupied, B is
  substantially occupied by finite-resolution and interaction papers, C's
  simultaneity side is occupied by Malament/Besnard and its RPM side by
  Clifton–Hogarth/Czerniawski/Thyssen.
- **Conditional covariant theorem** lost as target scope because it assumes a
  common tail factor for all principal post pasts plus cyclic normalization;
  it is a conditional follow-up, not an unconditional result.
- **Creative clock certificates, clock memory and covariant stopping** are not
  selected: their audited outputs are finite checks or known instrument/stopping
  results at the read depths, and the complex-percolation candidate is the
  strongest creative output.
