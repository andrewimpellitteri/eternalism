# Priority audit and referee report: the doubly-principal complex-percolation note

Sole-worker report, 2026-10-21. Target: `research/paper-novel/main.tex`
(7-page working manuscript, commit `e616ad7` at review time). This is an
internal referee-style report and a bounded priority audit, not an external
referee decision or a global novelty certificate.

## 1. What the paper claims

For complex transitive percolation with
`A_p(C)=p^{L(C)}(1-p)^{h(C)}` on the sigma-algebra generated by naturally
labelled **doubly-principal** cylinders, a finite countably additive complex
measure matching those values exists iff `p in [0,1]` (Theorem 1). A separate
conditional theorem gives a covariant common-factor classification iff
`0 < p <= 1` under the all-principal rule (T) and normalization on infinitely
many posts (Theorem 2). A Dirac construction shows why (T) cannot be weakened
to doubly-principal pasts alone.

## 2. Priority audit

### The published open case

Dowker–Zalel, arXiv:2212.01149v2, Sec. V (published as *Class. Quantum Grav.*
40 (2023) 155015), define the smaller algebra and state:

> “... their application to Complex Transitive Percolation is inconclusive. On
> the one hand, we cannot prove that an extension exists since ζ is equal to
> |p|+|1-p|-1 on every doubly principal causet ... On the other hand, we can
> [cannot] rule out an extension, since every level n>1 in doubly-reduced
> poscau contains nodes with valency equal to 1 and on these nodes ζ vanishes
> ...”

They then note that the valency-one sum “in future ... could be computed using
techniques similar to those used to obtain the probability of a post [17].”
The paper’s delta is exactly that computation: the two-cap reduction, the
explicit divergent bound, and the resulting classification. The paper honestly
says the individual level sum `V_n` is not literally DZ’s cumulative quantity.

- **Targeted web/arXiv searches** for “doubly principal” + complex
  percolation, “doubly-reduced poscau”, and valency-one complex percolation
  returned DZ, Surya–Zalel 2020, Dowker–Johnston–Surya 2010, and general
  causal-set reviews. Those establish the larger-algebra obstruction and the
  bounded-variation criterion, not the smaller prescribed-value case.

### Verdict

**Plausible narrow priority.** The paper solves the explicitly open
doubly-principal case with the method DZ suggests. It is not a new general
extension criterion, and the argument is elementary enough that a referee may

remain unsearched at full text).

## 3. Referee report

### Recommendation

**Accept as a short note after minor revisions**, subject to an external
referee and a final priority check. The theorem and proof are internally
coherent; the main risks are priority framing and presentation, not a
mathematical gap found in this audit.

### Proof checks performed

- **Two-cap bijection and cover count:** verified. Every doubly-principal
  `(k+2)`-order is `G ⊕ {u} ⊕ {v}`; the first cap covers the maximal elements
  of `G`, the second covers the first cap; no new incomparables.
- **Endpoint product:** verified against the edge-percolation derivation and
  BSW Sec. 2, Eq. (2). The sum is over naturally labelled orders; the closure
  probability is `r^L s^h`; the last-vertex event gives
  `F_k(s)=prod(1-s^j)`.
- **Variation bound:** verified algebraically. `V_{k+2}=a sum_G a^w b^h`, and
  `w+h >= k` gives the lower bound `a t^k F_k(s)`. Divergence for `p notin
  [0,1]` follows.
- **Converse at endpoints:** the Dirac antichain at `p=0` and Dirac chain at
  `p=1` have the prescribed cylinder values under the zero-power convention.
- **Covariant theorem:** the laminar-cover argument, `kappa != 0` step, rank
  variation and endpoint exclusions are coherent. The `p=0` exclusion is
  correct because the ranks `|H|>=2` cover `P_infinity` when there are
  infinitely many posts.
- **Dirac counterexample:** correct; deleting the post from its inclusive past
  leaves a two-element antichain, so no doubly-principal `D_H` is hit.

### Major comments

1. **Quote DZ exactly.** The paper paraphrases the closing suggestion as a
   “cumulative doubly-principal amplitude sum”; DZ’s sentence is about the
   valency-one sum `S_{n-r}^{v=1}` and says it “could be computed using
   techniques similar to those used to obtain the probability of a post.”
   Quote the sentence or paraphrase without “cumulative” unless that word is
   in the source.
2. **Keep the thesis clearly non-load-bearing.** The current note is good;
   consider moving the inherited-read caveat to a footnote so the main
   priority sentence rests unambiguously on DZ.
3. **State the priority claim as “solves an explicitly open case,” not “new
   theorem.”** The abstract already does this; keep it that way in any cover
   letter.

### Minor comments

- “Finite countably additive complex measure” is redundant: a complex measure
  is finite by definition. “Countably additive complex measure” is enough.
- In the appendix, the overfull hbox from the long `reproduce.py` path is
  cosmetic; use `\path` or a break if desired.
- The COVARIANT theorem’s `nu(Omega)=nu(P_infinity)=1` is strong; say
  explicitly that `P_infinity` has full measure, not merely that the two
  numbers are equal.
- The paper should explicitly say that the published DZ version was not
  page-collated; the arXiv v2 locators are what were checked.
- The abstract’s “completes a calculation suggested in the direct
  predecessor” is fair; “direct predecessor” should name DZ in the abstract or
  first sentence.

### Journal fit

A short mathematical-physics note is the right format. *Classical and Quantum
Gravity* is the natural venue given DZ and the causal-set literature; a
philosophy-of-physics venue would require more interpretive framing than the
note provides. Do not submit before an external referee and a final thesis /
unindexed-source priority check.
