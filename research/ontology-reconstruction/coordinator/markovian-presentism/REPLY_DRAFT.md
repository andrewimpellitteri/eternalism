## Working abstract

Builes and Impagnatiello argue that physics favours presentism. Their reason: the laws
are Markovian, and certain non-Humean presentisms entail that they must be. We make
two points.
- **The derivation.** On the semantics of Nomic Presentism's own source (Markosian
  2013), it proves a restated screening-off principle that holds for every law. That
  includes the non-Markovian toy law the authors themselves give. To reach the laws, a
  further premise is needed, that governing laws take only present input. With it, the
  two presentist strategies collapse into one.
- **The datum.** Markovianity is a datum only relative to a choice of state. It holds
  for the global wavefunction. It is contested for primitive-ontology states, where
  GRW flash dynamics depends on the whole flash history. It conflicts with Bell for
  local event states, by an adaptation of Horvat (2026).

So the confirmation the argument claims is available only to presentists who already
hold wavefunction realism, a global foliation and governance from the present.

## Outline

1. **The argument.** Reconstruct (∗) and the two strategies (B&I §§4–5). Credit
   Lettie's prior-based reply, and set it aside. We grant (∗)'s left-hand side
   throughout.
2. **What Nomic Presentism proves.**
   - MD_pres follows from the p. 14 schema for any law.
   - Run the …0011… law through Markosian's route semantics. The law is true, the
     history atoms are false, MD_pres holds, and the law is non-Markovian on its state.
   - So the step from MD_pres to "the laws are Markovian" needs governance from the
     present.
   - That premise is §4.1's Aristotelian premise. It is false for constraint-type
     non-Humeanism (Chen–Goldstein, Adlam), which B&I note in their footnote 28.
   - Consequence: "any version of Non-Humeanism" (p. 13) must be withdrawn.
3. **Markovian in which state?**
   - Splicing (O1) and past-induced or predictive states (O3) make the property
     relative to the state.
   - Their footnote-10 pair …0011… and …000111… does not separate cleanly, because a
     phase state makes the second Markovian.
   - So the datum needs an independently fixed state. Three candidates:
     - **(a) Global ψ.** The datum holds.
     - **(b) Primitive ontology.** GRWf flashes with nomological ψ
       (Goldstein–Tumulka–Zanghì; Esfeld–Gisin) are history-dependent.
     - **(c) Local events.** Adapting Horvat's Proposition 3, local-beable Markovian
       Chance plus probabilistic locality gives Bell local causality, which experiment
       violates.
3b. **The foliation surplus (RESULT §5b).** This may be the strongest point.
   - Their relativistic datum is Markovianity relative to *every* Cauchy foliation.
     Presentism with a privileged foliation explains it only for that one foliation.
   - A two-site toy model shows Markovianity on one foliation does not give it on all.
   - Relativistic locality does give it on all, through the time-slice property.
   - So the surplus is evidence for locality, which their eternalist local-powers option
     has and NHP lacks.
   - The non-local Markovian examples they give (Newton, Bohm) fail their own
     relativistic datum.
   - Jäger's Boundary Markov extends screening-off to loop spacetimes, where presentism
     cannot be stated.
   - This point is independent of §§2–3. It survives even if governance from the
     present is granted and ψ is intrinsic.
4. **Consequences for (∗), footnote 20 and footnote 24.**
   - The confirmation goes only to the conjunction of NHP with wavefunction realism, a
     global foliation and governance from the present.
   - Footnote 20's falsifiability: on reading (b) the "discovery" may already be made.
     On reading (a) it is shielded by the choice of state.
   - Footnote 24's local presentisms: they meet reading (c).
5. **What survives.** A narrower, still interesting argument: global-ψ Markovianity
   bears on presentists who already accept ψ-realism and a preferred foliation.
   Whether eternalists who accept the same two premises have a matching explanation
   is left open.

## Angles considered and parked (7 November)

- **Time symmetry.** The Markov property is symmetric: given the present, past and
  future are independent. The Humean eternalist can read it as a conditional-independence
  structure of the 4D mosaic, the temporal analogue of spatial locality. This is
  absorbed into §3b, which makes the same point with more force.
- **Empirically equivalent non-Markovian formulations.** All-at-once and retrocausal
  models (Wharton–Argaman 2020; Argaman, arXiv:2609.00848) are locally mediated and
  depend on future inputs. They are a fourth choice of state for §3. It could be added
  if space allows.
- **The Past Hypothesis as a law about one time.** Under Nomic Presentism with
  indeterministic laws, a Past-Hypothesis law could supply truths about the past that
  the present does not necessitate. That is interesting for Builes's (2026) open-past
  worry, but tangential to (∗). Not pursued.
- **Macro-level chances are non-Markovian** (spin-echo, records). B&I restrict their
  claim to fundamental laws, so this is not an objection.

## The exact question for an outside specialist

Does Builes and Impagnatiello's §4.2 derivation establish that the *laws* are
Markovian? Or does it establish only the presentist restatement, so that it needs a
governance-from-the-present premise? And is their Markovian datum independent of
whether ψ counts as part of the present intrinsic state?
