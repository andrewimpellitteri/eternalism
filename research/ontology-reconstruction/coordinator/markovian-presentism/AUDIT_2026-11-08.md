# Adversarial audit, 8 November 2026

Status: coordinator assessment, not independent review or acceptance. This audit qualifies RESULT.md and REPLY_DRAFT.md; their stronger conclusions must not be used without resolving the issues below. No novelty established.

## Verdict

There is a worthwhile question about whether the empirical datum and the metaphysical conclusion use the same notion of state and history. The existing reply does not yet establish its advertised correction to the authors. Its abstract is too strong.

## Required repairs and strongest replies

1. **Address Markovian*, not only strict Markovianity.** The authors explicitly allow interpretive qualifications and discuss intrinsic versus extrinsic velocity (2022 preprint pp. 4–6). Their strongest reply is that a compatible intrinsic-state interpretation suffices. Showing that one primitive-ontology description is non-Markovian does not refute that weaker claim. The research question is which state enrichments their compatibility qualification permits, and whether those restrictions have independent justification.

2. **The semantic countermodel needs a shared truth target.** A mathematical route with a detailed past is not automatically a true past admitted by Nomic Presentism. If MD_pres ranges over true tensed claims, a route construction that declares those claims false may change the subject. To establish equivocation, specify a common observational language, translate both theories into it, and exhibit a concrete discrepancy. Conversely, the authors owe a link from the thin tensed history to the historical evidence used in physics. Governance from the present is a possible repair, not proven to be the unique necessary repair. The claim that both strategies collapse into one is premature.

3. **Foliation non-entailment is not a likelihood inequality.** A countermodel to H entails D does not establish Cr(D|H)<1 without a credence model assigning positive weight to countermodels. It certainly does not establish comparative confirmation against a rival. Compare fully specified packages and their background conditions. A presentist may adopt relativistic dynamics as shared background. Then the proposed surplus need not discriminate between ontologies.

4. **Locality is not yet the asserted screening theorem.** Distinguish causal propagation, microcausality, the time-slice axiom, Bell local causality, and conditional independence. The time-slice axiom concerns generation/determination of observables from a neighbourhood of a Cauchy surface; a classical conditional-chance equation does not follow just by renaming it Markovianity. The Chilian–Fredenhagen abstract establishes a result for scalar fields in formal renormalized perturbation theory, not the universal inference in RESULT §5b. Delete the unconditional locality likelihood of one pending an exact theorem.

5. **The swap model is bounded evidence only.** With free initial bits a,b, the histories alternate (a,b),(b,a). The tilted slice records a duplicated bit and adjacent tilted slices recover the other. This establishes loss of sufficiency under that sampling. A full relativistic claim additionally needs a specified causal spacetime, complete slice state and admissible foliations. It establishes no failure for an empirically adequate Bohmian theory. The draft's claims about such theories exceed its own abstract-depth source record.

6. **Bell excludes a conjunction.** A result excluding local-beable Markovian chance AND probabilistic locality does not exclude Markovian chance alone, or local presentism alone. Specify setting independence and the exact conditioning sigma-algebras. The existing Horvat transfer remains unverified in this audit.

7. **History dependence is not a proof of non-Markovianity.** Being computable from a whole history does not exclude a sufficient current statistic. For GRWf give two admissible histories agreeing on the proposed present state with different conditional future laws, fixing the same law and initial-condition policy. Nomological psi may affect what is held fixed. Wavefunction realism is also stronger than the bare existence of an adequate current state; the claim that confirmation requires it is not established.

8. **Bayesian factorization is not preservation of net support.** If E entails M, the likelihood ratio for E factors into the ratio for M and a residual ratio. The latter may cancel or reverse the former. RESULT's revision correctly abandons an automatic defeat from backward determinism, but should not imply guaranteed net confirmation from the factorization.

## A defensible replacement thesis

The proposed empirical support requires a bridge between the state's physical predictive role and its admissibility as intrinsic present reality. Whether that bridge is independently motivated remains to be shown. This is a burden-of-argument diagnosis, not a refutation of presentism or a demonstrated new theorem.

## Candidate contribution: a constrained state-realization problem

Ask whether a specified physical process admits sufficient states on every admissible Cauchy surface that simultaneously (i) depend only on intrinsic surface data, (ii) transform covariantly, (iii) have compatible evolution under changes of surface, and (iv) preserve a fixed observable probability law. Define admissible enrichments before searching for a counterexample. Arbitrary encodings of the whole past must not silently qualify as intrinsic states.

Potential deliverable: a necessary-and-sufficient realization criterion or a counterexample separating a preferred-foliation realization from an all-surface realization under these constraints. This could sharpen the reply, but may already follow from state-realization theory, covariant dynamics or stochastic causal-model results. It is a candidate, not a novelty claim. Compare at least behavioural state maps (Rapisarda–Willems), covariant hypersurface evolution/time-slice results, and spacetime probabilistic screening (including the recorded Jäger and Horvat leads) before implementation. Kill the proposal if it is just ordinary Markovization, built-in locality, or an existing integrability condition in new vocabulary.

The philosophical consequence would remain conditional: failure of one independently motivated intrinsic-state requirement challenges packages adopting it; it does not settle which times exist.
