## 1. First philosophical priority: identify the modal category

Task: `ontology-modal-category-bridge`.

Reconstruct the exact comparison in C&R chapter 7: what is held fixed by
ω_t^ex, what the explicitly nomological □ ranges over, and what it means
for later existence to fail. Then compare three categories with the same
local field equations: arbitrary smooth developments, maximal developments,
and the selected two-sided complete scattering class. Keep an ontological
stage different from a complete possible-world history.

Deliver one explicit translation with its extra assumptions, or a precise
obstruction. In particular, determine whether a same-past causal model
actually fixes ω_t^ex, and whether changing later existence requires a
nonmaximal restriction, a physical boundary rule, or a different ontological
interpretation of one physical history. Do not impose the chapter-9 bow-tie
realm on the classical chapter-7 world-state definition without a bridge.

Success would identify a defensible extra premise and its strongest rival.
Failure would locate exactly why the physics does not instantiate that
existential-openness claim. Either is more useful than repeating that
bivalence does not entail eternalism. Primary source reading precedes
any new formalization or novelty assertion.

## 2. Positive ontology priority: one genuine constitutive comparison

Task: `ontology-constitutive-extent-test`.

Choose one bounded physical system, beginning with an explicitly specified
interacting Newtonian bound system if adequate. State a candidate criterion
of unity before choosing its temporal extent. Compare two regions that
are predictively sufficient but differ in the proposed physical
constitution of that unity. The relevant contrast is information versus
constitutive activity, not a preference for the word “present”.

Deliver the criterion, exact dynamics/model category, candidate extents,
necessity/sufficiency argument or counterexample, and a charitable rival
interpretation preserving the same physical success. Multiple constitutive
grounds are allowed unless the proposal claims uniqueness. Keep the
universe-stage extrapolation separate; no global foliation requirement is
to be smuggled into the subsystem test.

This tests the two bridges in CONSTITUTION_IS_NOT_PREDICTION.md:
determination→unity and unity→concrete present. A successful result must
say which bridge it actually supports. It need not select a complete
temporal ontology to make philosophical progress.

## 3. Stronger physical control: fix incoming prefix masses too

Task: `lawful-prefix-energy-control` (after integrated finite-control review).

The current energy compensator comes after every readout. The total E is
fixed, while m_-(V)=¼∫_{−∞}^VΦ² can vary before that tail. A sharper target
also fixes

    E_j=m_-(v(γ(T_j)))

for each finite reading time, where the incoming radial null hypersurface
through γ(T_j) geometrically specifies the boundary cut. These are incoming
cumulative Hawking-mass quantities, not local detector energies or ADM
mass. A gauge/invariance check of the definition is part of the task.

Possible route, not a completed proof: place a disjoint compensator before
each sample window, with its leading scalar value zero at all T_j, and
retain independent control bumps with F_k(T_j)=δ_kj. The leading cumulative
profile norms give a triangular system in the compensator squares. Choose
positive baseline amplitudes and strictly increasing target prefix budgets
so a compact parameter chart stays away from zero. The center clock shifts
the incoming cutoff by O(ε²); since Φ²=O(ε²), this contributes O(ε⁴) to
the cumulative norm on the fixed window. A joint degree argument for
readouts/ε and prefix masses/ε² may therefore work on the common base
interval. A final late compensator can still fix the total mass.

Every step needs proof: profile support/positivity, triangular invertibility,
parameter-uniform clock and integral errors, finite-cone continuity, a
degree boundary, and the varying-parameter limit recovering the actual
incoming boundary quantities. Avoid assuming a differentiable scattering
map. A finite grid cannot verify these claims. A negative result naming
the first failed obligation is a valid handback.

This would strengthen a physical comparison, not eliminate the spacelike
exterior data or establish metaphysical indeterminism. No need to postpone
the philosophical tasks until it succeeds.
