   measure, C1/C3/C4 hold in the displayed context and the reverse C4* fails.
   The native audit independently checks every stated marginal and conditional.
   Its strongest criticism is adopted: the directed equation and shared
   exogenous measure put direction and invariance into the comparison. A
   complete-history reading alone does not supply their explanation.
3. **A distinct modal premise.** An inventory may grow and persist in every
   compared history while root probabilities differ between law settings.
   Thus that inventory pattern alone does not entail the cross-law invariance.
   This is a bounded distinction between two quantifier domains, not an example
   satisfying all of Tooley's causal-law requirements and violating one.
4. **A stronger live reply.** The growing account can propose actuality as the
   ground of the privileged cross-law comparison. The rival can propose real
   directed governing dependence. The former must establish the need for its
   actuality ground; the latter must defend direction and mechanism boundaries.
   Neither burden has been discharged by the finite example.
