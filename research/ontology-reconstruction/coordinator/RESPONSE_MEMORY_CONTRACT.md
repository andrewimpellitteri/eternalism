# A constructive path: response duration without an assumed present

30 October 2026. Claim contract and research gate, not a novelty verdict.
The immediate local work is an analytic retarded-response benchmark. An
independent worker checks the nearest prior results in parallel; it is not
asked to invent a temporal ontology or duplicate the calculation.

## Exact target

Fix an inertial source and detector in 3+1 Minkowski spacetime, separation
r>0, a classical retarded Klein–Gordon field, and the same prescribed
source amplitude f(t). The detector response is R_m f=h_m*f. Changing m
compares different operators, not same-law alternative completions.

Use the known kernel

    h_m(s) = δ(s−r)/(4πr)
             −1_{s≥r} m J_1(m√(s²−r²))/(4π√(s²−r²)).

Proposed bounded deduction: for every m>0 the exact response has no finite
past cutoff, although

    ||R_m−R_0||_{L∞→L∞} ≤ C_* m/(4π),
    C_* = ∫_0^∞ |J_1(z)|/z dz < ∞,

and for fixed r the ratio of that norm to m tends to C_*/(4π). For a
fixed finite time-lag window the error is instead O(m²). Test the exact
constants, input class, retarded/no-free-field assumption, point-detector
idealization, and spatially smeared extension before promoting the claim.

The point is the separation of exact support from a stable response norm.
It is not a proof that response duration constitutes existence, and it is
not the coupled Einstein–scalar fixed-energy result. Its norm uses bounded
externally prescribed sources; no fixed energy, source-duration or bandwidth
constraint is presumed. Compact smooth source histories may approximate a
supremum with no common duration bound.

   and §§8.1–8.2 of the author-hosted published PDF. Fading memory and its
   independence of a state-space realization are already established
   concepts. Theorem5 uses bounded convolution measures. **Do not claim
   input/output invariance or fading memory as new.**
2. Salnikov–Satunin–Kirpichnikov–Fitkevich2021,
   DOI10.1007/JHEP03(2021)143: directly read AppendixA, printedp15,
   especially(A.1). The retarded kernel and mass-dependent tail are
   standard. Their harmonic-source calculation is not our worst-case
   bounded-history norm. A direct corollary may nevertheless defeat
   mathematical novelty.
3. Dong arXiv:1905.08620v1: selected introduction/Theorem1.1 and §3

   research subject, with different Cauchy/source norms and nonlinear
   hypotheses. No priority from failing to find our exact norm in the
   passages read. NIST DLMF10.7.3 and10.17.3 provide the Bessel inputs.

Brading's law-constitutive present and Savitt's distinction between a
localized present and presentism are philosophical predecessors already
recorded in the project. A response window is not an ontology by relabeling.
The constitutive worker's instant-reconstruction argument must also be
repaired: reconstruction does not establish grounding or constitution.

## What could be distinctive, and what would kill it

The potential contribution is a precise robustness test for a proposed
temporal boundary: a sharp support-based extent may change discontinuously
while every bounded-source response stays uniformly close. Its use in an
independently specified sustaining system would have to be demonstrated.
The elementary flat-space estimate may be familiar or an immediate
corollary. No new theorem or physical discovery is claimed on that basis.

Kill or narrow if the kernel normalization is wrong; the claimed norm
requires forbidden sources; hidden homogeneous fields spoil uniqueness;
smearing removes the claimed contrast; the exact result is already printed;
or no constitutive criterion independently connects the response to unity.
Do not infer ontological equivalence from equal response operators.

## Two ambitious continuations with concrete obligations

**Constitutive transport.** Use a finite transport channel with actual
material carriers, finite transit time and an outflow boundary. Establish
an exact response window independent of coordinates and state realization.
Then identify a physical organizing activity, rather than declaring every
response-sufficient region constitutive. Challenge it with the same
physical channel under an unrestricted event ontology. A successful
local organization need not yield an exhaustive inventory restriction.

**Robust temporal organization.** For a fixed physical input/output
protocol and tolerance δ, derive the worst-case error of removing older
inputs; compare it across small physical perturbations and changes of
internal representation. Determine which extent properties are invariant,
which depend on resolution, and whether a multiscale process ontology
explains this dependence better than its strongest rival. A nonzero
tolerance is part of the protocol, not a fundamental constant to invent.

Curved-spacetime tails and matter-coupled feedback are later targets,
not consequences of the flat linear calculation. The immediate success
condition is one source-checked support/norm theorem with an honest
prior-result verdict and a separate philosophical scope argument.
