## Finding

The attempted explanatory argument for a growing inventory fails to show
that advantage. It wrongly requires event realism to identify processes with
rigid aggregates of all their actual events. A physically organized execution
with an explicit counterpart criterion supplies a stronger rival account.
The one revision properly demands organization beyond a list of parts; the
feedback mechanism provides such a bounded account without deciding the
temporal inventory.

The feedback/replay contrast is material to this test: identical actual
liquid-height and inlet-flow traces can conceal different responses to a
changed supply rate. Both inventory interpretations can retain the organizing
mechanism and that response. This is an explanatory replacement for the
specified control phenomenon, not a proof of complete metaphysical parity.
The physical unity criterion remains a candidate and is not derived merely
from predictive sufficiency.

## Prior work and ancestry

The conceptual route is already in the literature: Stout's own interruption
discussion leaves structured stages open, and Steward provides a modally
robust process account retaining temporal parts. Steward §3 also directly
precedes the project's happening/presence criticism. We therefore do not
present that distinction or this control example as a philosophical discovery.
The priority of any particular formal encoding has not been investigated.

The earlier occurrence/exhaustiveness family stays stopped. This test asks
whether a specified physical organizing explanation favors one inventory;
it does not try to infer an exhaustive concrete domain from constitution.
The exact restriction matters: agreement here does not establish that all
ontological disagreements are verbal or that every explanation is replaceable.

## What remains substantive

The first unresolved step is why the material organizing relation constitutes
one genuine process, rather than merely a useful operational grouping. If
that needs a further form/grounding fact, its rationale and its relation to
the physical mechanism must be stated. Its requiring a particular temporal
inventory would be another claim. Neither extra claim follows from the
calculation. This identifies a changed question with an actual predecessor,
not another positive recovery of the old inventory inference.
