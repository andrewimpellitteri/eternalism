# The constitutive submission does not establish its reconstruction dilemma

30 October 2026. Coordinator inspection of clean isolated GLM commit
`9842259`, task `ontology-constitutive-extent-test`, based on `1d1102a`.
The original submission and reports remain unchanged in its worktree and
branch. Status: **in review; central conclusion not accepted**. This is a
bounded logic/model review, not validation of its cited source readings.

## 1. The invalid step is determination to qualification

RESULT.md §4 argues: when a constitutive criterion's truth is fixed by
the instantaneous state and law, the instantaneous extent satisfies it
whenever the process interval does. That does not follow. The criterion
can retain an explicit physical-region argument even though its value
is calculable from other data.

For a deterministic harmonic oscillator with x(t)=cos t, p(t)=−sin t,
consider the physical activity functional

    W(I)=∫_I |x(t)p(t)|dt,
    C(I) iff W(I)>0.

The state at zero and the law determine W(I) for every specified I.
Nevertheless W([0,2π])=2 whereas W({0})=0. Therefore the interval and
instant receive different C verdicts, despite every verdict being
state-and-law reconstructible. Dropping the region argument is precisely
the error. This is a counterexample to the inference, not a proposal
that gross work is the correct criterion of physical unity.

The worker moves from “data at b determine C(I)” to “{b} qualifies as I”.
That confuses the data sufficient to evaluate a property with the region
having that property or realizing its physical activity. No premise that
constitution is always a primitive nonphysical predicate is forced.
Conversely, a positive value of W does not establish constitutive grounding
either. The state-grounding and process-grounding rivals each need their
own argument. The worker's exhaustive two-horn dilemma is withdrawn at
this point, not replaced with an automatic victory for the process rival.

## 2. A useful positive repair: compare the right invariants

[RESPONSE_MEMORY_BENCHMARK.md](RESPONSE_MEMORY_BENCHMARK.md) gives a
concrete alternative. A distributed transport channel has a filled
input/output response window L/c, while its instantaneous field state
encodes that input history. Both facts can be true. Internal state
reconstruction does not erase response duration, and response duration
does not itself constitute an ontology.

Thus a productive test first fixes physical inputs, outputs, carriers
and clock; identifies an invariant organizing activity; and then asks
what makes that activity constitutive. It must not infer either
constitution or the absence of a physical distinction from predictive
reconstruction alone. Fading memory and realization independence have
established prior literature, identified separately in the new contract.

## 3. The proposed examples need narrower claims

* **Work is not a general unity test.** In an exact circular Kepler pair,
  the internal forces are perpendicular to each body's velocity. The
  force does no work, yet changing momentum direction is essential to
  the bound orbit. U3's positive gross-work requirement therefore excludes
  this ordinary dynamically maintained pair. It can be a test for a
  chosen activity, but it cannot silently represent all sustaining unity.
* **The undamped Floquet criterion is misstated.** For the displayed
  second-order periodic equation without a first-derivative term,
  the first-order system has trace zero. Its monodromy determinant is
  one, so both multipliers cannot lie strictly inside the unit circle.
  The elliptic |Tr M|<2 case has multipliers on the unit circle. This
  follows analytically and does not validate the worker's numerical values.
  The familiar high-frequency averaged Kapitza inequality must also not
  be called exactly equivalent to finite-frequency Floquet stability
  without a source/theorem supporting that extra claim.
* **Delay is relative to the state description.** A pair (x(t),x'(t))
  alone may be insufficient for a delay equation, while the present
  history-function state is sufficient in an enlarged, infinite-dimensional
  realization. This does not show that its physical mediators have no
  instantaneous state. A computed characteristic root also cannot alone
  certify the absence of all roots in the unstable half-plane.
* **Anchoring does not force straddling.** A past activity window [b−T,b]
  need not straddle the standpoint. The driven pendulum as written is
  explicitly time dependent; it is not homogeneous under arbitrary time
  translations with drive phase held fixed. Adding a drive-phase state
  is possible, but is a separate change of description.

These issues do not show that all subsystem criteria fail. They show that
this submission has not established its stated impossibility or unique
missing process premise. The broad claim that dynamics cannot test any
physical distinction between process and instantaneous data is not accepted.
