## Strong replies and remaining limits

The growing-block theorist can grant that laws fix events absent from a given
standpoint's real fragment and maintain that existence is added as passage
continues. This distinguishes law-relative fixity from ontic addition, but the
formal results do not establish that temporal asymmetry or decide whether
ontic addition suffices for openness. The eternalist can
grant all three horizons predicates as relational or nomological facts while
holding that every event exists. Neither reply is refuted by the mathematical
results.

The clock-equipped rival shows that the Stein–GR no-shared-present result is
conditional on metric-only off-shell universality and its reflection clause.
It does not show that a preferred clock is physically justified, nor that all
clock categories work. Claims about “locality” must name the category,
morphisms, and whether the condition is preservation alone or preservation
and reflection.

The source depth here is intentionally bounded. Primary-source verification
reported in the inherited Stein audit was not repeated; no broad literature
search was undertaken. The mathematical arguments and their correspondence to
external papers were not audited. The claim map is suitable to guide a
replacement discussion, not to certify truth, novelty, priority, physical
adequacy, or a resolution of the ontology dispute.
