## Outcome

RESULT.md supplies a complete analytical classification for all smooth real
conformally coupled scalar solutions with any positive ordinary physical mass
on the fixed closed dust background. Before maximum expansion, only J^−(b)
is fixed. At maximum expansion and later, all M is fixed. Thus all extra
massless fixing is lost for standpoints strictly before maximum expansion;
the exact-threshold dense-trace effect and post-threshold determination remain.

The proof is independent of the massless three-diamond theorem; that theorem
is needed only to compare m=0 with positive m. It does not assume small mass,
generic modes, absence of special Floquet parameters or positivity of the
global kernel. It rules out an open gap in JOINT Cauchy-kernel support using
a normally convergent characteristic Volterra construction, exact spherical
periodization, source-time Cauchy uniqueness, connected-cell analyticity and
two nonzero local limits. CLAIM_CONTRACT.md states predecessor boundaries
and defeat conditions; REVIEW_STATUS.md states the narrow acceptance scope.
