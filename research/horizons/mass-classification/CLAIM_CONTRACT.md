# Claim contract: positive-mass fixing on closed dust

27 October 2026. Research candidate, not a manuscript amendment or novelty
verdict. User asks whether all positive masses destroy all extra future fixing.

## Exact question

Keep the closed dust metric, a_m>0, conformal strip (0,2π)×S³, and the class
of ALL smooth real conformally coupled scalar solutions with ordinary mass
m>0. For b=(τ,Y), ask whether

    F_m(b)=J^−(b) for 0<τ<π,
    F_m(b)=M for π≤τ<2π.

This would remove all massless extra fixing before maximum expansion, including
spacelike and timelike-future parts, without a small-mass restriction. It says
nothing about amplitudes, actual measurements, physical backreaction or ontology.

## Candidate mechanism and independent checks

Reduce exact fixing to joint support of both position and velocity Cauchy
kernels. Construct their radial kernel via a one-dimensional retarded Riemann
function and periodization. Prove joint analyticity on the direct and double
image cells by normally convergent characteristic Volterra iteration. Joint
vanishing of both Cauchy kernels on an open patch would give an open spacetime
zero and force the analytic cell to vanish. A nonzero near-diagonal tail defeats
this in the direct cell; a strictly positive antipodal-corner tail defeats it
in the double cell. The latter depends on a second front jet, separately
derived by a native reviewer.

Independent sidecars address (i) front jets/signs/corner limit and (ii) the
support and geometry reduction. The coordinator must fill, not assume, their
remaining analyticity and double-cell dependencies. Bounded checks should
include constant-potential exact series, a zero-potential negative control,
and periodized radial/spectral normalization. Finite modes cannot establish
exact past agreement or prove the all-target quantifier.

## Defeat conditions

- A missing image, wrong derivative in the initial time or wrong radial
  intertwining normalization invalidates the proposed support argument.
- Local analyticity at one point is insufficient: joint analyticity and
  connectedness of each relevant cell must be established.
- Non-Huygens behavior, a nonzero tail at one event, nonnegative spectrum,
  sampled masses or agent agreement do not prove absence of open support gaps.
- Special Floquet or Whittaker–Hill solutions do not by themselves prove
  exact propagator revival. Conversely a genuine open joint support gap at a
  positive mass would defeat the claim. The proposed proof avoids assuming
  that resonant cases are generic or absent.
- An existing original theorem with the same hypotheses and conclusion defeats
  a novelty claim, even if the local proof here is correct. No such priority
  conclusion has been reached in this bounded search.

Mathematical classification, physical interpretation, philosophical relevance
and originality remain separate questions. The paper is held unchanged.
