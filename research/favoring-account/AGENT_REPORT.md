# Agent report: favoring-account

Sole-worker analysis, 2026-10-21. File: `RESULT.md`, this report.

## Result

A positive account of defeasible favoring is written. An ontology is treated as
a composite hypothesis with a realization class, a prior over realizations, and
a marginal likelihood. The reviewer's O1/O2 example (BF=10 while O2 retains a
compatible realization) is used to show that compatibility survives while
evidential parity does not. The account states what must be justified: the
comparison class, the admissible realizations, the prior, the likelihood and
the defeat conditions. It applies to Brading (realization-class/prior
dependence; system-relative vs privileged-actuality) and LAGEOS (parameter
constraint without an ontology-to-model link).

## Verdict

The paper can legitimately reach "physics defeasibly favors package A over B
under realization class/prior X" without excluding B. That is the positive
verdict form the external review asked for. It is conditional on X; without a
defended X it is stipulation, not evidence.

## Limits

- The account does not settle which prior is correct.
- It does not make the comparison quantitative where the likelihoods are
  qualitative; the Bayes-factor form is a discipline, not a claim of numbers.
- It does not supply the exclusivity premise the discriminator audit found
  missing; it explains how favoring can proceed without it, conditionally.
- No new empirical prediction, priority or ontology claim.
