# Primary-source checks and mathematical counterexample

Access date for web sources: 2026-10-21. No long quotations are reproduced.

## Direct predecessor: doubly-principal algebra

**Fay Dowker and Stav Zalel**, “Observables for cyclic causal set cosmologies,”
arXiv:2212.01149v2 (last revised 2023-07-17),
[primary arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.01149v2).

- **Read depth:** targeted full-text reads of §§III.2, III.4, V; not a whole
  paper proof audit.
- **§III.2:** principal means a naturally labelled causet with a unique
  maximal element.
- **§III.4, discussion after Eq. (26):** the paper states the extension
  criterion on its labelled tree and classifies the full labelled algebra for
  complex transitive percolation. This larger-algebra classification cannot by
  itself settle the smaller doubly-principal algebra.
- **§V, Eqs. (29)–(31) and following discussion:** defines the doubly-reduced
  tree/generator sigma-algebra as the subalgebra generated by principal
  cylinders whose restriction after deleting the last label is principal.
  The paper says its earlier minimum-`zeta` test is inconclusive there because
  valency-one nodes make the corresponding minimum vanish, then gives a
  revised finite-level absolute-sum criterion. The outstanding `v=1`
  contribution is the sum over doubly-principal causets; its evaluation is
  deferred. This is the direct unresolved term that the current lower-bound
  argument addresses.
- **Important distinction:** the paper's covariant algebra `R_p` is the
  intersection of this labelled algebra with the covariant algebra. It is not
  interchangeable with the labelled doubly-principal generator algebra for
  the present extension theorem.

The separate HTML rendering metadata date is not the arXiv version date; use
v2 / 2023-07-17 when identifying the read source.

## Thesis confirmation

**Stav Zalel**, *Discrete random spacetimes: covariance and quantization in
growth dynamics for causal sets* (PhD thesis, Imperial College London, 2021),
[official Imperial repository record](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/entities/publication/dfb9da9a-7d49-4b1a-9f34-7f19b5c9afcf),
[official repository PDF](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/bitstreams/bdaf1f36-5de8-42cd-b155-95721435d74c/download).

- **Read depth:** Ch. 6 §6.2.3, printed pp. 89–90; not full thesis.
- **Finding:** the thesis identifies the super-reduced poscau extension test
  as inconclusive because of valency-one nodes, introduces the amended
  criterion, and leaves the valency-one absolute-amplitude computation for
  future work. This is the same open remainder as the 2023 paper's
  doubly-reduced tree.
- **Scope:** this supports the claim that the case was explicitly recognized
  as unresolved in those sources. It does not establish global publication
  priority for a subsequent solution.

## Vector-measure standard construction

**Joseph Diestel and John Jerry Uhl, Jr.**, *Vector Measures*, Mathematical
Surveys 15 (Providence, RI: American Mathematical Society, 1977),
[official AMS record/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/surv/015). The full-text
route I inspected is a [public scan mirror](https://djvu.online/file/kFpFTA4J8a9KZ),
not the official publisher-hosted text.

- **Read depth:** targeted full text, book pp. 2–3 and 7–8; the exact
  application to causal-set cylinders is my calculation, not a theorem claimed
  by Diestel–Uhl.
- **pp. 2–3:** defines norm-countable additivity, variation and semivariation;
  Examples 6–7 give the indicator map into `L∞` as bounded-semivariation but
  infinite-variation. This establishes the standard distinction between the
  two finiteness notions.
- **pp. 7–8, Example 16:** for finite `1<p<∞`, the indicator map
  `E ↦ 1_E` into `L^p[0,1]` is countably additive in norm by the tail-set
  estimate and has infinite variation on every positive-measure set. Taking
  `p=2` supplies the closest primary-text analogue to the Hilbert construction
  here.

### Direct CTP application

Let `(Ω,F,P_r)` be the ordinary real CTP probability measure on the full
labelled history space, and take `H=L²(P_r)`. Define `μ(E)=1_E`. For disjoint
`E_j`,

`||1_(union_{j>N} E_j)||²_2 = P_r(union_{j>N} E_j) → 0`,

so `μ` is countably additive in the Hilbert norm. For any CTP cylinder `C`,

`||μ(C)||_2 = sqrt(P_r(C))`.

If `|p|²+|1-p|²=1` and `r=|p|²`, then for each finite causet cylinder,
`P_r(C)=r^{L(C)}(1-r)^{binom(n,2)-R(C)}=|A_p(C)|²`. Hence `μ` has the
prescribed norm on every full cylinder, including every doubly-principal one.
Example `p=(1+i)/2`, `r=1/2`, is nonreal.

For a dual unit vector `h∈L²(P_r)`, the scalar measure `hμ` has density `h`
with respect to `P_r`, so its variation is `||h||_1≤||h||_2≤1`. The constant
`h=1` attains one; therefore `μ` has semivariation one. Its total variation is
infinite: finite partitions by CTP cylinders at increasing levels have sum of
vector norms equal to the absolute scalar level sum `V_n(p)`, which diverges
for this nonreal `p` by the project lower bound. This explicit construction
disproves an infinite-dimensional bounded-semivariation no-go.

For finite-dimensional `X`, choose coordinate functionals `f_1,…,f_d` and
constant `K` with `||x||≤K Σ_j |f_j(x)|`. For each finite partition,
`Σ_i ||μ(E_i)||≤K Σ_j |f_jμ|(Ω)`. Each coordinate variation is bounded by
`||f_j||` times semivariation, so bounded semivariation implies bounded total
variation in finite dimensions. The existing finite-dimensional obstruction
therefore survives.

## Claim-by-claim recommendation

| Claim | Severity / evidence | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Labelled doubly-principal scalar iff theorem | Closest primary predecessor directly leaves its exact extension contribution unresolved; project computes a divergent lower bound. | **Survive narrowly.** State as solution of a named open case; global priority and significance remain bounded. |
| “This is just the full-algebra theorem” | Restriction works only from a measure on the larger algebra; failure there cannot rule out extension on a smaller algebra. | **Kill as objection.** The algebra distinction is material. |
| Conditional covariant classification | Requires normalized measure plus all-principal common-factor rule. | **Survive conditionally only.** No inference to arbitrary covariant measures. |
| Rank-only classification | Stationarity/convolution reduction has no extension construction or no-go. | **Unresolved.** Finite root search does not settle it. |
| Covariant local-renewal refinement | Elementary top-extension calculation; p=0 Dirac boundary remains. | **Survive as a narrow lemma; shrink novelty.** |
| Norm-prescribed vector extension is open | Explicit `L²(P_r)` indicator measure matches all cylinder norms on a nonreal locus. | **Kill.** An infinite-dimensional norm-prescribed extension exists. |
| Bounded-semivariation vector no-go in general | The Hilbert example has semivariation one and infinite variation. | **Kill.** Keep bounded-variation no-go; keep finite-dimensional semivariation obstruction. |
| Fixed-direction vector lift | `μ(C)=A_p(C)v` directly reproduces the scalar complex measure. | **Survive as scalar-equivalent excluded case.** |

The Hilbert construction is a standard indicator-valued measure plus a direct
parameter identity. Its existence is not evidence for a temporal ontology,
physical vector amplitude, or priority in vector measure theory.
