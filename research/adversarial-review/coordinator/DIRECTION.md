Coordinator judgment, 2026-10-21. This records proposed course corrections while
four user-authorized headless GLM lanes run. It is not a declaration of newness,

## Where the project stands

The broad literature and source infrastructure are mature enough for the present
question. They have repeatedly defeated broad novelty claims. The strongest
current mathematical asset is the labelled doubly-principal scalar
classification: analytic review has found no counterexample, and primary reads
identify the precise case earlier authors left inconclusive. Its contribution
is a short estimate/application of an existing criterion, with bounded priority
coverage. The conditional covariant theorem requires an additional value rule;
it is not a result about arbitrary covariant dynamics.

The vector correction is useful and necessary: a standard indicator-valued
measure defeats the earlier infinite-dimensional semivariation claim. It is an
application of established functional analysis, not automatically a second new
result. The finite-Lalpha construction may broaden the explicit example, but
will not by itself classify arbitrary Banach-valued extensions.

The clock lane's current coordinator report says none of its candidate findings
survives as new. It distinguishes a constraint eigenvalue problem from step
invariance/group-action formulations, identifies cases without a dynamical map,
and withdraws a standard-backflow interpretation of nonlinear normalization.
That is a useful negative result. Its claim remains another coordinator's to
review and close; the report is not silently accepted here.

None of this presently establishes eternalism, presentism, objective passage or
an empirical difference between temporal ontologies. A measure-extension result
requires additional physical dynamics and observer/interpretation premises to
bear on those questions. Keeping a mathematical note separate protects both the
mathematics and the original philosophical question.

## Recommended course corrections

1. Finish the scalar adversarial gate and corrections, then write a short note
   only if the precise statement survives. Lead with that statement and its
   predecessor; put conditional variants and scope counterexamples in clearly
   separate sections. Do not delay a defensible small result indefinitely while
   searching for a stronger headline, and do not inflate its significance.
2. Give the current rank-only attempt one sharply bounded analytic target:
   prove an appropriate weighted-convergence/inversion obstruction or construct
   a normalized countably additive measure. The renewal identity in
   RENEWAL_OBSTRUCTION.md sharpens the obligation. Retire finite-root hunting as
   evidence of existence. If neither route closes, record the precise unresolved
   condition and stop expanding this probe.
3. Treat the vector task as scope clarification unless it reveals a result
   beyond the standard indicator construction. Preserve the difference between
   norm matching, phase-preserving amplitudes, and a physical quantum dynamics.
4. Recommend closing the present clock probe after its existing review.
   Further work needs a new, well-posed operational contract: specify physical
   state family, constraint formulation, linear map domain and readout
   instrument before selecting a memory criterion. More parameter sweeps of
   the present example will not supply the missing problem definition.
5. Defer further literature collection, agent multiplication and large Lean
   development. Resources should close the specific proof/priority obligations.
   Agents' arguments, counterexamples and source reads matter; agreement does not.
