## What each found

**Monadic principle.** The geometric condition is known in pieces: Geroch's domain of
prediction, Manchak's theorem that predicting one's own future needs a compact slice,
and the Malament–Manchak results that an observer's past generically fails to fix the
spacetime. No source names "every event's past contains a Cauchy surface" as a
principle. As a law it reduces to two parts: a conformal past of at least π in
curvature units for every event, and no incoming radiation at every event. Since the
hot big bang the conformal past is 0.10 to 0.67 radians for curvature between −0.001
and −0.044, so a pre-history must supply the rest.

*Correction.* The lane says inflation adds about 10⁻¹⁶ of a Hubble time of conformal
time and "rescues nothing". That figure is the conformal time near the end of
inflation. The conformal time accumulated during inflation is dominated by its start
and is exponentially larger. In closed de Sitter space it is exactly π/2 from the
bounce onward, which I checked numerically. So inflation that begins at or near a
curvature bounce supplies up to π/2, and with the later 0.1 to 0.9 radians the total
stays below π. The lane's conclusion stands for a different reason: inflation alone
cannot reach π, and about one further radian must come from before the bounce. A
bouncing closed universe therefore satisfies the principle for late events, as do the
emergent and cyclic histories the lane names.

**Finite openness.** The ultimate visibility limit and the volume factor are in Gott
et al. (2005), who give 2.36 as the ratio of eventually visible to presently visible
galaxies, which is the session's 40 percent. Davis and Lineweaver (2004), Loeb (2002)
and Krauss and Scherrer (2007) state the rest. No one was found drawing a consequence
for temporal ontology, and the lane gives the reason it should not be drawn as stated:
comoving volume is finite, but proper volume, arriving signals and four-volume are not,
so "finite budget" holds on one conventional measure.

**Entanglement present.** A present defined by a correlation threshold in a given state
is selective in space and evades the no-spacelike-scale theorem by using the state.
Its thickness is a few Compton wavelengths for massive vacua, unbounded for massless
ones, and thermal or horizon-scale otherwise. Two things defeat it as an objective
present: the threshold is free, and by Fewster and Verch no state can be chosen
covariantly, so the relation is relative to a world and a state. The nearest published
neighbour found is an unrefereed preprint.

**Cohomology of time.** The germ-precedence order, read as a category, has a
contractible nerve exactly when there is no local clock. Sanders's use of first
cohomology classifies sign choices, which exist because the fibre has automorphisms.
A clock takes values in an ordered set with none, so there is nothing for a first
cohomology to classify. The lane stops, as the brief asked.

*Slip.* The lane says no local clock is equivalent to the flat germ being initial.
The criterion in the local-clock note needs the flat germ to be reachable from every
germ as well as to reach it, so initial and terminal. The verdict is unaffected.

**Bell for passage.** Horvat's paper was read at source. His Proposition 3 shows that
probabilistic locality together with irrelevance of history implies Bell's local
causality, so that conjunction is excluded by experiment. Passage alone entails neither
premise, each premise can be dropped separately, and empirically equivalent theories
can carry different ontologies of becoming. So no inequality bears on passage as such.
A corollary for the Markovian lane: Builes and Impagnatiello's datum is falsifiable
only jointly with a locality premise that can be detached. This resolves point 6 of
the 8 November audit for that lane, at the worker's read depth.

## Disposition

All five are integrated as bounded longshot records. Four are negative results and
should stay closed. The entanglement lane leaves one checkable question: whether a
covariantly defined entanglement measure between causally disjoint regions of a massive
free field decays exponentially with separation. The monadic lane leaves one sharpened
statement: in a closed universe the principle holds for late events exactly when the
conformal time before the bounce is at least about one radian.

exhausted their quotas, so coverage rests on a scholarly index and one semantic search
