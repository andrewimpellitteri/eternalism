# Novelty verdict after the non-atomic construction

22 October 2026. **Novelty unestablished. Potentially a distinct, narrow
constructive example; known methods and ideas.** Mathematical validation and
priority are different gates. The independent review supports the former,
not the latter. This is not a new general QSG theorem or a physics result.

The only candidate originality claim worth testing is:

> An explicit nonzero scalar ratio-BC/MSR growth pair with norm-only cylinder
> covariance, equal magnitudes on every corresponding individual finite
> labelled cylinder, atomless finite complex-measure extensions, and unequal
> rank-one quantum measures on one specified intrinsic completed event.

The recipe making this combination work is the chain-ideal phase counterterm:
the only unequal early weight is the two-chain weight, so putting its total
ideal multiplicity into f_A-f_B makes every later w exactly common. Choosing
the real part by a square root preserves common |f|, and a real-percolation
reference controls the resulting small phase errors. That explicit combination
was not identified in the passages checked below. This is a report of search
coverage, not a proof of absence or a claim that it resolves an author's stated
open problem.

## Strongest defeat case

The construction may be an elementary interpolation exercise in the known
scalar parametrization. Once the only unequal seed weights are identified,
their ideal multiplicities directly prescribe a compensating imaginary part
of f. Exponential positive reference weights then dominate polynomial errors,
and standard summable-variation machinery completes the measures. Even if this
exact recipe is unpublished, an expert may reasonably regard it as a worked
example rather than a substantial new theorem.

There is also a relevance gate: full vector/DF covariance is weakened to
cylinder norms. The example rigorously shows that this weaker data condition
does not determine intrinsic completed quantum-measure values, even in an
atomless setting. It does not demonstrate why that weaker condition is the
right physical symmetry requirement. The completed gap is already visible
in some finite coherent-union data, and no instrument is given. It provides
no empirical or temporal-ontology conclusion.

## Search coverage and decision

Targeted queries included “causal set same norm phase complex”, “sequential
growth phase covariance complex measure”, “complex measures cylinder
magnitudes”, “causal set non-atomic complex”, and the primary amplitude-
process / quantum-measure-extension searches. Unrelated results and secondary
summaries were not used as mathematical evidence. The inspected primary
passages do not identify the exact constrained example, but these bounded
searches cannot establish priority. `sources.jsonl` records exact versions,
locators and limits.

Retain the result as an independently reviewed mathematical construction.
Use “explicit non-atomic matched-cylinder example”, not “new theorem” or
“new physics”. Before a publishable novelty claim, compare the full nearest
primary lineages and cited examples, test whether a general finite-seed
extension lemma is already standard, and obtain an external specialist's
assessment of both priority and significance. If it is an immediate known
corollary or lacks use beyond the engineered example, keep it as a checked
counterexample without an originality claim.
