# Non-atomic matched-cylinder continuation: claim contract

22 October 2026. Proposed target, subject to independent review.

Keep the nonzero scalar rule t(C,P)=q(C)w(P), MSR reconstruction
w(C)=f(C)-sum_proper_ideals w(P), f=1/q, and norm-only cylinder covariance.
Keep the two-phase seed: q(single)=1/2, q(anti2)=u/4 in both models,
q(chain2)=u/4 in A and conjugate(u)/4 in B, u=(3+4i)/5.

For n>=3 let L=1000, R_n=L^n and c(C) count actual two-element chain ideals.
Propose h(C)=-16c(C)/5, x(C)=sqrt(R_n^2-h(C)^2)>0 and

    f_A(C)=x(C)+i h(C),    f_B(C)=x(C)-i h(C).

The proposed exact cancellation makes w_A(C)=w_B(C) at every size >=3:
the only earlier unequal weight is chain2, whose difference is -32i/5;
f_A-f_B equals that difference times its actual ideal multiplicity. The
parent f moduli stay R_n, so both models match every individual finite
cylinder magnitude. The construction does not impose unit timid modulus.

Compare to real transitive percolation f0(C)=L^|C| and
w0(C)=L^|C|(1-1/L)^|Max(C)|. Prove nonzero w, summable row distance and
an explicit L1(P0) complex-measure completion. Absolute continuity of
variation with respect to an atomless reference would give atomlessness
on the labelled-history sigma algebra. Only gregarious births change minima,
so bound completed E2 amplitude changes by sum_(n>=3) V_n/R_n, rather than
by all non-timid transitions. Preserve a strict completed E2 diagonal gap.

Nearest predecessors: Rideout–Sorkin factorization / real percolation;
Srivastava–Surya's explicit norm-only phase option; Surya–Zalel's complex
extension and summable variation results. The prior one-phase non-atomic
example changed cylinder magnitudes; the matched example was forced atomic.
This target combines the two properties by an explicit cancellation. Known
machinery, with priority for the exact example unestablished.

Kills: radicand or transition zero; wrong ideal multiplicity; failure of
all-stage matching; divergent variation / no countably additive extension;
only removing point atoms on a space where that fails to imply atomlessness;
or a completed E2 error exceeding the early gap. No finite cutoff may stand
in for the all-stage proof. Standard-library exact/interval checks only.

The data must be NEW compared with the earlier atomic pair: all-cylinder
magnitudes already determine the variation measure. Single-cylinder data
do not include coherent finite-union quantum measures. No assertion about
atomlessness of an unlabelled quotient, operator QBC, full vector covariance,
an instrument, physical realism, temporal ontology, or theorem priority.
