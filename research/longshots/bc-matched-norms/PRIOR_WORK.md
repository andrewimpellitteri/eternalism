# Prior-work gate for the matched-cylinder pair

22 October 2026. Bounded audit before implementing the proposed continuation.
The earlier `../bc-phase-audit/PRIORITY_REVIEW.md` verdict remains in force:
**known machinery / immediate adaptation; no theorem-priority claim**.

The mathematical question is narrower than generic phase retrieval: can the
same magnitudes of **every individual finite labelled cylinder** coexist with
different values of a fixed intrinsic completed event, while scalar ratio BC,
MSR, nonzero transitions and a finite complex-measure extension also hold?
The one-parameter example was defeated by its global-conjugation rigidity.
The proposed two-phase seed must overcome that specific obstruction.

The expected value, if the construction survives review, is a concrete
counterexample to an overly strong inference from cylinder magnitudes. It
would not discover phase ambiguity, establish empirical distinguishability,
or address temporal ontology. Agreement on all finite **unions** is a stronger
requirement: the accompanying result must state its exact data boundary.
