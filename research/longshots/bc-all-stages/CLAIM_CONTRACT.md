# Claim contract: a scalar phase continuation under weaker covariance

22 October 2026. Mathematical existence / scoped parametrization, no priority
verdict and no physical prediction. Original implementation followed a bounded
primary audit; this contract records the resulting scope after proof review.

**Question.** Do nonvanishing scalar transitions, MSR, scalar ratio Bell
causality and norm-only cylinder covariance force finite phase differences
to vanish when extended beyond stage four or when a labelled complex-measure
completion is required?

**Result.** No in the specified isomorphism-invariant scalar Markov class.
The q(C)w(P) recursion parametrizes that nonzero class, with norm covariance
precisely a common |q| per cardinality. A fixed earlier cp phase admits both
an atomic summable-timid-tail completion and a non-atomic reference-percolation
completion, with no zero transitions. The latter has |nu| absolutely
continuous with respect to the reference and no variation on eventually-timid
histories. No vector/DF covariance or noncommutative result is asserted.

**Three nearest predecessors.** Rideout–Sorkin gr-qc/9904062v3 provides the
classical gregarious/past factorization and replacement argument under full
classical covariance. Srivastava–Surya 2603.25503v1 explicitly sets aside
norm-only phases while studying stronger vector covariance and operator QBC.
Surya–Zalel 2003.11311v1 provides the bounded-variation and summable-excess
extension criteria. Exact read scopes and primary locators in sources.jsonl.

**Delta being tested, not a novelty assertion.** Explicit persistence of the
accepted phase seed at every stage, with nonzero transitions and a labelled
completion; the second variant removes an eventual-timid support artifact.
All algebraic and extension machinery has known predecessors. The restricted
norm adaptation could already be an immediate known corollary. No paper or
originality claim should be promoted from this lane without a separate
priority search and physical correspondence audit.

**Checks.** Independent analytic review; exact Gaussian-rational enumeration
through five births; 407 nonzero transitions and 1619 BC equations; two
independent expressions for the second variant's weights (ideal recursion
versus maximal-subset Möbius inversion); analytic inductive bounds and L1/TV
proofs carry the infinite claims.

operator-valued or phase-modified Bell causality is another problem; a physical

The methods do not select an ontology or address empirical gravity.
