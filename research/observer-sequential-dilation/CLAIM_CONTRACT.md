## Proposed statements and kills

1. A deterministic prefix-preserving isometry cannot change the parent-sector
   probabilities; the next normalized variation state demands factors s(C).
   Nonconstant s(C) on occupied parents obstructs that specific preparation.
2. A success Kraus map with coefficients sqrt(|t|/B_n) times the transition
   phase exists for any common upper bound B_n>=sum|t|. A failure flag
   completes an isometry. Use B_n=1+zeta_n, not a global state oracle.
3. Success telescopes. The row bounds may make accumulated heralding losses
   bounded even as the number of finite growth steps grows.
4. Use the adjoint COMMON positive-growth dilation for readout, rather than
   assuming a free projector onto the terminal reference state. Count its
   additional success factor and all failure outcomes.

Kill if phases/normalizations drift, parent information is silently erased,
rejected flags retain uncharged information, readout uses a hidden final-state
oracle, or success conditioned on preparation is reported as unconditional.
Bounded finite arithmetic evidence plus independent review are required.

Finite history-register causality is not spacetime locality. Inverse gate
access is not cosmological backward evolution. A positive limit of finite
success probabilities is not a finite implementation of an infinite-event
detector. Full completed-label covariance and physical resources remain open.
