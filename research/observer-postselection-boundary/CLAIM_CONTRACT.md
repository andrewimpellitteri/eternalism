# Observer continuation contract

22 October 2026; coordinator claim codex-observer-postselect.

Question: what operational structure can be constructed from a normalized
finite complex history measure, and which extra apparatus assumptions does
that construction require? Fixed input is the matched atomless scalar pair.
A conditional laboratory-register realization is useful; identifying it with
an internal causal-set observer without dynamics is not an allowed shortcut.

## Prior comparison before implementation

1. Dowker–Johnston–Sorkin, arXiv:1002.0589v2 (10 June 2010), Section 2.3,
   Eq. (2.1) and quotient/completion construction: the decoherence functional
   supplies the history Hilbert space. The rank-one specialization is elementary
   and must be separated from the auxiliary L2 spaces in our prior pilot.
2. Aharonov–Vaidman, quant-ph/0105101v2, Sections 2 and 3.2, ABL rule and
   Eq. (21): pre/postselected matrix-element ratios are weak values, not
   ordinary probabilities. Our proposed dilation is this established structure
   applied to a complex measure, with a variation-norm bound derived directly.
3. Mozota Frauca–Sorkin, arXiv:1610.02087v1, Section 5.2, Eqs. (42)–(46):
   actual history filters include apparatus and endpoint structure. A bare
   scalar measure does not import those instruments automatically.
4. Wagner et al., arXiv:2302.00705v2, Sections 3 and 4.2: weak values can
   also be estimated by circuits without postselection. An optimal success
   bound for a named pre/postselection realization is therefore not a universal
   measurement/sample-complexity bound. Their circuits/results are not reproduced.

Named primary sections were inspected in full text this turn; no full-paper
priority audit or experiment replication. Established ingredients, no novelty
claim. Existing record-channel adjoint-image no-gos are prior project results,
not a new theorem to reannounce here.

## Candidates and checks

- Reconstruct the actual rank-one history Hilbert space; test whether event
  intersections define operators on its null quotient.
- Use the intrinsic total variation lambda=|nu| as the auxiliary measure,
  removing the earlier arbitrary P0 choice and any need for L2(P0). This is a
  mathematical construction, not a dynamically selected observer space.
- Realize nu(E) as <phi|P_E|psi>/<phi|psi> and prove or refute the attainable
  bound p_success<=1/|nu|(X)^2 for normalized pure states and one PVM.
- Give a complete finite instrument and all outcomes; test record-only versus
  coherently postselected access on the actual seven-history seed. Retain
  controls that expose disturbance and postselection-rate bias.

Kill conditions: missing normalization, a claimed event operator not descending
through null vectors, interpreting a weak value as frequency, using baseline
postselection probability after an intervening measurement, or calling a
register construction a completed-causal-set detector. Model-dependent coins
and classically hardcoded answers do not demonstrate a common apparatus.

Deliver an analytic bound, exact finite certificate, bounded independent review,
source-depth record and an honest physical limitation. No installs/large runs.
