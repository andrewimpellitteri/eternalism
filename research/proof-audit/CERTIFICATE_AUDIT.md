mathematical claims, their assumptions, and the correspondence between the
paper statements, the proof draft, and the reproducible checks. It is not a
Lean build; see `formal/INFEASIBILITY.md` for why a faithful Lean encoding is

measure-existence statement on a labelled event algebra.

## Claim 1 — labelled doubly-principal classification

**Statement.** Let `q=1-p`, `p∈ℂ`; `A_p(C)=p^{L(C)}q^{binom(|C|,2)-R(C)}` for a
naturally labelled finite causet. `C` is principal iff it has a unique maximal
element; doubly principal iff `C` and `C` with the last label deleted are
principal. Let `R̃_p` be the sigma-algebra generated by doubly-principal
cylinders. There is a countably additive finite-valued complex measure on
`R̃_p` agreeing with `A_p(C)` on every doubly-principal cylinder **iff**
`p∈[0,1]`.

**Scope.** Labelled generator algebra; not the covariant intersection
`R_p=R̃_p∩R`; not Hilbert-valued or decoherence-functional measures. The
larger principal-cylinder algebra is excluded by restriction.

### Step audit

| Step | Claim | Check | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every doubly-principal `C` on `k+2` labels is uniquely `G⊕{u}⊕{v}` for a naturally labelled `k`-order `G` | The last label is the unique max; deleting it leaves a unique max (the penultimate); conversely two universal caps make both conditions hold. Bijection on labelled orders. | **Correct.** Reproduced at `k≤4` by the exact enumeration (7, 40, 357 causets at levels 3–5). |
| 2 | Covers of `C` are `L(G)+M(G)+1`; incomparable pairs are `h(G)=binom(k,2)-R(G)` | `u` covers each maximal element of `G`; `v` covers `u`; all cross pairs otherwise related; the `k` new-to-`G` pairs and `k+1` `v`-pairs are relations. | **Correct.** |
| 3 | `V_{k+2}=a Σ_G a^{w(G)} b^{h(G)}`, `w=L+M`, `a=|p|`, `b=|1-p|` | Factor one `a` from the `u,v` covers. | **Correct.** |
| 4 | `w(G)≥k` | Each nonmaximal element has an outgoing cover; `k-M` nonmaximal, so `L≥k-M`. | **Correct.** |
| 5 | `V≥a t^k Σ_G r^w s^h`, `t=a+b`, `r=a/t`, `s=b/t` | `w+h≥k` and `t^{w+h}r^w s^h=a^w b^h`. | **Correct.** |
| 6 | `Σ_G r^{L+M}s^h = F_k(s)=∏_{j=1}^k(1-s^j)` | Independent-edge random graph order with edge probability `r`; a cover must be present, an incomparable pair absent, redundant comparable edges sum out. The event "last of `k+1` is above all earlier" has this probability; reversing order and exposing edges gives the product. | **Correct.** The exact finite check reproduces the identity for `k≤4` at rational `r+s=1`. |
| 7 | `F_k(s)≥c_s=∏_{j≥1}(1-s^j)>0` for `0<s<1` | Partial products decrease to the positive infinite product; `c_s>0`. | **Correct.** Standard analysis; no formal build. |
| 8 | `V_{k+2}→∞` for `p∉[0,1]` | `t>1` by strict triangle inequality; `a>0`; `a t^k c_s→∞`. | **Correct.** |
| 9 | Contradiction with a finite complex measure | At each level the doubly-principal cylinders are pairwise disjoint; finite additivity gives `Σ|μ(C)|≤‖μ‖_TV`; `V_{k+2}` is exactly that sum; a finite countably additive complex measure has finite total variation. | **Correct**, provided "finite-valued" is read as a countably additive complex measure (which is automatically bounded). The draft should state this standard lemma explicitly. |
| 10 | Existence for `p∈[0,1]` | Classical transitive percolation gives a probability measure on the full labelled algebra; restrict. `p=0` is the infinite antichain; `p=1` the deterministic chain. | **Correct.** |

### Finite certificate

Exact `Q(√2)` computation at `p=(1+i)/2`, `q=(1-i)/2`, `|p|=|q|=√2/2`:

- `V_5 = 3/4 + 3√2/16 ≈ 1.015165042945`
- `V_6 = 31/32 + 55√2/64 ≈ 2.184089780164`

The independent implementation (`experiments/baseline/reproduce.py`) and the
fresh-audit script (`research/creative/proof-audit/verify_small.py`) agree.
These are finite instances; they do not prove the all-level divergence.

### Assumptions and axioms (paper proof)

- Classical logic and standard set theory; no large-cardinal or nonstandard
  axiom.
- Countable additivity and finite total variation of the complex measure.
- Finite additivity on disjoint cylinders; finite inclusion–exclusion.
- Strict triangle inequality; convergence/positivity of the Euler product.
- No `sorry`, no assumed target theorem, no proof-assistant axiom. There is no
  Lean proof at all, so the "transitive axiom audit" is of the paper proof, not
  a compiled dependency closure.

## Claim 2 — conditional covariant post-factor theorem

**Statement.** On the covariant post algebra `R_p`, let `ν` be a finite
countably additive complex measure with `ν(P_∞)=1`. For a principal `H` of size
`n`, let `D_H` be the event of a post with inclusive past `H`; let `N(H)` be
the number of distinct naturally labelled representatives. If there is one
finite `κ` with `ν(D_H)=κN(H)A_p(H)` for **every principal** `H` (assumption
(T)), then such a measure exists **iff** `0<p≤1`.

### Step audit

| Step | Claim | Check | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | `D_H` is measurable and covariant; it lies in `R_p` | The post has label `n-1` in every natural labeling; intersect with `P_∞`; Theorem 14/15 identification. | **Correct at the read depth.** |
| C2 | The `D_H` are laminar; if `|H|<|J|` and `D_H∩D_J≠∅`, then `D_J⊆D_H` | A history in the intersection has an earlier post with past `H` and a later post with past `J`; the earlier post is a post inside `J`, so every history in `D_J` has it. | **Correct.** |
| C3 | The countable family of all principal `D_H` covers `P_∞` | Every infinite-post history has a first post; its inclusive past is principal. | **Correct.** |
| C4 | `κ=0` contradicts normalization | All `D_H` zero; finite unions of a laminar family have measure zero by inclusion–exclusion; finite unions increase to `P_∞`; continuity from below gives zero. | **Correct.** |
| C5 | `p=0` contradicts normalization | Principal `H` of size ≥2 have `A_0(H)=0`; those events cover `P_∞` (at least two posts); same argument. | **Correct.** |
| C6 | For `p∉[0,1]`, grouping by unlabeled shape gives `Σ|ν(D_H)|=|κ|V_n` | `N(H)` counts natural labelings; `A_p` is isomorphism-invariant; same-rank `D_H` are disjoint. | **Correct.** |
| C7 | Divergence contradicts finite total variation | `|κ|V_n→∞` with `κ≠0`. | **Correct.** |
| C8 | Existence for `0<p≤1` | Classical CTP with `κ=c(1-p)>0`; `p=1` chain with `κ=1`; `p=0` excluded because the antichain has no posts and `ν(P_∞)=0`. | **Correct.** |
| C9 | (T) is not implied by covariance | The Dirac history in `experiments/extensions/countermodel.py` is covariant, normalized, satisfies the doubly-principal-only rule with `κ=0` for every `p`, but violates the all-principal rule. | **Correct.** Explicit witness. |

**Countermodel check.** The history `(A_2⊕{z_1})⊕(A_2⊕{z_2})⊕…` has posts `z_j`
whose inclusive pasts `H_j` are principal but not doubly principal. The Dirac
measure assigns zero to every doubly-principal `D_H`, so the weakened rule
holds with `κ=0` for all `p`. At `p=0.5`, `N(H_1)A_p(H_1)=0.25` and
`N(H_2)A_p(H_2)=0.015625`, so no single `κ` fits both in (T). Verified by
`countermodel.py`.

### Added-assumption audit

- (T) is a **dynamical renewal axiom**, not a consequence of covariance or of
  the labelled cylinder values. The finite-cutoff prescription
  `ν(D_H)=lim_M V_{H,M}(p)=c(q)N(H)A_p(H)` is one way to satisfy it, but it is
  a specified limit of finite sums, not continuity of a measure already proved
  not to exist.
- A rank-only factor `κ_n` only gives the necessary attenuation bound
  `|κ_n|V_n≤‖ν‖`; no rank-dependent construction is known. A shape-dependent
  factor is vacuous (any measure defines one). Vector-valued measures are not
  covered because a countably additive Banach-valued measure need not have
  finite total variation.
- Dropping `ν(P_∞)=1` admits the zero measure for every `p`; dropping the
  all-principal coverage admits the Dirac countermodel.

## Meaning audit

- The mathematical statement is a no-go/existence classification for a scalar
  complex measure with prescribed cylinder values on a labelled algebra. It
  does not assert that the amplitudes are physical, that the measure is a
  quantum measure or decoherence functional, or that the events are observable.
- The conditional version adds a factorization axiom on post events. It is a
  measure-theoretic boundary, not a dynamics. "Post" is a causal-set element,
  not an observer record.
- No conclusion follows about temporal ontology, objective passage,
  determinism, causal order, clock time, or the thermodynamic arrow. Any such
  inference needs an explicit bridge premise not supplied here.
- The verified finite certificate is narrower than the theorem; the theorem is
  narrower than any physical interpretation.

## Verdict

- **Finite certificate:** verified exactly and independently.
- **Paper proof:** audited step by step; no falsifier found in the read
  sources; the one wording gap is that "finite-valued" should be stated as a
  countably additive complex measure with finite total variation.
- **Lean certificate:** not built. A faithful encoding is a staged project;
  the infeasibility review and route are in `formal/INFEASIBILITY.md`. No
  vacuous or axiomatized encoding was substituted.
- **Meaning:** the result is a measure-existence classification only; no
  physical or ontological claim is certified.
