## Open limits

The cited Brading papers accept special relativity as a complete account of spatiotemporal structure in the relevant regime, but do not specify a concrete solution, complete matter-dynamics package, and operational access regime sufficient to compute empirical likelihoods or identify the sustaining region for a specified system. Brading’s 2015 extent is system-relative and potentially nontransitive (p. 17); the case does not infer that other genuine systems outside one selected system’s present are unreal. The 2013 and 2015 copies are not separately publisher-file-collated. The Balashov–Janssen version inspected is the longer archive preprint and the Brown–Pooley version is an arXiv draft; neither was collated against its final publication. A worked model or an independent defense of the disputed ontological bridge could change the result.
