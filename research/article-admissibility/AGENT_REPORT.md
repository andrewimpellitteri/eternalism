## Outcome

**Exposition-only, with an explicit incomplete-model/bridge diagnosis.** The
bounded case does not establish a new method, empirical discriminator or
preference for a temporal ontology. It tests the article's two admissibility
profiles on Brading's law-constitutive proposal and a reconstructed strongest
block-compatible rival. The rival may grant dynamical unity without accepting
that a sustaining region exhausts existence for that system. The explanatory
arguments of Balashov–Janssen and Brown–Pooley inform different versions of
this reconstruction; neither directly argues against Brading.

The empirical profile yields only conditional eligibility: consistent and
adequate realizations in a common model have not been constructed or checked.
Equal likelihoods are a sensitivity-test stipulation, not a measured or proved
property of these programs. The constitutive profile does not establish a
winner from the arguments inspected. Requiring fidelity to Brading's own
extent conditions is legitimate; exporting her contested bridge as a neutral
rule excluding rivals, or treating it as independent evidence, would beg the
question. Neither conclusion rules out a better future argument or model.

The useful manuscript contribution is this worked limitation and a faithful
strong rival, not a claim that symmetry of standards makes them neutral.
`case/PROPOSED_PAPER_EDITS.md` gives reviewable proposals only. The working
manuscript, source registers and selected mathematical result were not changed.
