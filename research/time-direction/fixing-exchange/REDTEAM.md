# Red team of the exchange note, and what replaced it

10 November 2026. Same session and model as the note, at the user's request. A
self-attack, not an independent review. It targets the first version of
[RESULT.md](RESULT.md) and then the second version that replaced it.

## Part A. Attack on the first version

| # | Claim in v1 | Attack | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Exchange gives the missing "symmetry at one base" | Exchange is about adding a *single event*. In a field theory above 1+1 dimensions one extra point value almost never fixes another, so the proposition is nearly vacuous where it matters. The paper's sentence compares the past base with the future base, which exchange does not touch | **Sustained.** Exchange demoted to §6. Replaced by reciprocity and balance for regions |
| A2 | "Fixed given" and "has become for" cannot be the same operation (matroid against antimatroid) | Cheap. The becoming closure is generated by a relation and F is not, which already separates them in one line. No one defends the identity. The combinatorial vocabulary was decoration | **Sustained.** Reduced to Remark 12 with the one-line reason |
| A3 | The heat equation's time asymmetry is "invisible to exact fixing" | True of exchange, trivially, because the class is linear. For regions it depends on backward uniqueness, which holds only in suitable classes and was not checked | **Partly sustained.** Now stated conditionally and marked as cited |
| A4 | One-way fixing "runs down the entropy gradient" | Correct for discrete readings. False for differential entropy. The equality clause needed "almost surely" | **Sustained as a scope limit.** Kept with the limit |
| A5 | Example of one-way fixing in either time direction | A one-parameter family of solutions is contrived | **Conceded.** Kept as an existence example only |
| A6 | Alignment with time "needs an entropy condition" | Asserted, not shown | **Sustained.** Replaced by the four-source table, which follows from the balance |
| A7 | The paper's sentence is unproved | Understated. It is false at a single standpoint in the paper's own closed dust model | **Strengthened.** See §4 of v2 |
| A8 | Exact fixing is the right object | Exact fixing of point readings is a measure-zero notion and hides what is conserved | **Sustained.** v2 moves to quantities and to entropies |

## Part B. Attack on the second version

| # | Claim in v2 | Attack | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Reciprocity | A tautology presented as a result | **Conceded and said so in the text.** Its use is to relocate the asymmetry to the remainders |
| B2 | Balance | The chain rule of entropy. Known to everyone who has seen it | **Conceded.** The claim is the application. If a philosopher of time has already used it this way, the note has no residue beyond §4 |
| B3 | Closed dust has an arrow of exact fixing | Depends on F(C) = M for τ ≤ π, which comes from the mirror proposition and the reflection symmetry of the scale factor, not from an independent calculation. Also "everything about the past is fixed by the future" needs K_C = 0, which is F(C) = M | **Stands, with inherited status.** The paper itself asserts the mirror for this model |
| B4 | The arrow there is "not entropy" | It is an asymmetry of information between two regions, which is an entropy difference in the sense of the balance | **Clarified.** Not thermodynamic entropy and not a boundary condition on the matter. It is the size of the cones |
| B5 | Stationary chance makes the past as open as the future | First attack: state by state it is false. Second attack: the doubling map is stationary, exactly future-deterministic and not past-deterministic | **Both sustained as scope limits.** The claim is now for discrete states or finite resolution, in total. The doubling map is in the text as the boundary case, and it turned out to illustrate the exact-against-practical distinction |
| B6 | Nomic Presentism needs a non-stationary posit | A presentist can deny that the past's fixity is nomic at all, and ground it in something else (thisnesses, tensed facts). Then the result does not touch them | **Sustained.** The claim is restricted to views that ground past truths in the present and the laws, which is how the lane reads Builes and Impagnatiello |
| B7 | Same | The chance measure in a nomic view is over futures given the present. A backward conditional needs a prior over present states. Stationarity supplies it. Without any prior, backward entropy is undefined, and the comparison may be rejected | **Open.** The possibilistic Proposition 8 avoids measures and gives the same verdict for counts. A defender who rejects both a prior and counting owes another measure of openness |
| B8 | Four sources are exhaustive | "Exhaustive" only in the sense that the balance leaves info(C) ≠ info(A) as the sole condition. The four rows are a sorting of ways that can happen, not a theorem | **Reworded** to "can arise only from" a difference in information, with the rows as kinds |
| B9 | Macroscopic sign caution | Could be read as contradicting the usual story that the Past Hypothesis makes the future open | **Stands.** The usual story concerns branching macrohistories, where description entropy grows. Relaxation to equilibrium gives the other sign. Both are in the text |

## Part C. What survives, in order of confidence

1. **§4 (closed dust).** A corrected reading of the paper's own theorem. It contradicts
   a sentence of the paper and is easy to check against Theorem `thm:massive`.
2. **§§2–3 (reciprocity and balance).** Certainly true. Their worth depends on whether
   the framing is already in the literature.
3. **§5 (stationary symmetry).** True within its scope. The philosophical use against
   nomic grounding of the past is the part most likely to be either known or contested
   (B6, B7).
4. **§6 (exchange).** True and minor.

## Part D. What would raise the status

- An outside reader in philosophy of time on B2, B6 and B7.
- A direct read of Markosian (1995), Earman (1986) ch. II, and the two
  computational-mechanics papers, to fix credit.
- An independent calculation of F(I⁺(b)) in the closed dust model that does not go
  through the mirror.
- A worked non-stationary example with a past condition, showing the balance with a
  physically motivated measure and not a three-state chain.
