## What the combined evidence means

The previous roughly 42.23% limiting baseline is still a conditional success
bound for the ideal FULL history-register protocol. It cannot be carried over
to the local restricted-precursor experiment: its absolute retained branch
mass decays when the number of vertices outside the past grows. Renormalizing
that local branch changes the instrument and calibration.

Likewise, the resource lane's fixed positive gap G belongs to the full
register protocol. Its sufficient precision schedule cannot simply be reused
for a different experiment whose ideal signal may shrink exponentially. The
quadratic-qubit lower bound constrains the faithful full-register architecture;
it is not a proof that a statistic-only observer needs that memory.

The failed orbit-uniform intertwiner is an obstruction to one specific route
to a quotient dynamics. It is compatible with correct behavior on the special
positive preparation phi_n and with covariant effects at a fixed finite depth.
It neither proves nor disproves full causal-set covariance or gauge freedom.
