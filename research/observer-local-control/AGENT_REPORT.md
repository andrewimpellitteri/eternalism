## Main result

For controls selected only by an observer record, the heralded encoding requires
the normalized square-root transition row to factor through that record. To
reproduce the full heralded instrument, row total variation must factor through
the record too. A row-wide phase is ignorable on an isolated branch but must be
tracked relative to other coherent parent branches. The exact finite example
has identical local records and herald rates, but different conditional rows
`(1/2,1/2)` and `(1,0)` with square-root-state overlap `1/sqrt(2)`. It is an
abstract access counterexample, not an actual matched-model causet witness.
Identical rows and precursor alphabets give the restricted positive case.
