## Core proof audit

**Exact dust causal criterion checks out.** For the conformal product metric with `a(τ)>0`, causal curves obey spatial round-sphere speed at most one with respect to conformal time. Thus `(τ,Y)≼(σ,Z)` implies `dₛ₃(Y,Z)≤σ−τ`. Conversely, when this inequality holds, a minimizing round-sphere geodesic traversed at constant speed `dₛ₃/(σ−τ)≤1` over the time interval is causal. At equal times the inequality forces `Y=Z`, so the relation including equality is exactly the causal order. This argument is confined to the stated cylinder and metric.

**The 1-Lipschitz chain representation and endpoint limits are sound, with two omitted attained-endpoint cases.** Distinct comparable chain events have distinct conformal times, so projection to `X⊆(0,2π)` defines `f:X→S³`; the exact order inequality gives `dₛ₃(f(s),f(t))≤t−s`. Completeness of `S³` makes images of sequences approaching `inf X` or `sup X` Cauchy, and the Lipschitz estimate makes their limits sequence-independent. For a lower-bounded nonempty chain without a minimum, the endpoint limit is its greatest lower bound; for one with a minimum, the minimum itself is immediately the greatest lower bound. Dually, for an upper-bounded chain without a maximum, the endpoint limit is its least upper bound; with a maximum, that maximum is immediately the least upper bound. The RESULT.md prose introduces a sequence only in the non-attained case, then uses it in the “any lower/upper bound” paragraph without separately disposing of the attained case. This is a small proof omission with the immediate case split just stated; it does not undermine the endpoint argument.

The strict interior endpoint claims are correct: a lower bound `q` keeps `inf X≥τq>0`, and an upper bound `b` keeps `sup X≤τb<2π`; nonemptiness of `X` supplies the other strict endpoint inequality. Thus both limits, when needed, lie in `M`.

## Errors and qualifications to record

1. **Definition 15(2a) interval-symbol transcription.** I inspected the supplied page image `/private/tmp/eternalism-placek-def15.png` (rendered from `/private/tmp/eternalism-placek-2014.pdf`, printed p. 200). The printed clause is `t_{>x} ∩ t_{<y} ⊆ O` (the source uses strict `\succ` and `\prec` symbols). RESULT.md has `t_{≤x} ∩ t_{<y}`. The correct lower interval bound is strict `>x`, not `≥x`; my earlier review's proposed `≥x` correction was itself wrong. Since `x,y∈O`, the proof's use of `O=M` still makes the subset clause automatic, so the typo does not affect its existence-of-neighbors argument; it misstates the source axiom. The page image is the direct visual check for this reading.
2. **Nonempty-chain convention remains unconfirmed.** RESULT.md elects to treat the chain clauses as applying only to nonempty chains. Definition 15(2b)–(c), as printed, does not visibly append `C≠∅` (contrast Placek’s earlier Definition 1, which explicitly writes `C≠∅`). If empty chains count, the proposed open cylinder has neither an infimum of the empty lower-bounded chain nor a minimum of `{x∈M:x≼b}` for clause 2(c), since it has no minimal events. Therefore the Definition 15 result remains conditional on the nonempty-chain convention. The same empty-chain issue also affects 2(b), not only 2(c). Coordinator synthesis will retain this qualification.

## Definition 17 and history consequence

Under the nonempty-chain convention, clause 2(c)'s unique minimum is the least upper bound of `C` within the interval below `b`. The endpoint proof identifies that point with the chain's global least upper bound in `M`, independent of which upper bound `b` was chosen. Hence Definition 17 cannot produce two distinct bound-relative minima in this one-patch model. There are no splitting pairs; Condition 18 is then vacuous, and Definitions 19–20 make every event-pair consistent. Consequently `M` is the unique maximal consistent subset and thus the unique g-history by Definition 23. Definitions 21–22 are compatible with this consequence; no extra assertion from them is needed once the inconsistent-pair relation is empty.

This conclusion concerns only the generalized-BST model under the clause-level construction. It does not establish a generalized manifold, local chart atlas, tangent-space construction, or atlas-level GR correspondence. The no-common-future antipodal events do not contradict this result: Placek's g-history criterion is maximal consistency controlled by splitting pairs, not maximal upward directedness.

## Disposition

The dust causal criterion and chain Lipschitz endpoint proof support the stated narrow result under the nonempty-chain convention, after correcting the source transcription to `t_{>x}` and explicitly splitting off the attained-minimum/maximum cases. The coordinator will add those attained-endpoint cases to the GR result/report during integration. The empty-chain convention remains unconfirmed and is carried into synthesis conditionally. No atlas-level GR correspondence is claimed or verified here.
