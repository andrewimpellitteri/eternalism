## Essential

Fabrice Correia and Sven Rosenkranz, **Bivalence, Future Contingents and
the Open Future**, chapter 7 of *Nothing To Come* (2018), pp.99–116.

- DOI: [10.1007/978-3-319-78704-6_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-78704-6_7)
- Priority: §7.2, “The Grounding Requirement on Truth Revisited,” starting
  p.106; §7.3, “Indeterminism and the Open Future,” starting p.112.
- Reason: the authors explicitly propose bivalence with temporally
  distributed truth-grounding, a direct alternative to the preliminary
  demand for complete currently existing grounds.

## What can be said at the checked depth

The chapter7 abstract and visible note3 explicitly oppose requiring a
truthmaker at the utterance time, suggesting that grounds may exist at
some time instead. This supports that narrow attribution. It does not
verify the complete logic, the indeterminism formulation, the handling of
negative truths or the response to all truthmaking objections.

Chapter9's abstract advertises spacetime-sensitive logic and competing
ontologies. It does not establish equivalence to our particular domain
model, nor classify which additional assumptions its theorems require.

The paired contracts in this directory are coordinator constructions,
not a substitute for either chapter's full theory.
