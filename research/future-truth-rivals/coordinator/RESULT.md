# A narrower obstruction and a stronger rival

27 October 2026. Coordinator synthesis. Bounded research result; original
book reconstruction remains open. No manuscript integration or novelty claim.

## Main conclusion

The past-data obstruction survives, but the proposed refutation of bivalent
growing-block truth does not follow. A truth assignment matching an outcome
that varies between identical past restrictions cannot be a function of
those restrictions alone. A rival can nevertheless reject the claim that
these restrictions exhaust the present truth-relevant facts, or permit
grounds to obtain later. The accessible original reply explicitly engages
both possibilities. POINT_OF_DISAGREEMENT.md isolates the extra premise our
stronger argument would need.

PAIRED_CONTRACTS.md supplies two deliberately simplified interpretations of
one fixed physical case: bivalent outcome matching with a later-ground
bridge, versus current truth as supervaluation over compatible solutions.
Neither interpretation follows from the field equation. Neither is a
reconstruction of the full Correia–Rosenkranz theory. Concrete event domains
do not automatically specify a domain of all facts and propositions.

## What the independent lanes establish

**Generalized histories.** The closed-dust causal relation is exactly
Δτ≥dₛ₃. A chain's spatial coordinate is 1-Lipschitz in conformal time.
An internal lower or upper bound keeps its relevant endpoint away from the
singular boundary; completeness of S³ and continuity of distance give a
unique infimum or supremum. Together with density, absence of endpoints
and a single patch W, this meets Placek's Definition15 order conditions
conditional on nonempty chains (a convention not explicit in its displayed
Definition15). Including empty chains would fail two clauses. A unique global
supremum prevents distinct splitting
pairs, so W is the unique generalized history. The old common-future
obstruction does not refute this existing generalized definition.

This is an order-level compatibility result, not a construction of his
later atlas or tangent spaces. It is a deterministic one-history example,
not a relativistic model of objective branching. The missing common future
of late antipodal events survives; it simply is not required of every pair
within this definition of history.

**Retrospection.** MacFarlane's source clauses keep utterance and assessment
contexts separate. Changing the latter can change an earlier prediction's
assessed status; it does not change its verdict with both indices fixed.
Our ContractS is only a current supervaluation policy. No correspondence
between its solution classes and his branching histories has been proved.
The author's revised 2007/2008 treatment is the next source, rather than
treating his 2003 motivation as his final argument.

**Selection.** A representative of a compatible solution class need not
be the actual realization. A function of past data cannot match every
outcome on a fibre where outcomes vary. Sign reversal alone does not
forbid a canonical representative, since zero is fixed. A free translation
action has no equivariant point selector, but no physical gauge symmetry
has thereby been established. These are elementary conditional distinctions.

## The sharp philosophical task

Test whether there is a non-question-begging reason to require present
truth to supervene on intrinsic existing event structure and laws. Do not
infer that requirement from the nonexistence of future concrete events.
Read the actual rival clauses before arguing that its derivative facts
are circular, secretly eternalist, or incompatible with relativity.

A useful next comparison is temporal asymmetry in grounding: if earlier
facts can explain later truths about the past, what principled condition
would prevent later facts from explaining earlier future truths? Merely
reversing temporal direction is not an argument. Conversely a theory of
past-directed grounds does not establish that future-directed grounds
work. Correia–Merlo's *Cross-temporal grounding*, DOI10.1111/phib.12287,
is a verified abstract-level lead for the past-directed side; its argument
has not been read here. This comparison must preserve operator scope,
factivity, existence domains and the difference between causal and
explanatory direction.
