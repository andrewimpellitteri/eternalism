# Final narrow review: disagreement and later grounding

27 October 2026. Reviewed `research/future-truth-rivals/coordinator/POINT_OF_DISAGREEMENT.md` and the latest later-grounding addendum in `research/future-truth-rivals/coordinator/PAIRED_CONTRACTS.md`, against Correia and Rosenkranz, “Replies to Critics,” §8, printed pp. 482–486.

## Source check and depth

The accessible ResearchGate full-text HTML reproduces the authors' article text, publication data, and printed page markers for *Disputatio* 13(63), 445–494 (2021 issue; ResearchGate lists April 2023), DOI 10.2478/disp-2021-0026. I read the relevant §8 passage and its immediate continuation at printed pp. 482–486, with the key distinction stated on p. 484. This is **selected full-text** reading of the primary authors' reply, not a complete audit of §8's argument chain, the original book's chapter 7, the critics' full paper, or the authors' quantified tense logic. I did not inspect the PDF page images. [Primary text on ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/370321332_Replies_to_Critics).

The key clause is the authors' treatment of a true-now future claim Q and a proposed explanatory sentence R: the fact that Q is true now is the *explanandum-fact* and, ex hypothesi, exists now; R's present truth does not imply that its embedded “because” claim, or its corresponding future explanans-fact, exists now. On their example, the future event/fact is to serve as explanans when it exists later. They further say some facts making up present reality may do so “only courtesy of what will but may not yet exist.” Thus their reply explicitly permits a present truth fact dependent on a later explanans while denying that the later event thereby exists at the earlier standpoint. This is their stated position, not an independently established grounding theory.

## Audit of the alleged equivocation

The coordinator's narrowed disagreement is sound provided “same” continues to mean **same intrinsic classical field data on the stipulated past, with the same fixed laws**, not “same complete current reality” under Correia–Rosenkranz's account. The scalar result shows that the former does not determine the future field outcome. It does not show that all present facts in the rival's ontology are shared: the rival can add a present derivative fact that Q is true now, and that fact can differ between otherwise past-matching alternatives. That is precisely an additional discriminator beyond the intrinsic field past.

Accordingly, there is no equivocation in `POINT_OF_DISAGREEMENT.md` where it explicitly says the solution pair shares intrinsic past data but need not share every present fact, and where it isolates the stronger supervenience principle linking all truths to the intrinsic existing event realm plus laws. Its conclusion is conditional: the pair pressures that principle; it does not establish that the rival must accept it. The criticism would equivocate if it moved from “the present field past fails to fix Q” to “the rival's entire current reality fails to fix Q,” or treated denial of a currently existing future concrete event as denial of the present truth-fact. The source reply blocks those inferences.

The newest addendum in `PAIRED_CONTRACTS.md` makes the same needed distinction: event existence E_b is not an unrestricted domain of facts; a present future-directed truth fact may be admitted while its explanans event is not yet in E_b. This prevents the earlier “same physical base” comparison from being read as already holding all current facts fixed. The controlled comparison holds fixed the mathematical spacetime, field law, intrinsic past restriction, and metamodel realization. Under the rival's enlarged present reality it does **not** hold fixed the derivative truth-fact whose presence is at issue.

## What remains open

The narrow factorization/no-go result still applies to any signature K_b(s)=k(R_b(s)): it cannot reproduce an outcome that varies among models with the same R_b. It does not refute an account whose present facts include a future-directed truth-fact not fixed by R_b. Including that truth-fact in the signature makes outcome truth extensional by stipulation or by the rival's proposed dependence; it does not by itself explain why the fact exists, how the future explanans grounds it, or why this dependence is acceptable. Those are the substantive explanatory and ontological questions, not failures of the same-past calculation.

The prior selection review's abstract consistency example should be read with this refinement: its claim that no **explanatory ground** for Q need be in E_b does not deny a present explanandum fact that Q is true. If “no ground for Q” is read as denying that current truth-fact, it conflates explanandum with explanans and is too strong for the source-backed rival. The primary passage instead permits the explanandum fact now while withholding the future explanans fact now.

No claim is made here that the full Correia–Rosenkranz theory maps without remainder onto Contract L, that its account applies unchanged to the scalar field predicate, or that its full grounding theory has been validated. The cited reply supports this specific distinction; it does not close the project's source gate or decide the ontology.
