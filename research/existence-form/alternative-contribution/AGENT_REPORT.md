## Result in one line

One candidate survives, and it is a short critical note rather than the article
currently drafted: **the totality reductio** — P1\*\* (a) is false of the
universe as a whole, because Brading's own identity of existence with unity
(2015, p. 16) makes the universe a thing that exists, while the region needed to
sustain it is the whole history. Ranked second, as a frame rather than a second
paper: **scale-generality** (existence = unity is scale-free, so no unity
criterion can select a present).

The other four listed candidates were assessed and rejected as contributions:
the constitution/eligibility distinction misreads Brading's 2015 p. 16 identity;
the P/Q/N trilemma is the standard triviality argument plus Savitt/Rovelli/Mason;
the deflationary reading is Rovelli 2019's own stated position; the
parity/favoring account is Balaguer 2026 ch. 9 plus standard Bayesian model
averaging.
