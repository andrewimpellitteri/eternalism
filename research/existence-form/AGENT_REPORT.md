## Result

The form-of-existence trilemma is developed: P-form (predicate presentism) gives
the coverage objection; Q-form (quantifier-restricted presentism) needs a global
present/privileged domain; N-form (no common domain) makes the debate
relational/deflationary. Brading's P1* is P-form; her interaction passage is
N-form; her defeat condition is Q-form. The missing bridge is an eligibility
principle, not merely an "actuality" patch.

## Novelty verdict

- **Modest, source-grounded novelty for the P1\*\* coverage point:** Brading 2015
  p. 17 says "In order to avoid the block universe, all we need is (a)," and
  (a) is local boundedness. The paper's negative result is that (a) does not
  exclude the union of system-presents covering the history. No prior statement
  of this exact point was found.
- **Not novel:** the bare set-theoretic non-implication; system-/frame-relative
  existence (Balaguer 2026 R-presentism; Mason 2025 domain/level point); the
  general deflationary upshot.
- **Not clearly novel:** the P/Q/N trilemma as a general thesis. It should be a
  diagnostic frame, not the headline.
- The paper must cite Mason and Balaguer and avoid a global novelty claim.
