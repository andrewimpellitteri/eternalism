# The form-of-existence trilemma

Sole-worker philosophical analysis, 2026-10-21. Companion to
`NOVELTY_AUDIT.md` and to the P1\*\* retarget recommended by the second referee.

## The issue

The coverage objection says that local boundedness of every system-present
`U_i` does not imply that `union_i U_i` is a proper part of the world history.
That is exact set theory. But the objection assumes a common domain of events
and a distributive existence predicate. A presentist may deny either. The
deeper question is therefore not "does the union cover?" but "what is the
logical form of the existence claim?"

## Three forms

### P-form: predicate presentism

"For every event `e`, `e` exists only if `e` is present."

Brading's P1\* and P1\*\* are P-form: they quantify over things and predicate
existence/presence. P1\*\* adds conditions on the extent of each thing's
present. On this form, the coverage objection applies: if every event is
present in some system's present, the predicate is satisfied by every event;
no global exclusion follows. Local boundedness is a condition on each `U_i`, not
on the union.

### Q-form: quantifier presentism

"The domain of quantification contains only present things."

There is no common domain `W` of all events; the quantifier is restricted. The
coverage objection cannot be stated in the same way, because there is no common
domain to cover. But to restrict the quantifier to "the present," one needs a
global present or a privileged domain. A disjunction of local presents gives
"there is a system `i` such that `e` is in `U_i`" — the coverage condition, not
a restriction. Local presents do not define a single quantifier domain.

### N-form: no common domain

There is no neutral inventory; existence claims are internal to a system or
frame. Presentism and eternalism are not two claims about one domain. The
debate becomes relational or deflationary. This is a substantive
meta-ontological position, but it is not a victory for global presentism; it
gives up the single disagreement.

## Brading's oscillation

- P1\* (2013, p. 1110; 2015, p. 17) is **P-form**.
- The interaction passage — "we belong to the same world as whatever we interact
  with, and the rest of that world is as real as we are"; the son exists
  relative to me, but there is no determinate fact about what he is doing now
  (2013, p. 1110) — is **N-form** or relational.
- The empirical defeat condition — if the only genuine system is the universe,
  "the block universe triumphs" (2013, p. 1109) — is **Q-form**: it is about
  which global system/domain there is.

Brading needs one form. P1\* gives coverage. The interaction passage gives
relational/deflationary existence. The defeat condition presupposes a global
domain. The view is doing three different things at once.

## Constitution versus eligibility

Brading's law-constitutive account explains **unity and persistence**: what
makes a system one thing and how it endures through change. It does not explain
**ontological eligibility**: which unities' presents confer existence on events.
Unity is a property of systems; existence is a property of events or of the
domain. No amount of law-constitutive detail bridges that categorial gap.

This is why "add an actuality premise" is not an ad hoc patch. It is a
different kind of principle: a principle of eligibility, not of unity. The
missing premise is not "add actuality"; it is "specify which unities are
existence-conferring."

## Objections and replies

**The common domain is an eternalist artifact.** The argument can be framed as
a comparison dilemma: if there is no common domain, there is no single debate
(N-form); if there is, the trilemma applies. The presentist cannot have both a
common domain for disagreement and no common domain for existence.

**Existence is not a predicate.** Then P1/P1\* are ill-formed; presentism must be
Q-form or grounding-form. Q-form needs a global present; grounding-form needs a
non-distributive grounding relation. The trilemma remains.

**System-relative existence is still existence.** It is a relational predicate;
whether it deserves the name "existence" is verbal. The substantive global
claim is gone. The paper targets the global claim.

**The union may not cover.** That is a substantive premise (no-coverage), not a
consequence of local boundedness. It is exactly the missing eligibility or
actuality premise. The trilemma says you need it.

**Genuine-systemhood might supply the criterion.** Genuine-systemhood is a unity
criterion, not an existence criterion. A genuine system's present can still
cover events; the union may cover. The criterion does not tell us which systems
are actual or whether their presents exhaust `W`.

**This is a verbal dispute.** Partly. The paper's conclusion is
meta-ontological: the local-present route either changes the subject or needs a
global premise. That is substantive even if the label is verbal.

## Positive alternatives and costs

- **Grounding presentism.** Existence claims are grounded in present tensed
  facts; the domain is not a set of events but a grounding structure. Avoids
  coverage, but needs a logic of ground and a global present or primitive
  presentness. Not derived from Brading's local unity.
- **Process ontology.** Becoming is fundamental; existence is a phase of
  process, not set membership. Different quantifiers/logic; may be compatible
  with local presents but not P1.
- **Priority presentism.** The present is ontologically prior, not a slice; the
  past/future are derivative. Needs a primitive priority relation, not a local
  present.
- **Privileged actual systems / maximal interacting unity.** A dynamical
  selection principle; if the maximal unity is the universe, its present is the
  whole history and the view yields the block. This is a real cost, not a free
  repair.

## Limited verdict

Local presentism faces a trilemma: P-form gives coverage; Q-form needs a global
present/privileged domain; N-form makes the debate relational/deflationary. No
form yields P1 from dynamical unity alone. Brading's P1\* is P-form; her
interaction passage is N-form; her defeat condition is Q-form. The view needs
one form and an eligibility principle.

This is a diagnostic, not a refutation. It does not establish eternalism or
refute Brading. Its force is to show that the global existential restriction is
not a consequence of the local, law-constitutive present.

## Novelty note

The P/Q/N distinction is not clearly novel. Mason 2025 already emphasizes the
domain/level-dependence of "what exists"; Balaguer 2026 develops frame-relative
existence (R-presentism). The defensible novel contribution is narrower: the
P1\*\* coverage point — Brading's own (a) is local boundedness and does not
exclude coverage, so "all we need is (a)" is insufficient. The trilemma should
be presented as a diagnostic frame, not the headline.

## Limits

This is a philosophical analysis, not a new empirical or formal result. It does
not settle the metaphysics; it maps the forms and the costs. Full-text read
depths are in `NOVELTY_AUDIT.md`.
