## Chosen principle

Countable additivity / regular Borel extension as a condition on a
completed-history reading (option (a) of the brief). The note states the
condition precisely, gives the strongest argument for it (countable
completion events; response-stability equivalence; Arageorgis's completion
critique; the selected non-extension theorem), and the strongest argument
against it (finite additivity on an accessible subalgebra; Dirac measures;
algebra choice; no empirical discriminator).

## Verdict

**Known/conditional, not new.** The condition is implicit in the extension
literature and in Arageorgis's critique. Its best form is the equivalence
between bounded variation, uniform response stability and regular extension.
It constrains a completed-history formulation but does not select an ontology.
The decisive question is what event algebra the reading claims to evaluate.
