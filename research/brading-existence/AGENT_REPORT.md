## Result

The core argument for the reframed article is written. Its thesis: Brading's
system-relative present does not establish a restriction on unrestricted
concrete existence. Her P1* is a per-thing/system-relative existence claim, and
her own interaction passage says "the rest of that world is as real as we are."
Local boundedness of each system-present does not imply that the union of
presents is a proper part of the history. The coverage objection is exact.
Recovering the original P1 restriction requires an additional actuality or
privilege premise, or a revision of presentism; each repair has a cost not
settled by the physics.
