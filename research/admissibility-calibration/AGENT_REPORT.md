# Agent report: admissibility-burden-calibration

Sole-worker analysis, 2026-10-21. File: `RESULT.md`, this report.

## Result

The weak/strong dilemma is resolved by replacing a uniform admissibility
checklist with a burden table keyed to inference type:

- contradiction/exclusion: premises, target, logical form, relevance;
- likelihood comparison: common protocol, likelihoods, priors/marginalization,
  decision rule;
- explanatory preference: explanatory virtue, comparison class, rival's best
  version, defeat condition;
- admissibility judgment: constitutive commitments, permitted revisions,
  eligibility evidence, exclusion rule.

The paper's methodological claim becomes conditional: specify what the
inference requires, no more and no less. The strong demand is defensible for
favoring/exclusion claims, not for every argument.

The note also separates background eligibility evidence `D_0` from
discriminating evidence `D_1`, states the filter-cost warning, and gives five
conditions for principled revision. It tests the calibration on Brading (an
admissibility/explanatory case) and LAGEOS (a parameter constraint).

## Limits

- The burden table is a proposal, not a derived theorem.
- It does not settle which explanatory virtues are legitimate; it requires them
  to be stated and defended.
- It makes admissibility disagreements inspectable rather than deciding them.
- It does not by itself produce a favoring verdict; that is the separate
  `favoring-account` task.
