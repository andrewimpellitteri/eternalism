## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Independent proof review reconstructs the argument and attempts a counterexample before comparison with the author's numerical checks.
- [ ] Prove actual loss of determination: the same field equation's two admissible solutions agree on the entire prescribed past but differ at a massless-fixed interior timelike-future target. Failure of an echo identity alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Quantifiers fix the data and target before the small positive mass interval; separate geometric guarantee from the larger law-relative fixed set.
- [ ] Check the resolvent normalization/positivity, period derivative, Duhamel sign, exceptional homogeneous-mode coefficient and target's interior location.
- [ ] Distinguish constant potential on the Einstein cylinder from ordinary constant physical mass on dust. Check the time-dependent rescaled potential and auxiliary endpoint extension.
- [ ] Do not infer exact past vanishing from finite spectral truncations. Check convergence/controls, but retain analytical support and nonvanishing proofs.
- [ ] State exact versus approximate determination: small solution changes are not an observed discontinuity, and error bounds need a norm/energy class.
- [ ] Restrict claims to linear classical fields on a fixed background; no interacting QFT, QCD, backreaction or temporal ontology inference.
- [ ] Primary predecessor/priority audit and final paper-scope decision recorded; integrate only a justified concise conclusion or defer the extension.
