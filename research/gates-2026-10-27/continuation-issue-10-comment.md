It supports choosing one fixed interior future target first, followed by an event-dependent interval of positive masses for which exact fixing is lost. This is conditional on the massless fixing theorem and limited to a classical scalar on a fixed background; no complete massive fixing set or uniform threshold follows. [Review](https://github.com/andrewimpellitteri/eternalism/blob/594827298e5d37103a7312ffa153960a925972a9/research/horizons/native/mass-final-review/REVIEW.md).

Exact loss therefore does not itself establish practical unpredictability.
