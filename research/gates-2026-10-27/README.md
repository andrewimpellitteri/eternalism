   merely because it was generated. #10 must pass before inclusion.

   observable; its alternatives are not ignorance or semantic unsettledness.
3. Determinist growing-block and eternalist replies must be stated at their
   strongest. Results constrain bridge inferences rather than automatically
   select an ontology.

   clock-equipped category and keep off-shell/on-shell restrictions visible.
5. Advocating local fragmentalism requires an identifiable explanatory or
   grounding contribution. A conditional mathematical paper can instead
