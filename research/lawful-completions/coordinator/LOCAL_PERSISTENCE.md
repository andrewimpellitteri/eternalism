# Local bodily persistence does not by itself select the whole elsewhere

2026-10-28. Bounded implication test for the published persistence motivation, not a refutation of the authors' preferred ontology. Primary source: chapter9 pp151--152 and Replies pp486--488, selectively reread.

## A local-thickness lemma

Let s have a chronological future point z. Then N=I^-(z) is an open neighborhood of s. Every x in N that is neither s nor in the strict causal future of s shares the strict chronological future z with s. Hence

       N intersect D_BT(s) subset D(s) subset H(s).

In particular all sufficiently nearby spacelike locations are admitted by H(s), as are all past points. This is a local statement requiring neither global future directedness nor admission of every remote spacelike point.

## What follows for the persistence objection

The authors' persistence objection asks for several distinct current locations for limbs, outside the causal past of the here-now, rather than collapsing all such locations to the here-now. H can supply arbitrarily many distinct nearby spacelike points, and the objects at those points may have past stages in the observer's causal past. Thus lack of all remote elsewhere points is not itself a failure of the local location resources demanded by that objection.

A concrete witness uses the dust--Lambda FLRW model in EXACT_DUST_LAMBDA.md. At a central standpoint s of conformal time sigma, choose finitely many small nonzero comoving vectors z_i with |z_i|<min(sigma,2(L-sigma)). Comoving timelike bodies at these locations have signal-emission events p_i at conformal time sigma-|z_i| in s's causal past, and later stages q_i at conformal time sigma, spacelike to s. The null rays from p_i reach s. The q_i share a common future with s because |z_i|<2(L-sigma), so q_i belong to D(s) subset H(s). Also p_i prec q_i along the bodies' worldlines. The same bodies therefore have both observed past locations and current non-past locations available under H, as the reply's second persistence definition requests.

These are ideal test bodies/worldlines in the exact background, not an added self-gravitating biological apparatus. The argument concerns available event locations. It does not prove that all real bodies have their required worldtubes in H or settle a theory of material persistence.

At the same s, sufficiently remote spacelike events are excluded from H by DIAMETER_CLASSIFICATION.md. Thus the causal-potential and local-persistence motivations can jointly be respected in this limited sense by a realm smaller than the full bow tie. This blocks an inference from these two motivations ALONE to universal (BO), unless a further premise establishes the status of remote points.

## Scope

The alternative H is our historical-potential repair, not the authors' theory. It depends on global causal information and is not selected by the local lemma. The result is not a complete metaphysical rival and does not show that the authors intended the local-persistence argument as a deductive proof of (BO). It identifies the limited reach of that argument: local thickness is weaker than all-elsewhere existence.
