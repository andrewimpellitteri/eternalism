# Lawful completions: ambitious candidate and first kill tests

28 October 2026. Research target, not an established theorem or novelty claim.
Coordinator owns this file. Workers use only their assigned subdirectories.

## Shared question

For a specified coupled Einstein–matter system, classify which relational
future outcomes are fixed by a pointed observer's complete causal past.
Geometry, matter and the observer must be compared under the same laws.
The scalar-on-fixed-dust theorem is background, not a coupled construction.

Represent a model by (M,g,fields,b,u), with marked event b and unit future
velocity u. Compare models using an isometry of their ENTIRE I^-(b),
preserving matter fields and whatever observer germs the outcome needs.
Decide explicitly whether matching extends to b and null boundary J^-(b).
Do not infer this extension from an open-past isometry without hypotheses.
Do not use coordinates to identify future targets across models.

Candidate outcome: a field or curvature observable along the geodesic
launched from (b,u), evaluated at a fixed proper time T, before caustics or
singularities. This is relational but may represent an ideal probe, not a
self-gravitating detector. State that distinction. Detector dynamics are
optional only if a separate physical experiment is claimed.

## Hierarchy, not interchangeable targets

A. One pair of lawful models, same past, different relational future.
B. Such pairs in a fixed matter theory and common Cauchy topology, with
   maximal globally hyperbolic developments and justified matching of full
   pasts, not only a finite diamond.
C. Local deformation/control of future observables, with asymptotic charges
   or other global data held fixed where meaningful; exact hypotheses on
   genericity, energy, smoothness, and norm of smallness.
D. A classification of fixed observables or an exact obstruction in a named
   family. Propose only after A–C feasibility and priority audit.

A may be elementary and already known. Do not inflate A into C or D.
Spherical null-dust/Vaidya incoming pulses are an explicit baseline/kill
candidate, not the desired final novelty claim. Einstein–massive-scalar
and vacuum gravitational-wave alternatives are candidate harder families;
no choice of family is accepted until the source/constraint audit.

## Critical traps

- Equality on a Cauchy patch gives agreement in its domain of dependence;
  it need not give the whole past of b in maximal developments.
- Local isometry of metrics alone is not preservation of matter/observer data.
- Coordinate changes are not physically different outcomes.
- Arbitrary cut-and-paste metrics need not satisfy one fixed matter model.
- Constraint solutions plus DEC do not necessarily realize a chosen matter
  field or provide evolution/existence/uniqueness for it.
- Initial-data gluing, null gluing and past-preserving spacetime gluing are
  different assertions with different boundaries and conserved charges.
- A Cauchy-complete agreed region can force full agreement by uniqueness.
- Exact equality, finite-precision similarity and stability within a
  matched-past family are distinct requirements. Specify relative topology.
