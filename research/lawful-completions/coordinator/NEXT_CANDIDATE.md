# Beyond one pair: candidate finite future control at fixed incoming energy

28 October 2026. Conjectural next target, conditional on the scattering-source
audit. This is not a result or priority verdict.

The collapse side-test and the main lawful-completion question must remain
separate. Black-hole formation is useful for defeating a common-future
rationale. A different future scalar reading needs no black hole. Smooth
weak incoming radiation in a globally complete Einstein–scalar scattering
solution may already give the main A/B baseline with an exactly flat early
observer past. Such a baseline would likely be prior art/corollary, not a
new result.

A sharper C target: within a fixed smooth spherical Einstein–massless-scalar
scattering class, fix the entire pointed flat past of b, fix total incoming
Bondi/ADM energy in its verified source normalization, and choose proper
times T₁<...<T_N on the central timelike geodesic. Can a neighborhood of a
baseline vector of scalar readings (φ(T₁),...,φ(T_N)) be attained while
keeping those past and charge conditions EXACT?

Proposed mechanism, all still to prove:
1. Source theorem for smooth complete scattering solutions from compact
   incoming radiation profiles, with exactly flat pre-pulse region.
2. A differentiable solution-to-reading map near a suitable small profile.
   At the zero-field flat limit the radial linear wave has the form
   φ=[f(v)−f(u)]/r, r=(v−u)/2, so its regular centre limit is 2f′(t).
   Distinct-time finite evaluations therefore have independent compact
   profile variations. Check the paper's precise radiation normalization.
3. Use N early profile directions to vary the N readings. A separate late
   pulse, causally after all readings, adjusts total incoming energy without
   changing the earlier readings or the marked past. Nonzero baseline tail
   gives a nondegenerate derivative for this energy adjustment, unlike the
   zero-energy base where energy is quadratic.
4. A finite-dimensional implicit-function argument could then give exact
   nonlinear control, PROVIDED source theorem and differentiability plus
   response Jacobian are established in compatible function spaces.

The geodesic's proper-time parametrization and its variation are part of
that response map; coordinate-time interpolation is insufficient. The
common pointed past must be identified, not merely approximately flat.
The charge relation at past null infinity must be derived from the exact
Einstein equations; do not substitute a linearized energy formula.

A signed scalar reading is coordinate-invariant but not invariant under
an imposed internal sign quotient; choose the ordinary real-scalar theory
with its physical global sign symmetry, or use suitable observer-energy
readings near a nonzero baseline and recheck rank. An ideal-probe functional
still is not an actual self-gravitating detector model.

Strong defeaters: the exact control statement is already in scattering or
inverse-problem literature; no compatible smooth dependence theorem exists
at the needed boundary; fixed-energy and exact-past restrictions make the
response rank deficient; or the construction is only an immediate standard
corollary with no substantive extension. Audit these before claiming novelty.
