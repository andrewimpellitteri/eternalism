## Primary philosophical target

Classify when the reconstructed causal-potential justification is compatible with persistence and when its natural persistent repair equals the intended bow-tie realm.

Already reviewed: on connected causally simple spacetimes the instantaneous point-level domain is persistent iff every pair shares a future. Thus horizons defeat that reconstruction's persistence in a precise class.

Newly derived, under independent review: in a flat finite conformal slab (a,B)xR^d, the least persistent enlargement H includes a spacelike x at s exactly when distance < 2(B-a)+eta_s-eta_x. It generally differs from the full bow tie. EXACT_DUST_LAMBDA.md realizes the difference in an exact fixed Einstein--dust--positive-Lambda model, without an empirical fit or collapse extension.

This gives three distinguishable positions:

1. Instantaneous potential: preserves the intended causal reading but can lose events as horizons develop.
2. Historical potential: preserves existence through time but does not always include the entire spacelike elsewhere.
3. Full bow tie: preserves growth and all elsewhere events, but cannot in general be derived from either potential rule alone.

These are properties of the explicit point-level definitions. The authors' location clause and composition assumptions may yield a fourth option; they are not disproved by the three-way comparison. A full criticism must inspect that clause rather than declare the options exhaustive.

## Philosophical consequence worth defending

The independently motivated existence criterion is the real issue. Determinism can make future outcomes fixed by a currently existing realm without making future events currently exist. Conversely, failure of prediction from a local past does not show absence of present exterior grounds. Neither implication should be smuggled into the mathematics.

The sharper question is whether a proposed causal motivation selects one invariant, persistent event realm across realistic curved spacetime models, or whether extra global/composition commitments do the selection. Our classification can expose those extra commitments and their consequences while preserving the rival's consistency.
