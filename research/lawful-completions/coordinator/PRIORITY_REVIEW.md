This review concerns implication/scope, not a re-audit of every citation.

1. CONTRACT B fixes a pointed entire observer past and Cauchy TOPOLOGY. It
   does not fix complete Cauchy DATA. Unique MGHD for one full initial-data
   set therefore does not kill B: two admissible models may have different
   data outside the shared past and different MGHDs. No need to cross a
   Cauchy horizon or construct two extensions of one common MGHD. The
   submission's move to Doboszewski's nonunique-extension problem changes
   the target and is not an established equivalence.
2. Failure to extend an isometric embedding of J⁻(b)∪J⁻(p) establishes a
   global prediction obstruction. It does not by itself give different
   values of a specified scalar/tidal functional at proper time T along
   two existing marked geodesics. One might lose an endpoint, encounter
   topology, or retain all chosen local measurements. The source result
   is a strong prior for broad same-past/different-future claims, but a
   direct-corollary claim for CONTRACT A's specific relational observable
   needs an additional demonstration. 'Different future' and 'different Q'
   cannot silently be identified.
3. Local-isometry preservation of local equations is relevant, while global
   hyperbolicity, smooth full-past marking, fixed charge data and topology
   remain separate. The source survey explicitly records many of these
   limits. Keep them attached when using its findings.

Disposition: retain the prior-art warning and primary locators. Hold the
claimed KILL for the exact relational target and the asserted coincidence
with the shared-MGHD extension question. No claim of novelty follows from
this correction; the harder target can still be known by other methods.
