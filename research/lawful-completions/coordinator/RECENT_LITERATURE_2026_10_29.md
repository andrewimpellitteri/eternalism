## Directly on our solution class

1. **Luk–Oh–Yang is published:** Ann. PDE 4:3, Art. 3 (2018), matching
   arXiv:1605.03893 — confirmed via Oh's publication list and ORCID this
   session. This partially retires the source-lane caveat "v1 is the only

2. **Kilgore, "Global Non-Linearly Stable Large-Data Solutions to the
   Einstein Scalar Field System", arXiv:2108.13377 (v1, 2021), published
   Ann. Henri Poincaré, DOI 10.1007/s00023-022-01162-4.** Abstract
   (fetched this session; earlier lane fetched abstract + §1): new bounds
   controlling **higher derivatives of both the metric components and the
   scalar field** for the same global SSESF class our limits belong to,
   plus a **generalized wave-coordinate construction** feeding Luk–Oh's
   stability theorem. Implication for the AXIS gates: (a) if Kilgore's
   derivative bounds are axis-inclusive, they could replace or
   strengthen the O7/§4-prose inputs for the reconstruction at higher
   order (the lift review had flagged Kilgore as cone-future-focused;
   the abstract says the bounds are for the global solutions — this
   discrepancy needs a full-text theorem-statement read); (b) the wave-
   coordinate gauge is a candidate regular gauge in which our C¹,¹
   limit's coefficients might be re-expressed — relevant to any future
   scattering-map differentiability work. Next task: full-text read of
   Kilgore's main theorems and their domain/uniformity statements,
   checking axis-inclusiveness term by term against our §1 input list.
3. **Luk–Oh, "Global nonlinear stability of large dispersive solutions to
   the Einstein equations", arXiv:2108.13379 (v2, 2021), published Ann.
   Henri Poincaré 23:2391–2521 (2022), DOI 10.1007/s00023-021-01148-8.**
   Abstract fetched this session: any regular, future causally geodesically
   complete, sufficiently decaying asymptotically flat ESF solution is
   future globally nonlinearly stable; combining with LOY and Kilgore
   gives an open set of large asymptotically flat data with complete
   solutions. Implication for the smooth-common-development gate: a
   smooth-category ambient stability theory exists around exactly our
   backgrounds. The gate stays open — our BV subsequential limits are not
   yet shown to be such backgrounds with regular data — but the named
   attack route is now: realize the scattering-family limits as the
   "unperturbed" solutions of Luk–Oh's theorem and read off the
   development of smooth perturbations, rather than building a
   field-preserving development theorem from scratch.

## Recent SSESF low-regularity results (context for the gates)

4. **arXiv:2605.16235 (May 2026), "Nonlinear stability of continuously
   self-similar naked singularities for the Einstein-scalar field
   equations I"** — abstract: naked-singularity family with initial data
   of finite Hölder regularity C^{1,k²/(1−k²)}; BV perturbations; MGHD
   of perturbed data develops black hole regions. Shows contemporary
   MGHD statements at finite regularity in exactly the spherical ESF
   setting; background for how far below C¹,¹ the field currently works.
5. **arXiv:2610.20468 (Oct 2026), "A Scale-Critical μ<3/8 Extension
   Principle for Spherically Symmetric Einstein-Scalar Field System"** —
   abstract: quantitative extension across a possible singular endpoint
   under μ<3/8, improving Christodoulou's threshold via scale-critical
   pointwise estimates, self-similar-domain refinements, and
   higher-order Morawetz identities; BV norm smallness feeds
   Christodoulou's BV extension. Relevant to any future pointwise/central
   extension work and to the curvature-outcomes gate.
6. **arXiv:2509.14110 (Sep 2025), CMC-gauge local existence for
   Einstein–nonlinear scalar field** — local existence, uniqueness,
   continuation criterion, Cauchy stability in CMC gauges on closed
   3-manifolds. Adjacent toolkit; not directly our double-null/BV
   setting. Also noted: CR Mécanique 2025 survey "On the regularity
   problems of Einstein equations" for mapping low-regularity LWP.
