## Most useful next target

Basic same-past/different-future is likely a consequence of existing
scattering theory. It is baseline evidence, not an ambitious novelty claim.
NEXT_CANDIDATE.md proposes a stronger, falsifiable control question: can a
finite vector of future central readings vary independently at fixed total
incoming energy and identical entire pointed past?

Required exact steps: (1) source regularity/uniqueness of the selected global
scattering map; (2) its differentiability and response rank in a suitable
profile space, including proper-time variation; (3) exact incoming-energy
functional and a causally later adjustment preserving earlier readings.
Each can fail or already be known. No inverse-function theorem may be used
until (1)–(3) are justified. Smooth scalar collapse and globally weak
scattering remain different model families.
