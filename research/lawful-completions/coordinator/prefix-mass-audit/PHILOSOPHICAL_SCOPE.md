# What matching finite incoming-energy records would establish

31 October 2026. Consequences of the conditional candidate in [RESULT.md](RESULT.md).
This is a source-conditional model comparison, not an accepted ontology result.

## 1. Exact determination claim

For a model M in the stated spherical/observer class let D(M) contain:

    the pointed field-preserving isometry class of J-minus(b),
    total incoming boundary mass E,
    the finite list (T_j,P_j),

where P_j is evaluated on the radial null cut ending at gamma(T_j). D is a
model-comparison contract. The mass entries at future cuts are not asserted to
be records an observer already possesses at b.

If the candidate is verified, then at fixed finite schedule, fixed strictly
increasing fractions alpha and each sufficiently small E, all models realizing
the small scalar target ball have the same D. Thus the scalar reading vector
cannot factor as a single-valued function of D in this model class. This is the
exact past-plus-mass non-determination statement; it adds finite timing data to
the earlier total-mass-only comparison.

## 2. Remove a possible global scalar-sign artifact

There is a cheap objection to using a single scalar sign: for the massless
scalar equations, phi->-phi preserves the metric equations and the mass.
A constant scalar shift also leaves the derivative equations invariant.
These are mathematical internal transformations; this note does not declare
them gauge or decide which detectors distinguish them.

Use instead centered readings and their products:

    Delta_j=phi(gamma(T_j))-phi(b),
    R_j=Delta_1 Delta_j,       2<=j<=N.

Under a global change phi->s phi+C, s in {-1,+1}, Delta_j->s Delta_j and R_j is
unchanged. The use of centered readings removes the constant shift as well.
No detector model or bare-metric completeness claim is being inferred.

For N>=2, set d=c sqrt(E)/(2 sqrt(N)). The candidate realizes every target

    Delta_j=d sigma_j,       sigma_j in {-1,+1},

at the same D, because its norm is c sqrt(E)/2. Then

    R_j=d^2 sigma_1 sigma_j.

Fixing sigma_1=+1 leaves all 2^(N-1) sign patterns of the product vector.
Consequently the variation does not reduce to a single overall scalar-sign
choice. Models with different product vectors cannot be related by a pointed,
observer-preserving field isometry even when one also permits the global
transformations phi->+phi+C or phi->-phi+C. This is a marked field-model
statement, not a count of unmarked bare-metric isometry classes.

This elementary corollary uses the proposed prefix theorem; it is not a
separate novelty claim. Its constants and resolution can shrink with N and the
fraction gaps. N=1 supplies no such product comparison.

## 3. Which philosophical inference remains unavailable

The result would show that a finite cumulative incoming-mass record does not
supply all the information needed to determine those future field relations,
even when it agrees at every sampled proper-time cut. It leaves incoming wave
shapes, other field data and complete spacelike Cauchy data free to differ.
No violation of deterministic Cauchy evolution follows.

It does not match every present fact admitted by a later-grounding truth theory,
identify a bow-tie realm with the causal past, or establish that the realizable
alternatives are all currently concrete. Eternalist, growing and other inventory
readings can still interpret the selected physical models differently. The
construction alone supplies no independently warranted upper bound on existence.

A claim about an entire incoming mass function or infinitely many readout cuts
would need a new argument with the correct parameterization and uniformity.
The finite theorem cannot be extended by interchanging the finite-schedule and
smallness quantifiers. The physically stronger finite comparison is worth
checking, but it is not a substitute for an ontological bridge or its defense.
