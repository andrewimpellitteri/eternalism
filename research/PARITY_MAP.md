# Parity map: how far the repo is from the field of time

31 October 2026. Claude Code (Opus 5.5), at the user's request. A working assessment, not a
verdict on the project or a claim of expertise. The named authors are leads from memory and from
LITERATURE_AUDIT.md, to be verified before citing. Hit counts are crude: surnames collide, and a
mention is not understanding.

## What the goal can mean

**Totality is not an achievable target.** The field has unresolved disputes, so there is no complete
understanding to hold. Files are also not understanding: the test is what can be done with them.

**Parity with the best human can be made testable.** For a domain, parity means all five hold.

1. **State:** give each main position in a form its own defender would accept.
2. **Reconstruct:** derive the canonical arguments from primary sources at full-text depth.
3. **Locate:** name the live open problems and what would count as progress.
4. **Survive:** withstand attack by a reader who did not write the note.
5. **Contribute:** produce something a named expert would have reason to revise or cite.

Tests 1 to 4 can be run in this repo. Test 5, and any honest claim of parity, needs outside experts.
No model review can stand in for that, and no expert has read any of this work yet.

So the repo is near the front on one question: what relativistic physics does and does not fix. On the
whole subject of time it is not near parity. A leading philosopher of time would ask first where the
direction of time comes from, and the paper's answer is that it takes it as given.

## Order of work

Each step ends in a note that names a rival and what it must revise, under BREAKTHROUGH_TEST.md.
Reading without that output does not count.

1. **Thermodynamic arrow.** Boltzmann, Reichenbach, Albert, Loewer, Price, Earman on the past hypothesis,
   Wallace, Carroll. Question: is the time orientation that Part I takes as input physical, and does
   the past-directed fixing asymmetry of Parts II and III depend on it? This is also the largest
   hole in *What the Past Can Fix*.
2. **Direction of causation.** Reichenbach, Salmon, Dowe, Woodward, Price, Kutach. Question: does the
   growing-block appeal to production survive?
3. **Temporal experience.** Husserl, Dainton, Phillips, Prosser, Hoerl, Callender. Question: does
   experience give any reason for a primitive index?
4. **Problem of time.** Kuchař, Isham, Barbour, Rovelli, Thébault, Gryb, Huggett and Wüthrich. Question:
   do the becoming and fixing relations survive if time is not fundamental?
5. **Aspect and language**, then **persistence**, **agency**, **history**, **value**, as LITERATURE_AUDIT.md
   orders them.

After each step, run tests 1 to 4 and change the level above only on that evidence.

The domain list and levels are my judgment, and a blind spot could sit outside this list. The earlier audit
showed that keyword scans can undersell or oversell coverage in both directions. Levels are not measures of
quality, and a 4 means bounded internal results, not field-level standing.
