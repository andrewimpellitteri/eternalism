## 1. The selected mathematical result

**Established (internally reviewed, narrowly novel).** For complex transitive
percolation with
`A_p(C) = p^{L(C)} (1-p)^{binom(|C|,2)-R(C)}` on the sigma-algebra generated by
naturally labelled **doubly-principal** cylinders, a finite countably additive
complex measure with those prescribed values exists **iff `p` is in `[0,1]`**.

- Proof: two universal caps reduce a doubly-principal `(k+2)`-order to
  `G ⊕ {u} ⊕ {v}`; the absolute level sum is bounded below by
  `|p| (|p|+|1-p|)^k c_s`, which diverges outside `[0,1]`; the real-segment
  construction supplies the converse.
- Artifacts: `research/paper-novel/main.tex`, `CLAIM_LEDGER.md`,
  `research/adversarial-review/AGENT_REPORT.md`,
  `research/novelty-verdict/AGENT_REPORT.md`.
- Finite corroboration: `research/paper-novel/reproduce.py` (six pinned
  hashes; 407 exact identities; 26/26 bounded checks).
- Priority delta: Dowker–Zalel 2023 and Zalel's 2021 thesis explicitly leave
  the valency-one/doubly-principal term to future work. This supplies that
  term. It is **not** a new general extension criterion, and the elementary
  estimate may reasonably be called a short computation.

**Conditional companion.** Under the all-principal common-factor rule `(T)`,
normalization on infinitely many posts, and finite complex countable
additivity, a covariant extension exists iff `0 < p <= 1`. Covariance alone
does not imply `(T)`; the doubly-principal-only weakening has an infinite-block
Dirac countermodel with zero factor. Artifact: `main.tex`, Theorem
`thm:cov`, and `research/creative/COVARIANT_RESULT.md`.

## 3. What physics supports

- Special relativity's kinematics and the causal-set programme's stated
  covariance constraints support the *mathematical* setup: a labelled growth
  law, a covariance condition on unlabelled events, and a completed-history
  extension question.
- The causal-set literature supplies the growth dynamics, the post-probability
  identity, and the bounded-variation framework used here. Those are prior
  results, not this project's discoveries.
- The project's finite models show that specific mathematical hopes fail:
  scalar premeasures on the selected algebra do not always extend; a
  common-factor covariant rule does not follow from covariance alone;
  norm-only vector realizations can change interference; Bell causality does
  not by itself kill scalar phase holonomy; and a finite clock's relational
  dynamics depends on how the constraint is written.

## 4. What is excluded, and under which premises

These are mathematical exclusions, not empirical refutations:

1. For `p` outside `[0,1]`, no finite countably additive complex measure on the
   doubly-principal labelled sigma-algebra matches the prescribed amplitudes.
   This does not exclude a measure with different values, a different algebra,
   a different summation rule, or a quantum measure.
2. Under `(T)` plus normalization on infinitely many posts, no covariant
   extension exists outside `0 < p <= 1`. Dropping `(T)` admits the Dirac
   countermodel, which is not a dynamical extension of the amplitudes.
3. Norm-only Hilbert/Banach realizations do not preserve the original
   off-diagonal (interference) data; they are counterexamples to a norm-only
   uniqueness reading, not new physical dynamics.
4. A scalar Bell-causal product rule with MSR and norm-GC does not force
   trivial holonomy, even with all transitions nonzero, for the fixed seed.
   This does not exclude a no-go under operator QBC or for all seeds.
5. A `Z_3` group-action formulation with integer-gap closure can still fail
   local readout covariance once the interaction is on. Closure and
   commutation with the clock Hamiltonian are not sufficient.

## 5. What cannot be decided

- **Temporal ontology.** Nothing here selects eternalism, presentism, growing
  block, or a relational becoming. Measure existence, covariance and
  non-extension are mathematical properties; an ontology needs bridge
  premises that are not derived from them.
- **Which bridge principle is correct.** The article's burden-of-proof and
  admissibility framework is a philosophical proposal; revision-2 review
  corrected overclaims but did not establish a unique bridge. Stein's printed
  theorem wording and a full Putnam formal reconstruction remain open.
- **Physical realizability of the control/instrument class.** The operational
  stability and event-filter results are conditional on an accessible control
  or coherent erasure model. No embedded causal-set observer is constructed.
- **Rank-only post values.** The weighted estimate is now proved under the
  imported posts-algebra/two-cap frame, giving a conditional no-go outside
  `0<p<=1`. An independent audit of that frame and of the two-cap bound is
  still required; no paper claim depends on it.
- **Empirical discrimination.** The 21-candidate audit in
  `research/discriminators-audit/RESULT.md` finds no genuine ontology
  discriminator without an independently defended exclusivity premise. The
  LAGEOS/preferred-frame and clock examples constrain parameters or
  formulations, not ontology. This is a structural result, not a failure of
  experimental imagination.

## 6. Falsifiers and what would change the verdict

- A finite countably additive complex measure on the doubly-principal
  sigma-algebra matching `A_p` for some `p` outside `[0,1]` would falsify the
  selected theorem.
- A covariant extension outside `0<p<=1` satisfying `(T)` and the stated
  normalization would falsify the conditional companion.
- A derivation of the all-principal common factor from covariance alone would
  remove the main condition.
- A physical event filter or internal observer that accesses the divergent
  witness at finite cost, with a covariant channel, would change the
  operational-stability/event-filter verdict from conditional to physical.
- An empirical protocol with matched models and different likelihoods under
  the two ontological readings would break the underdetermination claim.
- A printed-source check contradicting the p. 149 Stein wording or condition
  (iv) would reopen the source correspondence; the gate is otherwise closed.

## 7. Failure to discriminate, stated plainly

The project has not produced a physical prediction that separates eternalism
from its rivals, a derivation of a bridge principle from physics, an
unconditional rank-only classification (the present result is frame-conditional),
a general operator-QBC phase theorem, or an embedded-observer realization. The discriminator audit shows why the first is not just missing
but structurally blocked without an exclusivity premise; the philosophy essay
in `research/philosophy-completion/ESSAY.md` states the completion/access
dilemma that remains. Those are open, not hidden. The verified floor is the narrow
scalar classification and the conditional covariant companion; the rest is
either known mathematics, a scoped countermodel, or a philosophical proposal.
