## Assessment

This is known functional-analysis machinery made explicit for the research
question. It offers a quantitative reformulation and a cleaner bridge premise;
no mathematical novelty or physical discriminator is claimed. Prior primary
passages inspected: Surya–Zalel 2003.11311v1 Theorem 2.1 and Appendix B;
Dowker–Johnston–Surya 1007.2725v1 Claims 1–2. The Riesz representation theorem
is invoked as standard mathematics rather than independently re-proved.

The binary example is separate from causal-set percolation. Application to
prescribed doubly-principal cylinders is conditional on their finite algebra
being consistent and on the selected variation lower bound. Arbitrary
subalgebra Stone spaces must not silently be identified with physical histories.
