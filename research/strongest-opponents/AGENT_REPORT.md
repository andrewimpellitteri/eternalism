## Result

Saunders and Pooley are reconstructed at their strongest.

- Saunders: presentism needs an invariant, definable, non-trivial equivalence
  relation of co-presence; the definability results leave no such relation.
  His paraphrase of Clifton–Hogarth is broader than the theorem; the narrower
  equivalence-relation reading is used.
- Pooley: the two easy routes are deflationary passage (not serious) and a
  privileged global present (not relativistic); the hard route eschews a global
  Now and links passage to the open future. Brading's local present is a
  candidate, but the coverage objection pushes it toward the deflationary horn
  unless an actuality/privilege premise is added.

The reframed paper's limited reply: it does not vindicate robust passage; it
shows that the local-present route does not by itself restore the presentist
existential restriction.
