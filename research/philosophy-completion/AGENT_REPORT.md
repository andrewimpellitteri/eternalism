# Agent report: completion-access-dilemma

Sole-worker essay, 2026-10-21. Files: `ESSAY.md`, this report.

## Thesis

The non-extension theorem for complex percolation outside `[0,1]` does not
refute growing-block metaphysics. It turns Earman's "ideal completion" into a
conditional and exposes a dilemma: a reading that claims a unique global
completed history with the prescribed scalar amplitudes is excluded for that
model class; a reading that retreats to finite, local or system-relative
becoming can survive, but it gives up the global completion claim and incurs
the costs listed in the essay. The choice is a bridge premise, not a
mathematical consequence.

## Verdict and limits

The essay is a philosophical argument, not a new theorem or ontology. It does
not claim that Earman, Arageorgis, Wüthrich–Callender or Brading would accept
the dilemma as formulated. The mathematical claims are the project's scoped
results. Brading's position is engaged through the source records rather than a
fresh page-by-page read; that is the main read-depth limitation. No novelty
claim beyond the formulation of the dilemma.
