## Dependency map

1. **Causal geometry:** global hyperbolicity, time orientation and stated causal conventions support guarantee, potential, persistence and screening results.
2. **Named dynamics:** a fixed operator gives the test-field theorem; ordinary smooth vacuum common-development uniqueness gives the geometric-readout theorem. Neither automatically supplies coupled low-regularity scalar uniqueness.
3. **Observable and comparison:** a marked germ and proper time identify readouts; an abstract re-marking is not independent dynamical control; a fixed common manifold is not an abstract isometry-extension theorem.
4. **Existence extent:** common-duration agreement is weaker than equal maximal lifetime. Completeness or a separately proved maximal-development argument belongs here.
5. **Interpretation:** a physical realm need not equal an observer's accessible records, and truth need not be identified with settlement. No theorem silently supplies those bridges.
