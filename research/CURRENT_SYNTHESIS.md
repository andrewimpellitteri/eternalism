## The unifying question and present answer

Which mathematical or physical structures fix a specified outcome, and what additional premise would turn that fixation into a claim about existence, truth or becoming?

The research repeatedly finds a useful mathematical boundary and a separate interpretive boundary. A measure can fail to extend without disproving growing-block metaphysics. An abstract observer circuit can exist without an embedded observer being able to implement it. A locality theorem can constrain a becoming relation without establishing that becoming is fundamental. A field can be fixed without its future events existing. Keeping those boundaries separate is the common result of the programme, rather than an unresolved inconsistency between its branches.

The new screening repair adds a precise distinction: matching the point-level potential realm can fix geometric readings under ordinary common-development uniqueness, even when equal survival duration and equality of the entire futures remain unproved. Completeness permits a stronger result under an explicit extra premise.

## 1. Completion of growth histories is a mathematical question

The causal-set result classifies extension of the prescribed complex transitive-percolation values on the labelled doubly-principal sigma-algebra: finite countably additive complex extension exists exactly for p in [0,1]. The covariant companion requires an all-principal common-factor value rule and normalization on infinite-post histories, giving the separately stated interval 0<p<=1. Covariance alone does not supply that value rule. Norm-only vector realizations can change phase/interference data and therefore do not rescue the original scalar premeasure unchanged.

These are the internally reviewed mathematical floor, with a narrow internally assessed priority delta and external review/priority limits preserved. They do not identify a completed-history representation with an eternalist ontology, or a nonextension theorem with a refutation of becoming. The completion/access dilemma is conditional on those bridge identifications.

## 2. Mathematical access is not physical observer access

Finite instrument, growth-register, coherent-erasure and resource results specify their control classes. The actual matched causal-set witness shows that the same observer-past record can accompany different global precursor alphabets, obstructing one record-only controller from preparing both prescribed global outputs. Full orthogonal history storage and the stated precision schedules carry architecture-specific resource costs. These are not universal hardware or observer bounds.

The clock work likewise separates constraint formulations, normalized postselection, channels, multi-time records and memory criteria. A finite calculation or one-channel comparison is not an all-state or intervention equivalence theorem. None of these results is an empirical discriminator of temporal ontology without an independently defended exclusivity premise.

Connection to the new work: identical accessible records, identical intrinsic causal pasts and identical whole existing realms are THREE different comparison contracts. A failure under the first or second does not establish failure under the third.

## 4. Geometric guarantee, law-relative fixing and semantic settlement differ

The horizons manuscript distinguishes a causal guarantee (all relevant past causal curves meet the stipulated past region) from agreement of fields across a fixed global solution class. Guarantee is sufficient under the stated domain-of-dependence law. Its failure does not produce a different outcome under every law. The solution class, operator, background, target observable and exact/approximate comparison must all be fixed explicitly.

Closed-universe refocusing makes the distinction concrete. The massless conformal scalar has the reviewed three-diamond determination structure. The separately reviewed physical-mass classification on fixed closed dust gives F_m(b)=J^-(b) for 0<tau<pi and F_m(b)=M for pi<=tau<2pi, for each stated m>0 and all smooth real solutions. The equality threshold includes continuity/dense-trace effects; it must not be inferred from the stricter geometric guarantee alone. This sidecar's acceptance is not manuscript integration or external priority clearance.

An arbitrarily small mass can change an exact fixing verdict without a large bounded response: the O(m^2) estimate and noisy-record questions have their own norms and quantifiers. Earlier claims that 'mass closes the future' are solution-class dependent and overlap established work; they are not a generic discovery of this project.

Direct chapter/reply reading shows why intrinsic past-field agreement does not establish agreement on every fact admitted by Correia--Rosenkranz. Their bivalent proposal permits future truths with later grounds; their notion of openness is not simply absence of present determination. A present truth does not, on that proposal alone, imply a presently existing concrete future event.

The repaired criticism therefore targets a NAMED supervenience/grounding premise. It does not impose present-ground completeness on a rival that rejects it. Supervaluation/settlement is a different semantic policy, not forced by the PDE construction. Nor is matching the causal past equivalent to matching the bow-tie realm, which includes spacelike exterior events and may already distinguish incoming radiation.

## 7. The bow-tie work now gives both a classification and a repaired rigidity result

For the explicit point-level causal-potential reconstruction, persistence holds on connected causally simple spacetimes exactly when every pair shares a future. Its historical enlargement H is persistent by construction. On a complete conformal cylinder with finite duration L and spatial manifold S, H equals the whole bow tie at every standpoint iff diam(S)<=L. This has independently reviewed proofs and an exact dust-plus-positive-Lambda realization of failure. It is not yet an external priority verdict or the uniquely intended semantics of the authors' motivation.

The location/fusion clause remains an independent premise question. The original text makes location non-existence-entailing. Fusions alone therefore do not prove every location exists. An unrestricted location-to-point-existence bridge combined with inherited potential risks admitting future points too; restricted alternatives remain open. Nearby bodily persistence requires less than universal remote elsewhere existence.

The repaired screening theorem characterizes the least region intercepting every relevant causal curve on every admissible SMOOTH Cauchy slice. This geometric minimum is not a minimal physical information base: exact dense data can fix smooth fields by continuity. On a fixed background the normal-hyperbolic uniqueness consequence is established at the stated scope.

For smooth vacuum GR, ordinary common-GHD extension, marked-path first-jet agreement and geodesic uniqueness yield equality of matched geometric readings at every proper duration defined in BOTH models. This avoids the unverified assertion that every restricted domain is itself maximal. Explicit marked timelike/causal completeness strengthens the conclusion to onto chronological/causal futures. A clipped-Minkowski example shows why equal survival cannot be inferred in the larger nonmaximal class. Matter requires the corresponding field-preserving theorem; the low-regularity scattering class is not silently included.

## The resulting philosophical position

The research constrains implications, rather than selecting an ontology:

- lawful determination does not imply present existence;
- absence from an existing realm does not imply physical indeterminism;
- agreement of accessible pasts does not imply agreement of whole existing realms;
- failure of causal screening does not imply failure of determination for a specified smooth law;
- agreement of shared-duration readings does not by itself imply equal future extent;
- a completed mathematical representation and an ontological completed history are different commitments.

An eternalist, a deterministic growing-block theorist, a later-grounded bivalent theorist and a settlement-based theorist can accept different parts of this mathematics. Their disagreement becomes sharper when each names the concrete existing realm, the total admitted facts, the truth policy and the bridge from physical structure to ontology. No branch has established a prediction separating those broad families.
