## Novelty boundary

The immediate predecessor already formalizes TOBC, NTOBC, and CPOBC; proves commutativity for the first two; states CPOBC commutator constraints and a center-implies-commutativity lemma; and records a failed pure-Pauli d=2 ansatz. Xu §29.11 reports further self-adjoint, triangular, perturbative, and restricted irreducible rigidity claims. The only clearly open boundary in the inspected source is a general nonsingular 2×2 irreducible CPOBC representation outside those reported classes, especially scalar R2 with delayed non-scalar onset, Jordan onset, and the complement of the stated first-simple-spectrum chart. Yet the displayed `diag(1,x)`, `diag(1,y)` coordinates appear not to follow from similarity alone. A novelty claim should wait on a valid normalization derivation and access to the unlinked companion/certificates.
