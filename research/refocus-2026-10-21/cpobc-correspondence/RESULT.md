Checked 2026-10-21. Primary source read depth is full-text for Srivastava–Surya arXiv:2603.25503v1 §§3.1–3.5 and Appendix (TeX source extracted from arXiv e-print); the APS record is metadata/abstract only because its article text requires authorization. ## Normalization audit of the Xu chart

The theorem introduces a “trace-balanced subchart d=a, x=−1” of the displayed chart Q_1=[[a,1],[q,d]], R_3=diag(1,x), R_4=diag(1,y). A change of basis (similarity) can diagonalize a diagonalizable R_3 and, because the R_n commute, preserve diagonal form for diagonalizable R_4; it yields the general form R_3=diag(u,v), R_4=diag(s,t). Similarity preserves eigenvalues. It cannot in general set u=s=1. Multiplying R_3 or R_4 by an arbitrary scalar is not a similarity and is not an evident symmetry of the CPOBC/MSR equations, which contain identity/complement terms such as 1−Q_1 and transition sums equal to I. Therefore the printed diag(1,x), diag(1,y) parameterization is an additional normalization/restriction unless the authors supply a separate gauge invariance or derive one eigenvalue equals 1 from the equations. The inspected §29.11 gives neither derivation. The trace-balanced d=a, x=−1 slice is an additional restriction even relative to that already normalized chart. Proposed correction: state the analyzed domain as “the subchart R_3=diag(1,x), R_4=diag(1,y), then its trace-balanced slice d=a,x=−1,” and do not call it exhaustive for the first-simple-spectrum chart. Before reusing it, restore independent eigenvalue parameters u,s and establish whether any valid scaling quotient removes them.

## Audit of `research/qbc-holonomy`

1. `check.py` launches `research/longshots/bc-nonzero/nonzero_continuation.py`, checks a few output substrings, and prints that the three operator orderings reduce to the seed. It does not encode or test any matrix/operator QBC residual, operator DGC, or operator-MS R equation. The 109 checked equations are scalar product-BC equations; the alleged TOBC/NTOBC/CPOBC scalar reductions are asserted in printed prose, not independently checked by this wrapper.
2. The exact continuation checks MSR, all 109 cross-multiplied scalar product-BC relations, nonzero transitions and **norm-GC** at stages 2–4. It explicitly exhibits a path phase ratio (3−4i)/5. Since primary GC is path-vector equality, that nontrivial scalar phase violates primary GC wherever the compared paths are in the same covariance class. This is a norm-covariant scalar model, not a demonstrated model satisfying primary vector GC. QBC scalar algebra and covariance are separate checks.
3. For scalar amplitudes, TOBC/NTOBC/CPOBC cross multiplication reduces to the same scalar product equation where denominators are nonzero; that algebraic observation is sound. It does not establish that the scalar model obeys the QSG paper's other axioms, nor that the phase is compatible with vector GC.
4. The statement “any representation is a direct sum of scalar ones” does not follow from commutativity. A commutative finite-dimensional matrix algebra may contain nilpotents and have nonsplit Jordan/upper-triangular representations; commutativity alone is not semisimplicity or simultaneous diagonalizability. The abstract algebra being commutative does not rule out nontrivial extensions.
5. The paper's Pauli calculation is a failed ansatz, not a general d=2 no-go. Correctly say only that its explicitly listed pure-Pauli assignments fail the required CPOBC path relation under invertibility.
6. The APS PRD record confirms publication metadata and an abstract that tracks the main operator-ordering results, but omits the arXiv v1 abstract’s final inference that a nontrivial representation must be higher-dimensional. Its article text was authorization-gated in this audit. Equation-level checks above are against the full arXiv v1 TeX; do not claim the final PRD text is identical to v1 without an accessible published copy.

## Candidate verdict

**Go only for a bounded, explicitly conditional claim; no-go for a general d=2 CPOBC no-go or for treating the scalar phase seed as an operator-QBC witness.** A defensible target would be an independent, auditable classification or counterexample for the still-unclassified nonsingular 2×2 irreducible CPOBC chart, with vector GC and operator-MSR stated separately from state-only/norm covariance. Xu's §29.11 occupies several neighboring branches already, so any theorem from this project must either reproduce/extend a specifically named uncovered stratum or correct a demonstrable normalization/assumption gap. Before implementation, obtain and audit Xu's cited companion and correct the R3/R4 normalization. No originality or priority claim is supportable from the present evidence alone.
