# Targeted priority and predecessor search

Checked 2026-10-21. This is a bounded search, not a global novelty certificate. The priority target was the exact mathematical statement: a normalized finite countably additive complex measure on the posts algebra, with literal shape-blind rank factors \(\nu(D_H)=\kappa_{|H|}N(H)A_p(H)\) for every principal past, is impossible outside (0<p\le1), even when \(\kappa_n\) varies arbitrarily with rank.

## Primary-source comparison

* **Dowker–Zalel, arXiv:2212.01149v2**, *Observables for cyclic causal set cosmologies*, Sec. V, Eqs. (29)–(31), Theorems 14–15. Read-depth: full-text for the target sections. Eq. (29) defines \(\mathcal R_p\) by including all or none of \(P_\infty^c\); the text identifies the doubly-principal event-algebra analogue. The authors apply the earlier tree variation criteria to doubly-reduced poscau, report inconclusive results for complex transitive percolation, and state that future work could compute a post-related level sum. This is the closest problem/domain predecessor and the clearest evidence that the underlying extension issue was open to those authors. It does not impose or classify rank-dependent all-principal values \(\nu(D_H)\).
* **Surya–Zalel, arXiv:2003.11311v1**, *A Criterion for Covariance in Complex Sequential Growth Models*, Sec. 3.1, Theorem 3.1 and Claims 3.2–3.3. Read-depth: full-text relevant sections. It supplies the bounded-variation level-sum method for complex sequential-growth measures on the full generated labeled event algebra and discusses complex percolation nonextension. It is a method predecessor; no covariant-post rule with free \(\kappa_n\) was found in the inspected text.
* **Dowker–Johnston–Surya, arXiv:1007.2725v1**, *On extending the Quantum Measure*, Sec. 4. Read-depth: full-text section. It proves nonextension of complex percolation from its labeled finite-cylinder amplitudes to the full labeled history sigma-algebra except when both p and q are real and nonnegative; since p+q=1, this is p∈[0,1]. This does not immediately imply the claim audited here: it is a larger sigma-algebra and it does not prescribe the values of post events according to rank factors.
* **Bollobás–Brightwell, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 10 (1997), DOI 10.1137/S0895480194281215**, *The Structure of Random Graph Orders*. Read-depth: abstract. Establishes finite random-graph-order post occurrence/decomposition results under stated asymptotic conditions. It is structural classical prior work, not a complex measure classification.
* **Bombelli–Seggev–Watson, arXiv:0809.2258v2**, *A Computation of the Expected Number of Posts in a Finite Random Graph Order*, Sec. 2, Theorem 2.1 and Eqs. (1)–(2). Read-depth: full-text relevant section. Gives the one-way infinite random-graph-order post-probability Euler-product calculation; it attributes the almost-sure infinite-post result to Alon et al. It supports classical sufficiency only. The original Alon et al. proof was not independently read.

## Priority judgment and limits

No matching theorem was located in these exact sources or bounded queries. The candidate increment, if independently verified and priority survives, is the all-principal, shape-blind, arbitrary-rank-factor obstruction on the covariant posts algebra. The convergence proof and most ingredients are elementary consequences of total variation, ordinal sums, and random graph order identities. It is not a new result about generic complex measures, and it is not the broader D–Z cylinder-extension problem. The exact all-principal factor rule is an imposed premise; dropping it permits normalized Dirac measures on cyclic histories. Non-indexed literature, alternate terminology, and later work remain unsearched. The literature pass therefore supports only “candidate narrow increment,” not “new theorem.”
