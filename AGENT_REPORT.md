# Novelty verdict: the selected doubly-principal complex-percolation result

Status: 2026-10-20. Independent priority/novelty gate for
`rt-selection-decision-gate`'s primary target. No novelty is claimed from
internal agreement, the Lean kernel, or a bounded search. The verdict is a
recommendation, not a priority certificate.

## Verdict

**PURSUE — narrow.** The labelled doubly-principal classification is a
**solution of an explicitly stated open case**, not a new general
measure-extension criterion. It is a credible short paper/note. It is not the
project's strongest novelty bet: the rank-dependent and vector-valued probes
in `research/NOVELTY_SPRINT.md` have higher upside. If one of those yields a
theorem, lead with it; keep this result as the verified floor.

## Exact target

`A_p(C)=p^{L(C)}(1-p)^{binom(|C|,2)-R(C)}`, `p∈ℂ`, on the sigma-algebra
generated by **doubly-principal** naturally labelled cylinders (`C` principal,
and `C` deleting its last label principal). Claim: a countably additive finite
complex measure with these prescribed values exists **iff** `p∈[0,1]`.
This is the labelled generator algebra; not the covariant intersection and not
the conditional post-factor theorem.

## The open case is explicitly in the literature

Two primary sources state the exact gap:

- **Zalel 2021 PhD thesis**, *Discrete random spacetimes* (Imperial), Ch. 6,
  p. 90: on “super-reduced poscau” (the 2023 paper's doubly-reduced poscau),
  the valency-one nodes make the earlier criterion unusable; the valency-one
  term “could be computed using techniques similar to those used to obtain the
  probability of a post in section 6.1. **We leave this calculation for future
  work.**”
- **Dowker–Zalel 2023**, arXiv:2212.01149v2, Sec. V: the application to
  Complex Transitive Percolation on doubly-reduced poscau “is inconclusive”;
  the valency-one/`S_n^{v=1}` term “in future it could be computed using
  techniques similar to those used to obtain the probability of a post [17].”

The selected proof supplies exactly that computation: every doubly-principal
`(k+2)`-order is `G⊕{u}⊕{v}`, and the absolute level sum is bounded below by
`|p|(|p|+|1-p|)^k c_s`, which diverges for `p∉[0,1]`.

## Three closest predecessors

1. **Dowker–Zalel 2023**, arXiv:2212.01149v2, *Class. Quantum Grav.* 40(15),
   155015. Defines the target algebra and gives the finite-level variation
   criterion, but leaves the valency-one/doubly-principal case open. *Delta:*
   explicit divergent lower bound and the resulting all-`p` classification.
2. **Zalel 2021 thesis**, Ch. 6. Introduces super-reduced poscau, states the
   problem and sketches the amended criterion, then leaves the valency-one
   calculation to future work. *Delta:* same; the thesis is the clearest
   evidence that the case was recognized as open.
3. **Dowker–Johnston–Surya 2010**, arXiv:1007.2725v1, and **Surya–Zalel 2020**,
   arXiv:2003.11311v1. Full labelled-algebra nonextension and bounded-variation
   criteria. *Delta:* a different, larger algebra; failure on the larger algebra
   does not imply failure on the smaller one, and restriction does not give the
   prescribed values on the missing cylinders.

The classical post probability/Euler factor is prior work (Bombelli–Seggev–Watson
2008); the proof uses it, it is not new.

## Strongest defeater (first)

**Significance/corollary.** The authors already sketched the criterion and named
the computation. The estimate is elementary: two universal caps, a cover count,
and a real-percolation endpoint probability. A referee may call it “a short
computation” rather than a theorem. This is a **solved open case**, not a new
method or a new general criterion. That is enough for a short note, but not for
a strong novelty claim.

**Second defeater:** a prior proof in an unindexed thesis, note, or non-English
source. The search below is bounded.

**Third defeater:** the conditional covariant extension is not an unconditional
transfer; it needs the all-principal common tail factor and cyclic
normalization, and the doubly-principal-only weakening has a Dirac
countermodel. It should be presented separately, not as part of the labelled
theorem.

## One-page claim contract (NOVELTY.md gate)

1. **Question and claim.** On the sigma-algebra generated by doubly-principal
   naturally labelled cylinders, does a finite countably additive complex
   measure with `A_p(C)=p^L(1-p)^{binom(n,2)-R}` exist? Claim: iff `p∈[0,1]`;
   the new ingredient is the divergent lower bound
   `V_{k+2} ≥ |p|(|p|+|1-p|)^k c_s`.
2. **Three nearest predecessors.** Dowker–Zalel 2023 (open case/criterion);
   Zalel 2021 thesis (states the open case, leaves computation); DJS 2010 /
   Surya–Zalel 2020 (full-algebra criterion, different domain).
3. **Delta.** The explicit valency-one/doubly-principal sum and classification;
   not a new measure-extension criterion. The conditional covariant theorem is a
   separate, assumption-heavy extension.
4. **Smallest example and verification.** `V_5=3/4+3√2/16`,
   `V_6=31/32+55√2/64` at `p=(1+i)/2`, exact `Q(√2)`; the two-cap decomposition
   and cover identities are checked in `experiments/baseline` and the light Lean
   kernel.
5. **Falsifier.** A prior proof of the same prescribed-value classification; a
   gap in the two-cap cover/incomparable identity; a finite certificate that
   contradicts the analytic bound; or a proof that the lower bound does not
   diverge for some `p∉[0,1]`.
6. **Novelty kind.** Mathematical: solved named open case + explicit bound.
   Formalization: finite Lean kernel. Physical: none without a model.
   Philosophical: none; no ontology follows from measure existence.

## Recommendation

- **Write a short note** for the labelled classification, citing the thesis and
  the 2023 paper's explicit open case. Title should promise the classification,
  not a general criterion.
- **Do not** claim a new general measure-extension theorem, a new probability
  method, or any physical/ontological result.
- **Lead with the rank-factor or vector-measure probe** if either yields a
  theorem; the labelled result is a solid floor, not the ceiling.
- **Keep the conditional covariant theorem separate**; its common-factor axiom
  and the Dirac countermodel must stay explicit.
- **Kill criterion for the short note:** if a prior proof of the same
  prescribed-value classification is found, retire the note and reuse the
  proof only as a check.
