## Our claims versus sourced claims

- **Philosophical thesis:** contrast-sensitive burden of proof for inferences
  from physics to ontology. A provisional argument, not consensus or established
  novelty; objections appear in the manuscript.
- **Logical observation:** exclusion of a conjunction does not exclude every
  ontology realization unless bridge/auxiliary premises or a universal scope
  argument are defended. Elementary inference, not new logic.
- **Probability proposition:** equality under a measure- and response-preserving
  map. Direct elementary proof included; no priority asserted. Measurability,
  preservation and complete allowed protocols are hypotheses, not discoveries.
- **Toy example:** finite Markov history versus pre-sampling and two small causal
  models. Exact fractions can be checked in `check_records.py`; these are
  constructions, not published-result reproduction or physical ontology models.
- **Bayesian observation:** fixed-package likelihood equality differs from
  marginalized evidence equality with different parameter/auxiliary priors.
  No numerical philosophical priors, posterior ranking, or model selection run.

No article claim relies on agent consensus, the number of collected sources,
the name of this repository, or an unaudited recent preprint. Bibliography
metadata and claim support are different checks. Verify both before submission.
