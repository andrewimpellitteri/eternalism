**Status: coordinator search, not an independent audit.** The planned
independent priority subagent could not run: the account's subagent credit
limit was exhausted before it produced a report. This note records what was

Date: 2026-10-21. Target: `paper/main.tex` at commit `0b4d147` (10 pages). The
argument under test: granting Brading's identification of what exists with the
genuine unities (2015 pp. 16–17; 2013 p. 1105 n. 3), the universe is a candidate
genuine unity; P1** is universal; its present is either W (so (a) fails), a
proper region (global or local, each with a cost), or outside the domain (the
global claim is lost). The paper presents this as a trilemma, not a refutation.

## 3. Closest prior art and the delta

- **Savitt 2021, SEP §3.3.** A population/co-presence objection: every system
  has a sustaining region, and it is unclear when two systems share a present.
  Savitt does not discuss the universe's own present, P1**, or the
  law-constitutive principle's application to the totality. The trilemma is
  distinct, though compatible.
- **Brading 2015, p. 17.** She admits that a system's needed region "might turn
  out to be the entire spatiotemporal history of the universe" and that this
  would return us to the block. The trilemma turns this admitted possibility
  into a question about the universe itself, using her p. 16 scope claim and
  p. 17 identification of what exists with the genuine unities.
- **Brading 2013, p. 1109.** The defeat condition is about every system's region
  being W, not about the universe's region being W while subsystems have proper
  regions. The paper's distinction between the universal and universe-own-region
  scenarios is the closest textual delta.
- **Brading 2015, p. 18.** "The crucial question" is whether there are genuine
  subsystems. The paper argues this is not the question on which P1**'s truth
  turns, because the universe case is independent of it.
- **Balaguer 2026, ch. 9.** The most likely unread location for a prior
  statement. Its abstract argues parity between eternalism and R-presentism; the
  chapter cites Brading 2013, not 2015. The body was not obtained. This is the
  main scoop risk.
- **Mason 2026.** Level- and domain-relative existence; treats Brading's local
  present as a live option, not as failing a universal clause. Adjacent, not the
  same.
- **Rovelli 2019.** Local, unoriented becoming; "neither presentism nor
  eternalism." The positive picture, not the trilemma.

**Delta statement (maximum strength, not a priority claim).** The note does not
discover a new objection family. It identifies an internal trilemma in Brading's
own texts: the principle that makes the universe a genuine unity and a thing
that exists also makes its present the whole history or leaves the proper-region
account unspecified; P1** is universal; so the sufficiency sentence "all we need
is (a)" does not settle the universe case. The closest prior art is Brading's
own p. 17 admission, which she treats as a possibility for a system, not as a
question about the totality. Whether anyone has drawn the trilemma explicitly
was not established.

## 4. Verdict

**Novelty not established; moderate risk.** The bounded search found no
published statement of the universe trilemma, but the negative side rests on
title-level citation graphs, two SEP entries, and web search, not on a full-text
philosophy index. PhilPapers, Semantic Scholar and Google Scholar were blocked;
Balaguer 2026's body and Brading 2011/2014 were unread. Absence of hits is not
priority.
