## 2. Severity-ranked issues and missing objections

### Blocking

**BL1 — The completion horn lacks the indispensable extension/agreement requirement (G6, G8).** A measure on the covariant sigma algebra is not an extension of a premeasure whose domain is the finite-cylinder algebra: neither algebra contains the other. Non-extension on the full generated sigma algebra does not alone prohibit assigning values on the smaller covariant algebra. For a direct independent counterexample to “has none,” choose any completed causet C and define ν(E)=1 when C belongs to E and 0 otherwise. This is a normalized countably additive measure on the covariant sigma algebra for every p. It generally does not encode the prescribed amplitudes. The manuscript must say which values are to be preserved, on which common domain. Its own `research/longshots/growth-completion/REPORT.md`, §2(C1–C2), already distinguishes these assertions. DJS §4 establishes an obstruction for the fixed scalar complex-percolation amplitude rule, not for every quantum growth model or every possible completed-history measure. See the [primary extension criterion](https://arxiv.org/html/2003.11311v1#S2.SS3).

This matters philosophically: the corrected result constrains a dynamics-plus-continuity package. It is not an obstruction peculiar to a block ontology. A growth interpretation using that same completed-event measure inherits it too.

### Major

**MA1 — The theorem does not exclude its premises (C6).** If A denotes the premises and K the three candidates, the result has the form A ⇒ B∈K. Its exclusion is A ∧ B∉K, not A. This is a direct logical error in the sentence intended to exemplify the manuscript's own exclusion schema. The three surviving relations supply counterexamples to the current wording.

**MA2 — The clock calculation has not located the sole failure of the interacting trinity (Q9–Q10).** With G(X)=Σλ PλXPλ and Pλψ=ψ, the equality <ψ,G(X)ψ>=<ψ,Xψ> is an immediate projector identity. To obtain the conditional mean one divides both numerator and denominator by the relevant clock probability. Equal ratios remain equal even when that probability is nonuniform. Thus the two calculations do not start disagreeing “at normalization.” What can fail is a proposed state-independent isometric identification with a reduced dynamics. Furthermore, full spectral pinching is not the [periodic-clock partial averaging construction](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.06479v3#S5). The project's `literature.md`, F1, expressly makes this distinction. The manuscript currently drops its most important caveat.

**MA3 — Step constraints are not generically a rescue, and changing a constraint may change the physics (Q12–Q13).** [BR §II](https://arxiv.org/html/1806.00956v2#S2) writes UΨ=Ψ as JΨ=0 for a Hermitian logarithm. Their larger sectors result from the engineered spectrum and consistency of the steps, not from avoiding eigenvalue equations. For an arbitrary unitary U, 1 need not even be an eigenvalue. [H §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.10967v4#S5) explicitly warns that its discrete model is not obtained by directly discretizing its continuous one. The strongest objection is therefore that the compared constructions change dynamics/access, not merely representation. Their nonequivalence needs no metaphysical explanation.

**MA4 — Finite-cylinder decidability is being made into a global criterion of reality (G4, G7).** The manuscript needs to distinguish a Boolean question decided uniformly by stage n on the entire history space from an existential question with a finite positive witness. For example, “there is a two-element antichain stem” is covariant and can become irrevocably true once two unrelated minimal elements appear. No fixed finite cutoff decides its truth and falsity across all possible completions. Hence an algebraic statement about cylinder events is not a theorem that there are no finitely established physical facts. This is a reviewer inference from the definitions, not a newly attributed theorem. Restrict the bridge explicitly to that algebra; distinguish mathematical completion from the claim that every future event already exists.

**MA5 — The causal-set attribution erases a live disagreement (G5).** Arageorgis criticizes the dependence at §4, p. 52. Sorkin's §7 footnote acknowledges it, but [§5](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/0703098v1) defends localized becoming. Wüthrich–Callender [§4](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.00018v1#S4) considers a cardinality-based form of growth and replies to the completed-future objection. A citation bundle making them proponents of one critical conclusion is inaccurate. Their response belongs next to the criticism, rather than being left for a generic final hedge.

**MA6 — Order-invariance is neither blanket ancillarity nor an observer theorem (G3).** Conditional uniformity over a finite poset's compatible orders does not imply that order is independent of poset structure: that structure already reveals the necessary precedence constraints. Nor is it an unconditional parameter-free distribution of an identified statistic. At most, the conditional labelling randomization has a parameter-free kernel in the specified finite model. [BL §5, Example 4](https://arxiv.org/html/0901.0240v3#S5) blocks a naive infinite-order generalization; it is not itself a Bell-causal CSG counterexample. More fundamentally, saying that every physically accessible record is covariant is a bridge about access. The source papers' beables do not supply a record instrument or an embedded observer. State the gauge interpretation as a postulate and limit the analogy.

**MA7 — Normalization is too weakly specified to establish unitarity, and “only commuting” lacks a stated theorem domain (Q3–Q4).** Normalized conditional states may be obtained by nonlinear, state-dependent renormalization. For example, normalizing diag(2,1) applied to (1,0) and (1,1)/sqrt(2) changes their overlap from 1/sqrt(2) to 2/sqrt(5), despite both output norms being one. No common unitary does that. All-state linear isometries, rather than unit norm along one history, are the relevant criterion.
