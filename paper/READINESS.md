# Argument, novelty and submission gates

The article may be useful as a working draft now. The following are not optional
before presenting it as an original, source-complete research contribution.

1. Reconstruct the nearest philosophical predecessors at full-text depth.
   In particular, challenge whether package/bridge analysis is already fully
   available in Stein, Brading, the philosophy-of-science underdetermination
   literature and existing physical-ontology debates. Absence of search hits
   is not priority. The novelty-prepass and independent novelty-audit tasks
   remain separate from this draft.
2. Identify the proposed new advance in one claim contract: exact thesis,
   three closest predecessors, changed assumptions, consequence, falsifier,
   philosophical significance and verification route. The present generic
   burden-of-proof framework alone is not yet that advance.
3. Resolve source correspondence: original Stein page image, full Brading,
   updated Putnam record, and complete defender/critic pairs for GBT and
   spotlight. Do not claim an original theorem is wrong from OCR alone.
4. Strengthen the substantive defense of admissibility and bridge principles.
   Why do the proposed admissibility standards track explanation rather than
   protect a desired answer? What costs constrain auxiliary rescue? When can
   explanatory physics favor a view without a unique empirical prediction?
5. If a physical classification is attempted, choose a model, observable and
   intervention class before computing. The finite path example is not a
   novel ontology theorem. Passive observational agreement does not establish
   interventional equivalence; arbitrary representation changes do not establish
   gauge equivalence.
6. Obtain independent adversarial review of the article's actual argument,
   not merely its spelling or citations; preserve disagreements. An initial
   working-draft review does not replace the later result/novelty audit.
7. Reproduce any claimed physical example, verify bibliography metadata,
   compile without unresolved references and visually inspect the complete PDF.
   Record tool versions, build command, exact-check output and limitations.
8. Only then decide venue, length, authorship, acknowledgments, disclosure,
   citation style and submission. No publication, upload or submission is
   authorized by creating the draft.
