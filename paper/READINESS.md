# Argument, novelty and submission gates

The article may be useful as a working draft now. The following are not optional
before presenting it as an original, source-complete research contribution.

1. Reconstruct the nearest philosophical predecessors at full-text depth.
   In particular, challenge whether package/bridge analysis is already fully
   available in Stein, Brading, the philosophy-of-science underdetermination
   literature and existing physical-ontology debates. Absence of search hits
   is not priority. The novelty-prepass and independent novelty-audit tasks
   remain separate from this draft.
2. Identify the proposed new advance in one claim contract: exact thesis,
   three closest predecessors, changed assumptions, consequence, falsifier,
   philosophical significance and verification route. The present generic
   burden-of-proof framework alone is not yet that advance.
3. Resolve source correspondence: original Stein page image, full Brading,
   updated Putnam record, and complete defender/critic pairs for GBT and
   spotlight. Do not claim an original theorem is wrong from OCR alone.
4. Strengthen the substantive defense of admissibility and bridge principles.
   Why do the proposed admissibility standards track explanation rather than
   protect a desired answer? What costs constrain auxiliary rescue? When can
   explanatory physics favor a view without a unique empirical prediction?
5. If a physical classification is attempted, choose a model, observable and
   intervention class before computing. The finite path example is not a
   novel ontology theorem. Passive observational agreement does not establish
   interventional equivalence; arbitrary representation changes do not establish
   gauge equivalence.
6. Obtain independent adversarial review of the article's actual argument,
   not merely its spelling or citations; preserve disagreements. An initial
   working-draft review does not replace the later result/novelty audit.
7. Reproduce any claimed physical example, verify bibliography metadata,
   compile without unresolved references and visually inspect the complete PDF.
   Record tool versions, build command, exact-check output and limitations.
8. Only then decide venue, length, authorship, acknowledgments, disclosure,
   citation style and submission. No publication, upload or submission is
   authorized by creating the draft.

## Totality rebuild and trilemma repair, 21 October 2026

The Savitt-delta and critical-note audits found that the P1** coverage retarget
is not an increment: Brading 2013 p. 1110 already states the local-boundedness
condition, and Savitt's 2021 objection is a co-presence/population objection,
not the coverage union point. The article was rebuilt around the universe case:
Brading's principle identifies what exists with the genuine unities and applies
the law-constitutive approach to the material universe (2015 p. 16; 2013
p. 1105 n. 3). The first draft claimed a categorical reductio: the universe is a
thing; its sustaining region is the whole history; P1** (a) is false of it.

The independent logic audit (`reviews/totality-logic/REVIEW.md`) rejected that
categorical form. Its main findings: T1 is an adopted premise, not a textual
entailment; T4 is not sound as stated, because a Cauchy slice or thick global
slab is a proper region and Brading's rejection of "a spatially global present"
is qualified by "common to all systems"; the 2013 condition is sufficient, not
exhaustive, and the 2015 condition is necessary, not sufficient; the four-event
model is valid but models the extensional shadow, not Brading's dynamics, and
must address 2013 n. 7; "scale-free" misdescribes a scale-general approach that
allows different principles at different scales. The draft was repaired
accordingly.

Current form: a **three-horn trilemma**, 10 pages. Horn 1: the universe's present
is W, so (a) is false on the unrestricted reading. Horn 2: the present is a
proper region, either spatially global (needs a qualification of Brading's
rejection) or local (needs an account of how a local region sustains the whole).
Horn 3: the universe is outside the domain, split into (3a) not a genuine unity
(so it does not exist) and (3b) a term-of-art restriction (so the global claim is
lost). A prior "no present" case is flagged: if the universe is not a dynamical
system in Brading's sense, P1** fails or is undefined for it. The conclusion is
conditional: P1** cannot be both universal and satisfied by the universe without
an account Brading does not supply. The 2013/2015 defeat and survival conditions
are criticized as a gap in framing, not as logical non-sequiturs; the four-event
model states its assumptions and n. 7; the trilemma itself is also marked as
internal to the common Minkowski representation; the diagnosis is
"scale-general," with a missing scale-selection guarantee rather than a missing
existence criterion.

A second independent pass (`reviews/totality-logic/SECOND_PASS.md`) found four
remaining gaps in the 0f09900 draft: the no-present case, the conflation of the
two exits in horn 3, the patch-only cost for non-global proper regions, and the
unextended n. 7 caveat. The current draft repairs all four. The second pass still
judges the note "close to publishable as a specialist note" but not yet
copy-edited; that judgement predates the current repairs.
