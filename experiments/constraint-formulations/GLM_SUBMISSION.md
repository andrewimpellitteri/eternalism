## What was implemented

Model (identical ingredients in all lanes): clock ℂ³, `H_C = diag(0,1,2)`,
readings `|τ_k⟩`, `t_k = 2πk/3`; qubit `H_S = (ε/2)σ_z` with ε ∈ {1, 2};
interaction `gK⊗σ_x` with `K_rd = |τ_0⟩⟨τ_0|` (reading, `[K_rd,H_C] ≠ 0`) or
`K_en = |n=1⟩⟨n=1|` (energy, commuting).

1. **Eigenvalue constraint (A)** — `H|ψ⟩ = λ|ψ>`, physical states in one
   eigenspace `V_λ` (clock-three-picture conventions, re-implemented).
2. **Step-invariance (B)** — `𝒰|Ψ⟩ = |Ψ⟩`, `𝒰 = Σ_t U_{t,t−1}⊗|τ_t⟩⟨τ_{t−1}|`
   (BR Eqs. (2)–(3), reading basis). Steps: B1 uniform kick every step; B2 kick
   on leaving τ_0 with BR closure `U_{0,2}U_{2,1}U_{1,0} = 1` (last step =
   `(U_{2,1}U_{1,0})^{-1}`); B3 kick with free last step (HSC-style, no closure).
3. **Z_3 group action (C)** — replica of Ludescher Ex. 32 (C0, validation) and
   our model (C1): action generated by `U_cyc = e^{−i(2π/3)H}`, physical space
   `Fix(U_cyc) = ⊕_{λ ≡ 0 mod 3} V_λ` (our stated convention for a real-valued
   constraint spectrum; coincides with their kernel for ℤ_3-valued spectra),
   seeds `M_g = U_cyc^g M_0`, `M_0 = √3|τ_0⟩⟨τ_0|⊗1`.

Key findings (all from `model_output.txt`):

- **R1 (dimension is formulation-dependent).** Same Hamiltonian ingredients,
  four different answers at ε=1 (2-dim surfaces / empty / 2 / empty / empty).
  The emptiness at ε=1 of B1/B3 and C is incommensurability: those constraints
  require system energies on the lattice `ε ∈ 2ℤ`, while formulation A's
  degeneracy condition (two system energies differing by a multiple of ω) is
  weaker and holds at ε=1. The "trinity at zero coupling" is thus itself
  tuning-dependent.
- **R2 (B2's universal 2-dim space is imposed, not derived).** BR's closure
  condition is an extra physical requirement (the fine-tuning Chataignier et al.
  remark about); with it, dim = d_S = 2 at every point by their degeneracy
  theorem — verified numerically (degeneracies [2,2,2], `U³ = 1` to 8.9e-16).
  Its conditional dynamics is the kicked sequence with a closure-compensated
  final step, and it does not coincide with either other lane's prediction.
- **R3 (C preserves dimension only under Ex. 32's premises).** With K_en the
  interaction moves the touched constraint eigenvalues off the 3ℤ lattice: dim
  drops 2 → 1 at g=0.5 (only the untouched sector `|2,↑⟩` at λ=3 survives) and
  returns to 2 only at the fine-tuned g*=√3 where `1 + √(1+g²) = 3`. In Ex. 32
  itself the dimension stays n because all spectra are ℤ_n-valued.
- **R4 (normalization splits into two independent conditions in C).** Seed
  completeness `(1/3)Σ_g M_g†M_g = 1` holds at g=0 (defect 2e-16) and fails at
  g≠0; the frame/factorization property `|ψ(g)⟩ = |τ_g⟩⊗|ψ_S(g)⟩` likewise holds
  exactly at g=0 (defects ≤ 5.4e-16) and fails at g≠0 (0.31). At g* each Fix
  basis state has p(0)=1/3 while superpositions do not (p dev 0.28) — per-basis
  uniformity is not the completeness condition (polarization).
- **R5 (validation).** The C0 replica of Ludescher Example 32 reproduces all six
  structural claims to ≤ 3e-15, including the g-dependent local-seed step maps
  (maxabs difference 1.732) and the fixed center-of-mass map of their Eq. (60).
- **R6 (cross-check).** Formulation A at ε=1 reproduces the prior lane's
  committed numbers exactly (g=0 surface list; g=0.5 spectrum and dims;
  "agree: True").

## Strongest contrary evidence and audit notes

- **The three formulations do not share an admissible-interaction class.** "The
  same interaction" is therefore only pairwise meaningful: A takes any K; B can
  only express reading-controlled kicks; C needs gauge-commuting K (and, for
  dimension preservation, ℤ_n-valued spectra). The table's per-lane interaction
  conventions are stated in SPEC.md; conclusions about "the same model" are
  conditional on those translations.
- **C1's physical space is our convention.** For a real-valued constraint
  spectrum we define the invariant sector as `Fix(e^{−i(2π/3)H})` (λ ≡ 0 mod 3).
  Ludescher et al. work with ℤ_n-valued spectra where this coincides with their
  kernel; they do not state the real-spectrum case. Anything built on this is
  our extrapolation, flagged in SPEC.md.
- **Errata found in Ludescher Example 32 (audited, non-propagating).** (i) The
  single-particle momentum definition `|p⟩ = n^{−1/2}Σ_g conj(χ_p(g))|g⟩` is
  inconsistent with Eq. (58)'s shift direction and the character statement; the
  position-level formulas (58)–(59) are mutually consistent and were implemented
  verbatim — every downstream construction reproduces. (ii) The displayed
  prefactor of `M_e^{(cm)}|d;Kd²⟩` should be 1 by their own definition (harmless
  for Eq. (60)). Details in sources.jsonl.
- **B2's success is by construction.** Imposing closure is itself the
  fine-tuning that other papers warn about; it should not be read as the step
  formulation being "more physical".
- **Defeat-search status.** No novelty claim is made. For the assembly: the
  primaries do not make this comparison (Ludescher et al. cite HSC but not
  Boette–Rossignoli; HSC's Sec. 5 cites Mondragón–Pérez–Rovelli, unread), and
  the prior lane's same-week searches found no three-way comparison. Both web
  search tools were unavailable in this task (recorded above), so the defeat
  search is bounded by primary-source citation chasing.

## Limitations

- One model family (N=3, one qubit, two K choices, ε ∈ {1,2}, g ∈ {0, 0.5,
  √3}); dimensions are numerical (eigenvalue clustering 1e-7, residuals
  asserted), not proofs. The K_rd fine-tuned set was not swept (only K_en's
  closed-form family at g=√3 was checked).
- The kick translation of `gK⊗σ_x` into formulation B (dwell time at a reading)
  is a stated convention, not a derivation; A↔B correspondence is approximate.
- Only pure states on single sectors/surfaces; mixtures, POVM blurring, and the
  twirl identity were deliberately not re-examined (covered by the prior lane).
- Floating point only; the ε=1 emptiness and the g*=√3 dimension recovery are
  confirmed against closed forms but not proved in a proof assistant.
- Numerical gotchas found and fixed during development (worth recording for
  reviewers): a σ_z-matrix mistakenly used as the σ_x kick; step-list ordering
  (accidentally hidden at ε=1 by commutation); outer-product conjugation
  convention in reading projectors (harmless at τ_0, not at τ_1, τ_2).
