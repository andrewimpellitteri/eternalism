and what is not established. It is not a result: no research contribution has been
selected, no novelty gate has been passed, and nothing here says anything about which
temporal ontology is correct.

## 2. What the checks found

Bibliographic fields from workers were wrong often enough to matter. Across the original
13 foundations records, six had a wrong DOI, venue or page range, and one citation
(Hawley, "The Future of the Universe", Mind 2009) could not be identified at all and is
flagged unverified. A repo-wide Crossref check of 18 DOIs caught four; the others were
caught by hand. Later lanes did better (the philosophy register passed 36 of 36; ico-qrf
passed all checks), but one more bad DOI turned up in a lane awaiting review. Rule going
forward: no lane is accepted before its identifiers are matched to Crossref or arXiv.

Mathematical content was checked where it could be done by hand. The five project
correspondence cases are correct: a universal relation satisfies the stated premises of
an isolated Stein-type claim, a strictly-timelike-past relation refutes the weakened
version, no finite-dimensional canonical clock exists (trace argument), the normalized
conditional update of `diag(1, 1/sqrt 2)` is not affine, and Gaussian dephasing is
CP-divisible without being a semigroup.

Several worker claims were weaker than stated. Examples: a "known-answer catalog" was an
exhaustive enumeration with internal cross-checks; "vacuous hypotheses" in a
formalization paper was a named risk with no reported incident; a lemma attributed to
Einstein 1905 (order reversal for spacelike pairs) is not in the 1905 text.

## 5. Process lessons

- Twelve concurrent GLM workers exceeded the provider's rate limit and eleven died; five
  at a time ran cleanly. A concurrency cap was added to the runner (not yet committed when
  this was written).
- A worker that finishes and then exits non-zero is recorded as `failed`; three finished
  lanes needed manual transition. After a relaunch the old result file makes a live
  worker look failed.
- Same-family agents agreeing is not evidence. The most useful review steps were
  mechanical (identifier matching, hand-checked counterexamples) and independent
  (full-text spot checks, defeat searches from a different model family).
