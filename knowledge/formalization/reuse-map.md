## Candidate proof slices

1. Quantum-clock equivalence: first reproduce the finite-dimensional statement of a specific relational-trinity result (the seed identifies arXiv:1912.00033v3 and its relativistic extension, arXiv:2007.00580v1). Model clock/system Hilbert spaces, Hamiltonian/unitary constraint, conditioning or relational state, and the map between descriptions with the paper's exact clock hypotheses. Next vary exactly one feature (e.g. specified interaction or finite-resolution POVM) and compare predictions. AxQM/Physlib can only be considered after their public declarations are inspected; they are candidates, not verified dependencies.
2. Assumption-sensitive relativity: encode a small first-order signature for events, observers/paths, and betweenness; state each physical axiom as a named premise. Prove one known theorem under a minimal named locale, then test axiom ablations with a model finder. Keep causal order, coordinate clock time, global foliation, and ontological bridge premises separate. The Isabelle Schutz work demonstrates why locale boundaries and strengthened readings must be explicit.

## Proof correspondence and dependency audit design

- Freeze each source paper version and code commit; make a source table from each Lean/Isabelle declaration to exact paper equation/theorem/definition, hypotheses, and any deliberate strengthening or weakening.
- Review physical assumptions separately from mathematical definitions and philosophical bridge premises. Record observer access, boundary conditions, idealizations, and operational observables.
- For Lean, inspect `#print axioms` on each result and recursively inspect imported declarations; search the transitive source closure for `sorryAx`, custom axioms, unsafe axioms, and theorem aliases. Record Lean/toolchain/mathlib revisions. For Isabelle, inspect the session graph, locale assumptions, axiomatizations, oracles, and any sorry-like admitted propositions.
- Re-run the small target in a pinned clean checkout only after permitted dependencies are available; retain command, compiler version, logs, and theorem names. Kernel acceptance validates the encoded statement and proof, not the English-to-physics correspondence.
- Independently check the translation: derive core definitions from the paper, have a physics reader review the model, and test limiting cases/countermodels. A finite model finder must report domain size and encoded signature; never elevate bounded search into general independence.
