# Cosmological and thermodynamic arrows: comparison contract

Scope: argument map, not verdict on the correct cosmology or temporal ontology. The unit compared is the named source/model version. “Arrow” means an asymmetry in a specified physical process, record, or probability distribution. It does not by itself mean that time objectively passes. A temporal orientation used to label a solution, causal order, clock time, determinism, and an ontology of past/present/future are separate notions.

Read-depth key: **full-text** means the listed primary text was read at the stated locators, not that every proof or calculation was independently audited; **abstract** is a screened lead only. Source identifiers resolve to records in sources.jsonl.

## Scope and limitations

- The low entropy premise is not interchangeable with a verified numerical entropy of the universe. GR has difficult choices of state space, coarse-graining, gravitational entropy, and measure; Earman's objections are serious but only screened at abstract depth in this lane.
- A typicality result is conditional on its measure and reference class. In infinite or non-normalizable settings, a cutoff or conditioning choice can change conclusions. Observer-selection arguments need an explicit self-location/measure premise.
- Penrose's WCH is a geometric boundary proposal. Weyl curvature is not a generally established gravitational entropy functional. Stoica's cited mathematical result is conditional on a singularity class. Wallace distinguishes gravity's effects on matter entropy from entropy of the field and contests an overly restrictive reading of Earman; this counterpoint still does not settle the cosmological entropy question.
- Time-reversal symmetry of the dynamics, time asymmetry of a solution, a cosmological boundary condition, and entropy growth are different claims. Empirical microscopic T violation exists in the BABAR neutral-B result, yet does not settle the thermodynamic arrow.
- None of these arrows establishes determinism, causal order as an ontological primitive, clock time as fundamental, objective passage, presentism, or eternalism. A block representation is compatible with entropy-increasing histories; an arrow model can use an oriented time parameter without saying that only the present exists.

## Nearest predecessors and novelty falsifiers

No novelty claim is made. Relevant prior lines already include Boltzmann/Goldstein typicality; Albert's PH package; Penrose's WCH; Earman's GR entropy critique; Carroll-Chen spontaneous-inflation/central-time cosmology; Barbour-Koslowski-Mercati gravitational typicality; DKS recurrence/observer typicality; and later Boltzmann-brain/self-location analyses.

Any suggested gap in a particular model comparison would be defeated by: (a) a prior paper deriving the same arrow under equivalent dynamics, entropy definition and measure; (b) a normalized, physically motivated measure resolving the supposed typicality ambiguity; (c) an established GR/quantum-gravity entropy functional that makes the proposed boundary condition redundant or inapplicable; (d) a model-specific failure of the assumed WCH/central-time behavior; or (e) a result showing the proposed distinction changes no accessible observable and only restates a representation or metaphysical choice. The present lane did not conduct a systematic backward/forward citation audit, so these sources are not exhaustive.
