# The problem of time in quantum gravity

11 November 2026. GLM reading lane. Depths as in `sources.jsonl`; paraphrase except
short phrases in quotation marks. Secondary sources marked (sec).

## 1. Main positions

**Frozen formalism.** In canonical GR the Hamiltonian constraint generates evolution
and foliation symmetry; imposing constraints as operator constraints gives not a
Schrödinger equation but the stationary Wheeler–DeWitt equation ĤΨ = 0 — "nothing
at all happens in the universe" at the level of the formalism (Anderson 2012,
full-text-partial, (sec); DeWitt 1967, discovery).

**Kuchař: a cluster of problems, ten attempts, none succeeds.** The state
functional "does not seem to depend on time"; ten attempts to circumvent this all
fall short (Kuchař 1991, abstract only — primary not openly accessible). His three
forms, per Butterfield–Isham: finding an internal time in classical GR; finding a
time "buried" in the Wheeler–DeWitt equation; interpreting it in a fundamentally
timeless way so time is approximate (Butterfield–Isham 1999, full-text-partial).
Isham's classification: time identified before quantization; after quantization
(WdW); or playing no fundamental role (Isham 1992, full-text-partial).

**Barbour: duration is redundant.** Ephemeris time δt = √(Σᵢmᵢ(δdᵢ)²/2(E−V))
defines duration from configurations alone; a "timeless law" governs change with
no time assumed in advance, and "time emerges only on the extremal curves"; GR
admits this reading; "it is entirely possible – indeed likely – that time as such
plays no role in the universe" (Barbour 2009, full-text; his hedges: infinite
universes, black holes). Instants are complete relative configurations (Barbour
1994, abstract). His group's quantum version: typical solutions split into "a
single past and two distinct futures", with records growing as complexity grows —
no special initial conditions needed (Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati 2014, abstract).

**Rovelli: forget time.** Treat all variables as partial observables; complete
observables are correlations between them; states solve Ĥψ = 0 and dynamics is
transition amplitudes. Thermal time hypothesis: "there is no preferred physical
time variable"; which variable is time is set by the statistical state via its
Tomita–Takesaki modular flow (Rovelli 2009, full-text-partial; Connes–Rovelli
1994, abstract: the flow "is determined by the thermodynamical state").

**Page–Wootters: evolution without evolution.** The Schrödinger time parameter is
unobservable, so it is consistent to treat the universe as stationary; dynamics
lives in conditional probabilities given a clock observable's value (Page–Wootters
1983, abstract; Anderson 2012 gives the formula and problems: good clocks "far
from guaranteed"; the scheme "does not per se address questions of becoming").
Modern work proves equivalences between the conditional picture, clock-neutral
relational observables, and the relational Heisenberg picture (Höhn–Smith–Lock,
abstract; already cited as arXiv:1912.00033 in NOVELTY.md).

**Gryb–Thébault: the freeze is a choice.** They propose an operator constraint
equation that "admits genuine evolution": reparametrization makes total energy a
constant of motion, not a gauge freedom, so "the Hamiltonian constraint should be
interpreted as an identity satisfied by the classical evolution but should not be
imposed formally as a constraint", Dirac's procedure is not applied, and
"superpositions of energy eigenstates are allowed"; York's conformal ontology
uniquely selects the constraints (Gryb–Thébault 2014, full-text-partial). "Time
Remains" diagnoses the received view: change as mere relative variation led to the
timeless formalism; they separate that from temporal succession (Gryb–Thébault
2015, abstract + partial text).

**Shape dynamics.** GR's content admits a description "that does not require a
Lorentz invariant spacetime", with local size irrelevant: foliation-preserving
3-diffeos plus volume-preserving conformal transformations, mapped to CMC slices
of GR (Gomes–Gryb–Koslowski 2011, abstract; Barbour 2011, abstract).

**Philosophy.** Butterfield–Isham: quantum theory holds time fixed (classical,
nondynamical, same in all models); GR holds it fixed only in the first sense; WKB
emergence of time is state-dependent and superposed WKB sectors carry separate
times (1999). Huggett–Wüthrich: spacetime emergence must be functional and
"physically salient", but usual salience criteria "simply cannot be satisfied by
derivations from non-spatiotemporal theories" (2021 chapter, full-text-partial).
Earman: "the gauge interpretation of GTR implies a truly frozen universe …
stripped of its B-series change"; philosophers "have generally ignored this
puzzle"; he tests whether quantization "is stymied until the dynamics is unfrozen"
on toy models (2003, full-text-partial; 2002 Philosophers' Imprint,
abstract/highlights only — full text inaccessible).

## 2. Canonical arguments

**(a) Frozen formalism.** (P1) Canonical GR's evolution and foliation symmetry are
generated by Hamiltonian constraints (Gryb–Thébault 2014 §1). (P2) Dirac
quantization imposes first-class constraints on states (Isham 1992, category II).
(P3) Hence ĤΨ = 0, a stationary equation (Anderson 2012 eqs. (2)–(4)). (P4)
Observables are gauge-invariant constants of the motion; "motion is pure gauge", so
no gauge-invariant magnitude changes (Earman 2003). *Objection:* every premise is
contested — Gryb–Thébault reject P2; Kuchař attacks P3's equation; and the
equation is not a well-defined operator statement (ordering, regularization, inner
product: Anderson §1.3; Butterfield–Isham); Isham warns discreteness approaches
could "render irrelevant" the whole WdW discussion (1992).

**(b) No internal time in a recollapsing universe.** (P1) A usable internal time is
a canonical functional T, monotone along solutions, turning solved constraints
into a Schrödinger equation (Isham 1992 category I; Kuchař's form (i) per
Butterfield–Isham). (P2) Geometric candidates fail: scale times "are not monotonic
in recollapsing universes" (Anderson §2.4). (P3) Matter clocks buy a foliation at
the cost of preferring one: dust introduces "a privileged dynamical reference
frame and time foliation" (Brown–Kuchař 1995, abstract), drawing the "unphysical
preferred foliation" critique (Gryb–Thébault 2014 intro, citing Kuchař).
Conclusion: for closed recollapsing cosmologies — this repo's closed dust model —
global internal time does not exist. *Objection:* the global-clock demand is the
disputable premise; Rovelli and Barbour deny it is needed, Gryb–Thébault deny the
clock must be classical.

**(c) Relational time from correlations.** (P1) The Hamiltonian flow is a gauge
direction; genuine quantities are correlations. (P2) All variables, clocks
included, are partial observables; complete observables are functions of two
("evolving constants"). (P3) Predictions are probabilities for correlations
(Rovelli 2009 §IV; his 1991 papers per its reference list). *Objection:* Hájíček
1991 — "the procedure does not work, at least in the presented form"; Anderson:
the base conditional probability is postulated "outside the conventional
formalism", and becoming is not addressed; philosophically, whether a static web
of correlations constitutes change is exactly what is disputed.

**(d) Barbour's elimination of duration.** (P1) Duration is definable from
configurations and the law. (P2) Only relative configurations are physical; the
law is a timeless selection among paths (Barbour 2009). (P3) In quantum cosmology
the state lives on configuration space; experienced history must come from records
(BKM 2014). *Objection:* the wavefunction and inner product now carry the
dynamics, and the frozen-equation and Hilbert-space problems recur (Anderson);
Barbour's own hedges (infinity, black holes).

**(e) Earman's gauge argument.** (P1) Hamiltonian constraints generate the motion
(2003). (P2) Gauge-related descriptions are one situation; genuine magnitudes are
gauge-invariant. Conclusion: B-series change is lost. (P3) The puzzle deserves
resolution, which Earman pursues on reparametrization-invariant toys (2002).
*Objection:* relational-change theorists accept P1–P2 and deny the sting —
"Time Remains"' target.

## 3. Live open problems

1. **Making ĤΨ = 0 well-defined** (ordering, regularization, inner product).
   Progress: one full model with rigorous constraint operator, anomaly-free
   algebra, and Hilbert space.
2. **Global internal time for recollapsing models.** Progress: a clock proven
   monotone on a recollapsing model's full phase space, or a precise no-go theorem.
3. **Probability and outcomes for relational observables** — Hájíček's negative
   verdict is the benchmark; Trinity's equivalence covers ideal clocks. Progress:
   extending it to interacting clocks and finite resolution (this repo's NOVELTY
   candidate B).
4. **Emergence of time from the freeze** — state-dependence, loss of Schrödinger
   form at next WKB order, sector selection (Butterfield–Isham's list). Progress:
   a controlled decoherence selection of one WKB sector with an error bound.
5. **An arrow from timeless structure** — records/complexity vs thermal time vs
   Past-Hypothesis posits. Progress: a general one-past/two-futures theorem for a
   GR-based model, or thermal time reproducing cosmological time for realistic
   states.
6. **Whether the classical standpoint-relations survive quantization at all** —
   see part 4; the question is essentially unposed in this literature.

## 4. What in this repository depends on this area

Every result in the paper assumes a classical spacetime with events. If time is
not fundamental, the four relations fare differently.

- **Having become, B_M(b): does not make sense as stated.** It is defined over
  globally hyperbolic manifolds under a locality premise whose carrier — a
  category of background spacetimes (the Loc/GH setting, from Fewster–Verch) — is
  exactly what a fundamental quantum theory would not supply. Replacements: order
  over complete relative configurations (Barbour), records in typical solutions
  (BKM), or nothing — conditional probabilities "do not per se address questions
  of becoming" (Anderson). `becoming_causets.tex` is the paper's only existing
  step toward event-free structure.
- **Being fixed, Det(b) and F_L(b): makes partial sense.** Fixing one variable's
  values from another's is what relational observables formalize; the algebraic
  version is already the cone-algebras note, on fixed backgrounds. But the
  slice-test machinery, domain-of-dependence, and region-agreement presuppose
  background regions; the frozen formalism suggests the fundamental notion is
  constraint-solving. A third comparison contract (agreement of relational
  records) should join the "name the comparison" list in `sec:setting`.
- **Existing, E_b: needs a replacement subject.** If standpoints are clock
  readings, "what exists from b" becomes the reduced state of the rest conditional
  on a clock value (Page–Wootters; Trinity's clock-neutral vs Schrödinger
  contrast), and pointy-vs-bow-tie becomes a choice of reduction map, not of
  inventory. If standpoints are configurations (Barbour), "from b" has no
  analogue and the paper's standpoint apparatus is parochial to classical GR.
- **Being true at a standpoint: transposes in structure, not support.** Policy L
  is the conditional interpretation's policy; policy S the unconditional one. The
  paper's finding that designating a realization is a debt matches the PW
  outcome-assignment problem (Anderson 4.2-1/2). Cite, do not re-derive.

**Revisions owed.** `main.tex`: (i) `sec:setting`/abstract need a scope statement:
all four relations are classical-GR notions; a fundamental theory without events
removes their domain rather than refining them; (ii) `sec:revise`'s "Clock-equipped
view" row ("changes the category") is naive as written — the recollapse
non-monotonicity obstruction applies to the paper's own closed dust model after
τ = π, and the dust/unimodular route draws the preferred-foliation critique; (iii)
`sec:direction`'s "three candidates" omit two defended ones: the BKM record
arrow (an arrow from a time-symmetric law with no special initial conditions —
though about typical solutions, not exact fixing, so Proposition `prop:mirror` is
untouched) and thermal time; the section's claim that the slogan "needs another
source" should register that defended sources exist which the paper does not
count; (iv) `sec:defeat` omits the most radical defeater — a fundamental theory
whose ontology contains no classical events; (v) `sec:truth` should cite
Page–Wootters as the standing formalization nearest policy L.
Notes: `stein-gr/local-clock` should state its kinship with Kuchař's internal-time
programme (uncited) and the real difference — germ-monotone on-shell clocks are
not off-shell phase-space functionals with {T, H} ≈ 1 — and note that its
curvature-clock class is by construction a class on which a local clock exists,
the local escape from obstruction (b); `time-direction/fixing-exchange` is
correct, but its closing "We have not proved it" reads as if the quantum
conditional-probability terrain were empty — Page–Wootters and Trinity are its
nearest neighbours and should be cited; `horizons/horizons-and-news` and
`cone-algebras` already flag "nothing here addresses gravity's own degrees of
freedom"; the natural addition is that the state-dependent time the cone-algebras
note leaves open is precisely thermal time. Nothing in the paper or notes is
*wrong* by the standards of this literature, but the paper's silence on whether
its relations have quantum analogues is a real gap: its own thesis is that the
four relations come apart, and this literature is where that separation would be
stress-tested.

## 5. Ten primary sources to read next

1. **Kuchař 1991** (reprint Int. J. Mod. Phys. D 20 (2011) 3–86,
   doi:10.1142/S0218271811019347). Settles the ten attempts and the recollapse
   obstruction first-hand; whether the repo's local-clock theorem adds anything.
2. **Rovelli 1991**, Phys. Rev. D 43, 442, against **Hájíček 1991**, PRD 44, 1337.
   Settles exactly what failed in the original conditional construction — the
   benchmark any repo analogue must beat.
3. **DeWitt 1967**, Phys. Rev. 160, 1113. Settles what the frozen formalism
   originally claimed, before print use in the repo.
4. **Gryb–Thébault 2015**, "Time Remains" (arXiv:1408.2691), full text. Settles
   the exact relation between relative variation and succession — the sharpest
   direct rival to the paper's becoming/fixed separation.
5. **Connes–Rovelli 1994** (arXiv:gr-qc/9406019), full text. Settles thermal
   time's exact hypotheses and whether it composes with the repo's
   entropy-imbalance results.
6. **Page–Wootters 1983**, PRD 27, 2885. Settles the conditional scheme's
   assumptions at the source (touches the truth section).
7. **Barbour 1994**, "The timelessness of quantum gravity, I", CQG 11, 2853
   (doi:10.1088/0264-9381/11/12/005), plus part II. Settles the quantum
   configuration-space construction behind the essay.
8. **Butterfield–Isham**, "Spacetime and the philosophical challenge of quantum
   gravity" (arXiv:gr-qc/9903072), full text. Settles the per-programme treatment
   of the problem of time — the map for the paper's bridge premises.
9. **Huggett–Wüthrich 2022**, *Out of Nowhere* (OUP; use open arXiv chapters,
   e.g. 2101.06955). Settles the emergence criterion the repo's relations would
   need to meet in the emerged regime.
10. **Gomes 2017**, "Quantum gravity in timeless configuration space" (arXiv:
    1706.08875). Settles whether a path integral on configuration space supplies
    the missing quantum dynamics — the strongest current answer to "what replaces
    fixing".
