Read depths follow knowledge/README.md and are labelled per source. Primary texts were read

## 1. Main positions

- **Boltzmannian typicality** (Goldstein; also Lebowitz). Entropy increase away from a
  non-equilibrium macrostate is typical under the dynamical laws *conditional on* a special
  low-entropy macrocondition; no proof of one-sided increase follows from the laws alone.
  Sources: Goldstein 2001, arXiv:cond-mat/0105242v1, and GTZ 2016, arXiv:1602.05601v3 —
  existing full-text repo records (knowledge/arrow-cosmology/sources.jsonl, IDs
  `arrow-goldstein-boltzmann-2001`, `arrow-gtz-no-ph-2016`); not re-read this session.
- **The Mentaculus package** (Albert 2000; Loewer 2011). Time-reversal-invariant dynamics +
  the Past Hypothesis (the universe began in a very-low-entropy macrocondition) + the
  statistical postulate (uniform measure over that macrocondition's realizers) account
  together for the thermodynamic, epistemic (records), causal and counterfactual arrows.
  Albert's book was **not accessed**; reconstructed from Callender SEP §§4.1–4.2 (full-text,
  secondary), North 2011 (full-text, below), and Loewer 2011, "The emergence of time's
  arrows and special science laws from physics", Interface Focus (PhilSci/Royal Society
  PDF; abstract and introduction read).
- **Wallace's derivation-first version** (Wallace 2011, "The logic of the past hypothesis",
  PhilSci preprint; printed in *The Probability Map of the Universe*, Harvard UP 2023).
  Full-text read. Some Past-Hypothesis-type posit is necessary for irreversible
  macrodynamics, but it is "not correctly understood as a constraint on the early
  Universe's entropy": it is an objective probability distribution over the initial state
  (the "Simple Past Hypothesis"), or in quantum theory the requirement that the initial
  universal state be "Simple", which under stated assumptions "amounts to" a Past *Law*
  Hypothesis.
- **PH as a fundamental law** (Loewer; Eddy Keming Chen 2023). Chen's thesis "Nomic Status":
  the Past Hypothesis "is a candidate fundamental law of nature", despite being
  non-dynamical; his "Wentaculus" incorporates it into a time-reversal-invariant quantum
  law. Chen 2023, arXiv:2008.00611 (abstract and introduction read).
- **Anti-law and anti-PH positions.** (i) Earman 2006, "The 'Past Hypothesis': Not even
  false" (SHPMP 37, 399–430 — paywalled, **not accessed**; reconstructed only from Callender
  SEP §3.1 and Chen 2023's summary): the PH cannot be stated in the language of general
  relativity, so as stated it is "not even coherent enough to be false". (ii) Frisch 2010,
  "From Arbuthnot to Boltzmann" (PhilSci 5204; extraction garbled, abstract-level only): if
  best-system lawhood licenses the PH, special-science laws earn their place no less — the
  Humean licence is more generous than its users admit. (iii) Dorst 2023, "Does the Best
  System Need the Past Hypothesis?" (*Philosophy of Science*; abstract read): recent BSA
  developments "render the Past Hypothesis otiose".
- **Price: one-asymmetry conception, double standard, Gold universe.** The thermodynamic
  puzzle needs exactly one temporal asymmetry, the low-entropy condition; two-asymmetry
  accounts are redundant; and "if something overrides them at one end of the universe, what
  right do we have to assume that the same does not happen at the other?" — so a smooth
  recollapsing (Gold) universe stays a live option and the residual question "becomes a
  puzzle for cosmology". Price 2004, "The Thermodynamic Arrow: Puzzles and Pseudo-puzzles",
  arXiv:physics/0402040 (large sections read); the book *Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point*
  (1996) was **not accessed**; see also Price 1995, "Cosmology, time's arrow, and that old
  double standard" (in *Time's Arrow Today*, CUP; metadata verified, not read).
- **Penrose's Weyl curvature hypothesis**: a time-asymmetric geometric restriction
  (vanishing Weyl curvature) at the initial singularity as the physical face of the low
  initial gravitational entropy; Penrose himself left the entropy functional open.
  Source: existing full-text repo record `arrow-penrose-wch-1979`; not re-read.
- **Arrows without a past boundary** (Carroll–Chen 2004, arXiv:hep-th/0410270; abstract and
  introduction read; existing abstract repo record `arrow-carroll-chen-2004`): from de
  Sitter vacuum, spontaneous inflation raises entropy without bound "both forwards and
  backwards in time", giving a universe "statistically time-symmetric on ultra-large
  scales", with local arrows pointing away from an entropy minimum.
- **Radiation arrow** (Callender SEP §3.2, full-text; North 2011, full-text-partial; Zeh —
  book, **not accessed**, metadata only). The Ritz–Einstein debate (1909) frames it: Ritz
  took the electromagnetic asymmetry as lawlike and prior; Einstein held irreversibility is
  "exclusively based on reasons of probability". Modern consensus per Callender: the wave
  asymmetry is thermodynamic, but electromagnetism has several distinct arrows
  (retarded/advanced, emission/absorption, damping) and "it is premature to say that the
  thermodynamic arrow is the one arrow to rule them all". Frisch's monograph treatment was
  not accessible; Frisch and Pietsch 2016 correct the standard Ritz–Einstein story (per
  Callender SEP §3.2).

## 2. Canonical arguments

**(A) The reversibility and recurrence objections (Loschmidt 1876/77; Zermelo 1896).**
Premises: (1) the dynamics is time-reversal invariant; (2) bounded Hamiltonian dynamics is
recurrent. Conclusion: no state function can increase monotonically; for every
entropy-increasing solution there is a mirror entropy-decreasing solution, and every
solution eventually decreases. Standard objection to Boltzmann's H-theorem: it "smuggled"
in an unannounced time-asymmetric premise (molecular-stochastic assumption). Boltzmann's
mature reply — statistical typicality plus a low-entropy boundary — converts the objection
into the problem of the condition itself. Reconstructed at secondary depth (Callender SEP
§2, read in full); primary sources not read; Goldstein 2001 (repo full-text record) gives
the Boltzmannian statement of the reply.

**(B) The retrodiction problem and the Past Hypothesis (Albert).**
Premises: (1) dynamics TRI; (2) the statistical postulate applied at a present macrostate;
(3) no condition at the past end. Conclusion: entropy is overwhelmingly likely to rise
toward *both* temporal sides (Albert's partly-melted popsicle case, as North 2011 recounts
it), so the account predicts anti-thermodynamic pasts that never occur. Repair: add the PH
at one end (and only then infer records, and from records the past). Standard objections:
(i) **subsystem worry** (Winsberg 2004; discussion Callender SEP §3.1): global low entropy
does not obviously give local branch-system behaviour; (ii) **double standard** (Price
2004): positing the condition at one end only is warranted only if something rules out the
other end; (iii) **coherence in GR** (Earman 2006, secondary). Depth: Albert's own text not
read; reconstruction secondary + North (full-text).

**(C) Wallace's derivation chain.**
Premises: (1) TRI microdynamics; (2) a coarse-graining C; (3) the "Simple Dynamical
Conjecture" — the coarse-grained forward dynamics is macrodeterministic enough; (4) the
Simple Past Hypothesis — an objective probability distribution over the initial state.
Conclusion: the initial state is forward-predictable; irreversible macrodynamics is
derived. Two marked corollaries: a PH construed as an *entropy* constraint is the wrong
posit; and in quantum theory the posit can be read as a law specifying the initial state.
Standard objections: Brown 2017 (role of objective probability "obscure" in both Albert's
and Wallace's versions — abstract read); Frisch 2010 (special-science consistency);
Dorst 2023 (modern best systems may not need the PH at all).

**(D) Price's one-asymmetry model vs two-asymmetry models.**
Premises: (1) the statistics underlying the second law are time-symmetric; (2) an
explanation of the thermodynamic asymmetry may posit a low-entropy condition at one end;
(3) no independent second asymmetry (e.g. a lawlike radiation direction) has earned its
place. Conclusion: the remaining puzzle is the ordered distribution of matter in the early
universe — "a puzzle for cosmology" — and healthy "scepticism about the universality of the
second law" is warranted in a recollapsing or Gold universe. Standard objection: the
anti-thermodynamic processes ruled out seem "absolutely forbidden", unlike Popper's
mechanically attainable time-reversed pond (Callender SEP §3.2's contrast), and defenders
of PH-as-law respond that a lawful posit has probability one, not "monstrous" improbability
(Callender SEP §3.1).

**(E) Earman's coherence attack (secondary reconstruction only — label explicitly).**
Premises (as Callender SEP §3.1 and Chen 2023 report them): (1) GR has no general
phase-space volume or Boltzmann entropy for the universe; (2) the PH is stated as an
entropy claim; conclusion: the PH is not well-formed — "not even false". Responses in the
literature: Callender 2010/2011 and Wallace 2010 (existing repo full-text record
`arrow-wallace-gravity-entropy-2009`) on self-gravitation; quantum formulations (Wallace
2013/2023, Chen 2023/2024, per Callender SEP §3.1). **The primary paper was not accessed;
do not cite its wording from here.**

## 3. Live open problems, and what would count as progress

1. **Stating the PH in general relativity.** Progress: an agreed gravitational entropy
   functional and measure on GR state spaces under which a low-entropy-past claim is
   either well-formed (answering Earman) or provably ill-formed (vindicating him).
2. **The lawhood of the PH.** Progress: a verdict under a named account of laws —
   best-system (Dorst 2023 against need; Loewer/Chen for), universalism (against, per
   Callender SEP §3.9), or Chen's quantum state-law route. Chen's "nomic vagueness"
   problem (what exactly the law says) is the same worry as Earman's in a quantum key.
3. **Global-to-local: the subsystem problem.** Progress: a derivation of branch-system
   thermodynamic behaviour from the global posit (Winsberg 2004's challenge; responses by
   Albert 2025, Callender 2011b, Frisch 2010, North 2011 per Callender SEP §3.1).
4. **Posit vs explanation of the low entropy.** Progress: either a dynamical derivation of
   the low-entropy boundary (Carroll–Chen inflation; Penrose's WCH as a geometric posit;
   loop-quantum-cosmology steps cited by Chen 2023) or a settled argument that no
   explanation is demanded (Callender 2004a,b; contra Price 1996/2004; Baras & Shenker
   2020 diagnose the disagreement — all as cited in Callender SEP §3.1, not read).
5. **Partition and measure relativity of openness claims.** Any claim that "the future is
   more open" measured by conditional entropies inherits the coarse-graining choice
   (Callender SEP §4.1 records how Reichenbach's "quasi-entropy" move already conceded
   this). Progress: a named partition + measure + boundary posit with the asymmetry
   proved, and its robustness across coarse-grainings checked.

## 4. What in this repository depends on this area

**research/what-the-past-can-fix/main.tex, §sec:direction (lines 526–577).**

- *The Albert sentence (line 571) is misleading as written and should be revised.* "Statistics
  over a present macrocondition make entropy higher on both temporal sides" conflates two
  claims: (i) the retrodiction problem — *inference* from a present macrocondition favours
  higher entropy on both sides; (ii) entropy being actually higher on both sides, which
  contradicts the PH itself. Albert's position also needs its second half: the records
  argument (epistemic arrow) is what the PH is meant to buy, per Callender SEP §4.1–4.2.
  The same sentence's page cite (p. 96) and the paper's other Albert cites (pp. 113–116;
  pp. 121–122 n. 11) are unverified here — the book was not accessible; check against a copy.
- *"Albert adds a law-like posit" is defensible but incomplete.* Albert and Loewer do treat
  the PH as law-candidate; but a defender (Wallace) would reject the paper's formulation
  "began in a particular low-entropy macrocondition" as the wrong posit — his derivation
  needs a probability distribution, "not [a] constraint on the early Universe's entropy"
  (full-text). The section should register that the PH's canonical formulation is itself
  contested (Wallace 2011; Chen 2023; Dorst 2023).
- *The mirror-failure claim (line 577, "If L includes the Past-Hypothesis it is not reversal
  invariant, and Proposition [mirror] fails") holds for the classical entropy-constraint
  formulation, but is formulation-relative*: Chen's Wentaculus builds a
  time-reversal-invariant law incorporating a PH-type posit (abstract+introduction read).
  Add a caveat or restrict to the classical package.
- *The conditional-entropy sentence (line 577) is right but under-cited, and "We have not
  proved it" is now outdated relative to the fixing note.* The identity
  H(c|a)−H(a|c)=H(c)−H(a) is a textbook identity; the two-directional predictability of
  stationary processes is Watanabe's classic theme (Rev. Mod. Phys. 27, 179 (1955);
  metadata verified only) and is stated in Crutchfield–Ellison–Mahoney, arXiv:0902.1209
  (abstract read; the fixing note credits the full text). The *use* of the identity to
  measure "openness of a base" is the project's bridge premise, not a result in this
  literature; nearest named positions are Albert's records account of the epistemic arrow
  and CEM's excess entropy/crypticity — which measures stored *memory*, and is symmetric
  for stationary processes. That is the honest status: right as a stipulated measure,
  elementary as mathematics, and not said in this form by anyone this pass could find.
- *Keep, with confidence:* Proposition [mirror], Proposition [balance], Corollary [epoch].
  They are elementary, and nothing in the literature read contradicts them. The paper's
  disclaimer that the closed-dust arrow "reverses at maximum expansion, so it is not the
  asymmetry of ordinary thought" matches the field's caution — and can be strengthened:
  by the paper's own guarantee machinery plus the horizons note, in a big-bang universe
  like ΛCDM there is no midpoint; the future of an early standpoint contains complete
  Cauchy slices while its past hides regions behind the particle horizon
  (horizons-and-news/RESULT.md §1–2). Whether the massive-law fixing corollary transfers
  to ΛCDM is unproved in the repo; flag, do not assert.
- *The abstractions and §sec:revise item "``Past fixed, future open'' as evidence for
  growth" (line 741) are supported by this literature*, not damaged: the thermodynamic
  grounding of the records asymmetry (Albert; Callender SEP §4) is exactly the
  "candidate explanation that uses no inventory distinction" the paper invokes. Cite
  Albert 2000 and Callender's SEP entry concretely at that spot.
