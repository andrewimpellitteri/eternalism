## What the lanes ask of the project

None found a proposition wrong. In order of how much I trust the finding:

1. **"Persistence" is the wrong word** (persistence lane; confirmed in the paper). The
   paper uses it for realms that accumulate. In the literature it means an object
   continuing to exist, and in that sense "a presentist denies persistence" is false:
   presentist endurantists exist. "Stage" and "constitution" collide in the same way.
   A rename to "permanence" or "cumulativity" fixes it without touching any result.
2. **The attitudes route is missing from the rivals** (value lane; Prior confirmed).
   The item "past fixed, future open as evidence for growth" lists records and
   influence. Relief and future bias are the other standard route, and the worker's
   reading is that it does not establish a tensed ontology either.
3. **The clock-rival passage takes a side** (history lane; passage confirmed). Counting
   a metric-definable clock as "no added structure" is the substantivalist's
   bookkeeping. A relationist counts any clock as matter. One sentence would say so.
4. **"Production" is two things** (causation lane; from abstracts and secondary
   sources). Tooley's primitive relation carries a direction. Conserved-quantity
   production does not. The causal-production note should say which it means.
5. **The thermodynamic paragraph skips a step** (causation lane; secondary only). The
   move from records to a past base is the causal-handle argument of Albert, Kutach
   and Loewer, with known objections. None of those authors was opened by the worker.
6. **Uncited neighbours.** McTaggart's dilemma for the "defined or primitive"
   diagnostic; the persistence literature for Part IV; Elga for the mirror
   proposition; Rugh and Zinkernagel for the local-clock conclusion.
