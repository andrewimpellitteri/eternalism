## Frequency sectors, conditioning and normalization: cross-cutting cautions

1. Trinity nonideality means nonorthogonal/overcomplete covariant POVM clock states and non-full spectral support, not arbitrary clock instruments. The formal equivalence depends on each paper’s reduction map and physical inner product.
2. The relativistic paper’s two signs are separate sectors. Results for each sector cannot be silently promoted to coherent sector superpositions; its probability discussion explains why sector bookkeeping matters.
3. Interactions are not an unoccupied territory: Smith–Ahmadi derive a kernel equation; Rijavec treats a restricted conserved, clock-independent interaction class; 2607.24976 gives instrument-based interaction treatment and a concrete finite non-local-clock example. Mendes et al. (arXiv:2107.11452; abstract checked) specifically study mixed-state motion under gravitationally induced interaction with a non-ideal clock. Chataignier et al. (arXiv:2409.06479; abstract checked) prove a periodic-clock relational trinity and a failure of the original PW conditional-probability definition for continuous system spectrum. These are novelty falsifiers; abstract-level leads are not theorem verification.
4. No result in this matrix entails presentism, eternalism, objective passage, or a thermodynamic arrow. Clock-relative parameterization and causal order are separate from those claims.

## Candidate question (unverified; do not call an open problem)

For a constraint `C=H_C+H_S+H_int≈0` and covariant clock instrument effects `E_t`, ask whether one can characterize conditions for a possibly non-local clock coordinate `q` to support autonomous relational dynamics. Before defining a candidate propagator, the contract must specify (i) one common reduced state space/image `K` for all `q`; (ii) reduction maps `R_q:H_phys→K` that are injective on the declared physical-state domain (or explicitly restrict to a common invariant subdomain on which inverses exist); (iii) the physical inner product on `H_phys` and the induced inner product on `K`, together with proof of norm preservation/isometry rather than just formal invertibility; and (iv) an explicit, fixed set of clock effects, system observables, and allowed preparations/access operations across compared frames. Then define, only on that common image,

`U_eff(q₂,q₁) := R_{q₂} (R_{q₁}|_{D})^{-1}`.

The question is whether/when this is a well-defined isometry on `K` generated by a single time-independent self-adjoint `H_eff`, rather than merely a parameter-dependent relabeling or a memory-kernel evolution. Here `D⊂H_phys` is the common domain and the inverse is only the inverse onto `R_{q₁}(D)`, with the additional requirement that `R_{q₂}(D)=R_{q₁}(D)=K` (or a separately justified common identified image). These are prerequisites to posing the research question, not established properties. A first bounded test is the finite Abelian model of arXiv:2607.24976v1; compare its center-of-mass frame to a finite-group family `V(X₁−X₂)` while explicitly retaining the group-average/physical inner product, measurement probabilities, and fixed accessible-observable restrictions. Without these contracts the expression may be undefined or amount to a vacuous reparameterization.

**Novelty falsifier:** an existing theorem in Smith–Ahmadi, Rijavec, the periodic-clock trinity, Mendes et al., or the 2026 instrument/algebraic QRF literature already gives necessary and sufficient conditions equivalent to this classification; or every apparent difference is removed by a frame/representation change preserving the same accessible probabilities. Do not implement until forward citations and the relevant full texts are checked.
