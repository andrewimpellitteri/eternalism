# Philosophy of time: dense research map

Status: living research infrastructure, 2026-10-19; broad but not exhaustive.
This is not a completed systematic review, a new theorem, or a verdict on time.
It extends the narrower [foundations audit](foundations/audit.md),
[frontier theorem matrix](frontier/theorem-matrix.md), and
[formalization audit](formalization/code-audit.md). Those files retain their
own evidential limits. This map does not complete the gated `theory-map` task.

Sources: short IDs `phil-*` refer to [the added source register](philosophy/sources.jsonl).
Existing `found-*`, `frontier-*`, and `form-*` IDs refer to their lane registers.
Publisher abstracts establish an author's stated project, not that the argument
succeeds. Historical texts are not contemporary empirical evidence. Schematic
arguments below are coordinator reconstructions unless explicitly attributed.

## 1. Starting method: constraints without reduction

Adopt **physics-constrained, non-reductive metaphysics** provisionally: empirical
success is a powerful constraint, not a rule that every meaningful philosophical
question reduces to an experiment. This stance is itself contestable, not a
neutral result of physics. Keep realist, structuralist, functionalist, and
instrumentalist interpretations distinguishable when they affect an inference.

Working commitments: fallibilism; explicit meanings and inference rules;
empirical adequacy within a theory's tested regime; source correspondence;
separation of mathematical consistency, physical possibility, actual truth,
and explanatory preference; defeasible simplicity; serious treatment of rivals.
Do not assume physicalism, verificationism, causal closure, Humean laws,
truthmaker maximalism, a global present, or the unreality of passage as defaults.
Classical logic is a useful initial proof language, not a discovery that tense,
future contingents, or quantifier domains must have classical semantics.

Main reference spine: Callender's edited *Oxford Handbook of Philosophy of
Time* (`phil-handbook`), not a single author's system. Contrast Callender's
*What Makes Time Special?* (`phil-callender`) with Maudlin's *The Metaphysics
Within Physics* (`phil-maudlin`); include direct defenders of presentism,
growing block, and moving spotlight rather than rely on opponents' summaries.

Expert views guide reading priorities, not truth scores. The 2020 PhilPapers
temporal-ontology question reports approximately 39.92% accepting/leaning
eternalism, 18.39% presentism, and 17.03% growing block among 734 answers after
specified exclusions. Endorsements can overlap; this is neither a probability
distribution over truth nor all philosophers' consensus (`phil-survey`). Do not
double-count physical arguments through both expert opinion and their evidence.

## 2. Disentangle the questions before comparing answers

| Axis | Question | Do not identify it with |
|---|---|---|
| Ontological extent | Which temporally located entities exist, unrestrictedly? | What is observable now; what a coordinate chart represents |
| Tense | Are past/present/future facts irreducible? | Whether natural language needs indexicals |
| Passage/becoming | Is there objective acquisition of presentness, existence, or definiteness? | Change, elapsed proper time, entropy increase |
| Openness | Is the future unsettled ontically, semantically, epistemically, or practically? | Indeterminism without specifying which sense |
| Determinism | Do laws and suitable complete data select a unique history, modulo the relevant equivalence? | Eternalism, predictability, fatalism |
| Persistence | How does an object exist through different times? | Whether nonpresent objects exist |
| Direction | What distinguishes earlier/later or causal/entropic orientation? | A moving present |
| Geometry | What order, metric, topology, dimension, and causal structure does time have? | A metaphysical doctrine about reality |
| Fundamentality | Is time primitive, reduced, emergent, or multiply realized? | Time being nonexistent or useless |
| Experience | How are succession, duration, change and anticipation presented? | A direct measurement of fundamental passage |
| Laws/modality | Are laws descriptive, governing, dispositional, or otherwise? | A temporal ontology entailed by a label |

Represent a candidate as a **package**: physical model/version + ontology +
tense/passage account + laws/boundaries + semantics + bridge rules + observer
access. Compare packages, not words such as “block” or “quantum gravity.”

## 3. Ontology and passage: principal packages and their burdens

| Position | Core commitment, with variants left open | Main burden / strongest reply to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Presentism | Only present concrete entities exist; abstract/ersatz times need separate treatment. | Define presentness and quantification; reconcile relativity; ground past truth and cross-time relations. Replies revise truthmaking, use primitive tense/ersatz structure, add preferred structure, or reject simultaneity as ontological unity. |
| Eternalism | Past, present and future entities have unrestricted ontological standing; not all are located at this moment. | Explain temporal experience, indexicality and asymmetries without illicitly assuming the conclusion. It need not entail deterministic laws, absence of genuine change, or temporal parts of ordinary objects. |
| Growing block / no-futurism | Past and present exist, future entities do not yet; versions differ over tense, domains, and what growth means. | Why believe oneself at the latest edge rather than in an existing past? Replies must specify consciousness, tensed actuality, knowledge and the status of past observers—not merely rename the edge. |
| Moving spotlight | A complete temporal ontology plus objectively distinguished presentness whose location changes. | Explain change of presentness, epistemic access and relativistic compatibility. A second temporal parameter is one modeling option, not a requirement established by the name. |
| Local/relativistic becoming | Definiteness/becoming is event- or observer-relative, often linked to causal past, rather than one global spatial present. | Show that the invariant relation supports becoming rather than merely redescribing causal order; distinguish observer-relative knowledge from ontic definiteness. |
| Fragmentalism | Irreducibly tensed reality need not assemble into one globally coherent, privileged description; Fine's framework requires exact reconstruction. | Specify fragment-relative truth/compatibility and avoid both global contradiction and trivial contextual relativism. Not a consequence of SR. |
| Minimal/primitive passage views | A temporal asymmetry or primitive becoming can be fundamental without the familiar presentist or spotlight package. | State what primitive structure adds, which explanation it earns, and whether it has independent consequences. |
| Process / event ontologies | Events, processes, or production are basic rather than persisting substances. | Give a particular account of identity, laws and temporal order. “Process” is a broad family, not synonymous with presentism or a tested physical dynamics. |
| Temporal anti-realism / eliminativism | Some or all temporal structure is denied at the fundamental level. | Distinguish elimination of time, reduction of its roles, and denial only of robust passage; recover evidence, records and effective dynamics. |

Defender/critic spine: Bourne (`phil-bourne`), Correia–Rosenkranz
(`phil-growing-block`), Cameron (`phil-cameron`), Skow (`phil-skow`), Fine
(`phil-fine`), Braddon-Mitchell (`phil-latest`), and the existing Stein,
Maxwell, Brading, Putnam and Malament records. Books are mostly abstract or
discovery depth here; their complete replies remain reading tasks.

## 4. Tense, McTaggart, and indexical knowledge

McTaggart distinguishes **A determinations** (past/present/future), **B
relations** (earlier/later), and a **C series** (order without temporal
direction). His challenge combines: time requires genuine change; mere fixed
B relations do not deliver the required change; A determinations are necessary;
A determinations are incompatible yet every event acquires them; invoking
further tenses yields a regress. Deny or reconstruct one premise at a time.
Do not compress this into “past, present and future coexist, therefore time is
contradictory.” Source: `phil-mctaggart`; transcription selected sections read.

B-theoretic reply: variation in properties at different times suffices for
change; no event must change its own temporal location. A-theoretic replies:
tense operators are primitive; A properties are not jointly possessed in the
same respect; the purported regress is not vicious. Consistency of a formal
tense model refutes only an alleged contradiction with corresponding premises;
it does not prove the model describes reality. Contemporary “C-theory” proposals
must not automatically be equated with McTaggart's original C series.

**Tensed language ≠ tensed world:** “the operation is over” can be cognitively
and practically different from a dated description even if their truth
conditions are related. Prior's relief argument (`phil-prior`) motivates a
problem, not an automatic entailment from attitudes to presentism. Perry's
essential-indexical argument (`phil-perry`) helps separate world knowledge from
self-location. B-theorists may retain irreducible indexical thought without
fundamental monadic presentness; A-theorists must explain why the phenomenon
requires more than self-locating content. Mellor (`phil-mellor`) is a systematic
tenseless-theory comparator, not the adjudicated winner.

## 5. Existence, truthmakers, and persistence

First distinguish “exists at time t” / “is present” from unrestricted “exists
simpliciter.” Possibility, representation and grounding are separate statuses
or explanatory relations, not interchangeable senses of existence. Otherwise
“only present things exist now”
can be a triviality about location or a substantive denial of nonpresent
entities. Distinguish objectual quantification from tense operators and actual
past events from presently existing representations of them.

**Past-truth challenge:** combine (i) presentism, (ii) objective past truths,
(iii) a truthmaker principle requiring suitable existent grounds. The apparent
conflict is conditional on the exact grounding principle. Options include
primitive past-tensed facts, presently instantiated historical properties,
ersatz times, and rejecting/restricting truthmaker maximalism. Each needs an
account of explanatory adequacy and accidental agreement; a memory trace can
be evidence without being a metaphysically sufficient truthmaker. Do not infer
that presentists deny dinosaurs or that eternalists automatically solve every
truthmaking problem. Bourne is a direct defense; full evaluation is pending.

**Persistence:** endurantism treats the same object as wholly present at times
when it exists; perdurantism gives an object temporal parts; stage theory treats
ordinary-object reference through stages and counterpart relations. Temporary
intrinsics, coincidence, fission, identity and relations to nonpresent objects
are key pressure points. Eternalism does not simply entail perdurantism;
endurantism does not simply entail presentism. A four-dimensional spacetime is
not by itself a thesis about the composition of tables. Use explicit definitions
of “3D/4D” rather than infer from terminology. See `phil-sider` and the secondary
orientation `phil-sep-parts`.

## 6. Future contingents, branching, freedom and laws

Separate: truth from settled truth; metaphysical necessity from nomic necessity;
determinism from predictability; epistemic uncertainty from ontic openness;
indeterministic chances from agency. Classical excluded middle does not simply
equal semantic bivalence. A future-tensed proposition can be evaluated relative
to a time/history pair while settlement quantifies over histories through that
time. A single actual complete history is compatible with laws assigning
nontrivial chances before outcomes; its representation does not erase those
chances by definition.

Aristotle's sea-battle problem targets the move from the truth of a future
claim to its inevitability. Schematic illicit move: from `p` infer `necessarily
p`, or move necessity from a conditional to its consequent. Exactly which
fatalist argument fails depends on its account of truth, fixity of the past,
ability, and modal scope. Do not claim that rejecting fatalism requires denying
logic, or that a formal branching tree establishes an objectively open future.
Belnap's branching space-times (`phil-belnap`) supplies a distinct formal
framework; histories, choice points and causal structure need interpretation.

**Laws:** Humean best-system, governing/primitivist, powers, dispositional and
constraint-based accounts impose different explanatory burdens. None gets an
ontology of time for free. Time-symmetric laws can have asymmetric boundary
conditions; deterministic evolution need not privilege past over future;
stochastic collapse need not imply presentism. If a paper claims a law ontology
entails a temporal ontology, extract the bridging argument rather than accept
the pairing as consensus. Maudlin is a useful anti-reductionist counterweight;
Earman's determinism work is a further primary reading priority.

## 7. Passage and the rate/regress objections

Require a model to identify what changes: properties of ordinary systems,
presentness of events, an existential domain, local definiteness, or conscious
contents. These are different proposals. “One second per second” can be a
trivial dimensional identity, an answer in a specified model, or a category
mistake; it is not a universal disproof of passage. A second time parameter
requires interpretation and may shift the problem, but no infinite hierarchy
follows unless its movement is required by further premises. Skow explicitly
addresses consistency, rate and relativity objections (`phil-skow`); Cameron
defends a different spotlight account (`phil-cameron`). Their conclusions
must not be conflated.

**Growth challenge:** is an enlarging history a physically new universe, a
sequence of descriptions of one completed history, or a bookkeeping order on
events? A bijection between descriptions alone settles none of this. The
comparison needs dynamics, stochastic measures, allowed interventions,
accessible observables and a bridge from the formal domain to existence.
**Spotlight challenge:** if all records and experiences remain unchanged when
the spotlight location changes, explain knowledge of it and why it is required.
This is an explanatory/epistemic challenge, not automatically inconsistency.

## 8. Geometry and the meaning of time

Distinguish order, metric duration, topology, orientation and operational clocks.
Earlier-than may be partial rather than total; spacelike separation is not an
unknown chronological order. Questions about continuity/discreteness,
branching, cyclicity, endpoints, closed timelike curves and multiple temporal
dimensions are distinct and theory-dependent. A finite past proper length or
singular/inextendible classical model does not establish creation from nothing
or settle a metaphysical “before.”

Relationism asks whether temporal facts reduce to relations among events or
processes; substantivalism assigns an independent spacetime role, with modern
sophisticated versions not reducible to Newton's container picture. Structural
realism emphasizes relational/geometric structure; functionalism emphasizes
roles and their realizers. A coordinate parameter is not a substance; a
relational measurement is not a proof of relationism. Shoemaker's time-without-
change argument is a targeted challenge about modal possibility, not an observed
freeze of the universe (`phil-shoemaker`).

The GR hole argument concerns diffeomorphically related models, identity and
determinism: do not turn it into “coordinates are arbitrary, therefore nothing
exists.” Gauge status must be justified through the theory's structures and
observables, not stipulated whenever metaphysical distinctions are inconvenient.
Primary hole-argument and sophisticated-substantivalist texts remain a gap.

## 9. Special relativity: constraint and bridge ledger

In standard units, a boost gives `Δt′ = γ(Δt − vΔx/c²)` with `|v|<c` and
`γ = 1/sqrt(1−v²/c²)`. For suitably oriented spacelike separations, inertial
frames can reverse coordinate time order; proper-orthochronous transformations
preserve future timelike/causal orientation. Proper time is a worldline
quantity, not a universal coordinate clock. Frame simultaneity is an
equivalence relation within a specified inertial frame; the union of different
frames' simultaneity relations is a different object.

**Putnam/Rietdijk:** relativity of simultaneity plus observer-neutral transfers
of reality can motivate a block conclusion. Putnam's Principle III, closure
and observer assumptions do the philosophical work beyond kinematics. Rietdijk's
full proof is still unaudited. **Stein:** invariant local causal-past becoming
is a competing reconstruction; whether the relation is genuine becoming is
not decided by the geometric characterization. Our isolated OCR transcription
was too weak: universal relatedness and reflexive chronological past provide
counterexamples. Do not attribute this to the printed original without images.
**Malament:** uniqueness is conditional on causal/observer structures and
definability/invariance assumptions, not uniqueness of a metaphysical present.
The original proof remains an audit gap. See `foundations/audit.md` for details.

**Presentist replies differ:** hidden foliation; restricted/local presentness;
revision of the relation between existence and simultaneity; law-constitutive
unity (Brading). They do not form one physics model and cannot be ruled out by
one slogan. Conversely, compatibility claimed in an abstract is not established
physical adequacy. Compare complete formulations and test their added premises.

## 10. General relativity and cosmological time

Proper time along a timelike worldline remains local to a path. Existence of a
global temporal/Cauchy function requires conditions; global hyperbolicity gives
a smooth Cauchy slicing (Bernal–Sánchez, `phil-global-time`). Existence is not
uniqueness or ontological privilege. Not every solution has a useful global
present; closed-timelike-curve models are a specific obstruction. A time
orientation, time function, foliation and distinguished foliation are distinct.

FLRW cosmic time exploits a chosen symmetric cosmological model and comoving
congruence. Its physical usefulness does not automatically make its slices
metaphysically present, uniquely extend it to realistic inhomogeneous spacetime,
or eliminate the need to define which entities exist. General covariance is
not an empirical theorem that all possible added structures are absent.
Observer-coordinate covariance and particle Lorentz symmetry are distinct in
Lorentz-violating effective theories (`phil-sme`). A preferred structure can
be dynamically observable, empirically hidden in a regime, or mere description;
classify it before attaching an experiment.

## 11. Arrows: thermodynamics, causation, records, radiation, cosmology

Keep at least five arrows separate: entropy increase; records/memory;
intervention/causal asymmetry; retarded-radiation behavior; cosmological
expansion. Their alignment needs explanation, not a definition. A statistical
arrow is not an event acquiring existence. Fundamental time-reversal violation
in specified interactions does not directly explain every macroscopic arrow.
CPT invariance is not invariance under T alone, nor a claim about metaphysical
passage or macroscopic reversibility.

**Past Hypothesis programs:** low-entropy past boundary + dynamics + appropriate
statistical/typicality assumptions aim to explain asymmetric histories and
records. Specify coarse graining, measure, subsystem conditions and gravitational
entropy; do not just infer a low-entropy past from present records and then
use it circularly to justify those records. Reversibility and recurrence
objections constrain naive derivations of monotonic entropy. Price's argument
asks which explanatory questions are substantive and which smuggle in an
asymmetry (`phil-price-arrow`); Horwich connects epistemic/causal asymmetries
(`phil-horwich`). These are comparative programs, not a completed derivation.

Memory is present physical structure interpreted as evidence; evidence of a
past does not by itself entail that past entities still exist. Anticipation
and control need not be mirror images of memory and explanation. Boltzmann-
brain and self-locating puzzles challenge probabilistic cosmology and evidence
models, but are not direct proofs for a temporal ontology. Quantitative work
must select an explicit cosmology and measure.

## 12. Quantum theory: do not substitute interpretations for results

Unitary quantum evolution, measurement updates, objective-collapse dynamics,
and primitive ontologies are different ingredients. Everett's relative-state
proposal (`phil-everett`) and GRW's stochastic modification (`phil-grw`) are
not interchangeable “quantum mechanics says” premises. Deterministic unitary
state evolution does not make an observer able to predict every experienced
outcome; stochastic laws do not by themselves settle whether outcomes exist
tenselessly. An interpretive branching structure is not automatically a
branching spacetime or a growing ontological domain.

Bohmian trajectories, wavefunction dynamics and foliation choices must be
specified model by model. Hypersurface Bohm–Dirac models (`phil-bohm-foliation`)
show why the relation between foliation structure, covariance and statistics
requires analysis, not the slogan “a preferred foliation is already excluded.”
Bell-type constraints rule out combinations of specified assumptions, not
presentism or eternalism by themselves. Retrocausal/all-at-once models relax
particular causal/boundary assumptions; they require explicit probabilities,
no-signaling conditions and a treatment of fine-tuning. Do not infer backward
signaling, human influence over a fixed past, or a block ontology from a label.

**Conditioning caution:** a CP linear instrument `J` need not give a linear
normalized update `J(ρ)/tr(J(ρ))`. State-dependent selection probabilities
matter. Likewise, non-semigroup dynamics need not be CP-indivisible, and
one-time channel criteria do not settle multi-time process memory. Our known-
mathematics [sanity cases](checks/README.md) isolate these errors without
alleging that any whole research program is refuted.

## 13. Relational clocks, thermal time and quantum gravity

Page–Wootters and relational-trinity constructions compare constrained global
descriptions with conditional clock evolution under stated assumptions. A
stationary total state can contain changing correlations. “Timeless equation”
therefore does not simply mean “nothing changes.” Conversely, formal conditional
evolution is not yet an argument that all temporal reality reduces to
correlations. Interactions, physical inner products, clock POVMs, sectors,
domains, normalization and accessible operations must survive each proposed map.
Broad non-ideal, periodic and interacting-clock extensions already have prior
work; see `frontier/theorem-matrix.md`, not an invented generic gap.

Finite matrices cannot obey the exact nonzero canonical commutator
`[T,H]=iℏI` in nonzero dimension, by the trace identity. This does not rule out
finite clocks, periodic clocks, POVMs or approximate canonical relations.
Finite discretization must preserve the target effect; numerical agreement at
selected points is not an exact equivalence theorem.

Connes–Rovelli's **thermal time hypothesis** (`phil-thermal-time`) proposes a
connection between a state on an observable algebra and a modular flow. The
mathematical flow and its identification as physical time are different claims;
its parameter, normalization, and recovery of measured time need specification.
It is not the established claim that entropy increase causes time to exist.

The canonical **problem of time** includes frozen formalism, choice of clock,
observables, global-time and refoliation/multiple-choice issues. A constraint
schematically written `H Ψ=0` is not by itself an argument against time.
Relational, semiclassical, histories and records programs answer different
facets (`phil-problem-time`). No consensus quantum-gravity completion should be
assumed. Lam–Wüthrich's spacetime functionalism (`phil-functionalism`) proposes
recovering empirically relevant spacetime roles from more fundamental structure;
which roles are sufficient is a substantive philosophical commitment.

## 14. Causal sets, discreteness and becoming

A locally finite causal order is not a preferred global simultaneity surface.
Sequential growth supplies stochastic dynamics under explicit covariance and
causality conditions (Rideout–Sorkin; existing source register). Birth labels
may encode a construction order whose invariant content differs from the label
order; operationally accessible growth must be defined independently of labels.
Callender–Wüthrich's becoming analysis is a key primary read to deepen.

Do not equate causal-set theory with philosophical growing block, identify
discreteness with indeterminism, or assume that an order encodes a unique
continuum geometry without reconstruction conditions. An observational-
equivalence target must compare probability measures on the same accessible
events, not merely draw the same final poset. A classification here would need
to defeat existing covariant-growth and becoming results before being novel.

## 15. Temporal experience and the manifest image

Distinguish duration judgments, perceived succession, simultaneity judgments,
experienced change, felt motion, the specious present, anticipation/retention,
and a belief in objective flow. **Snapshot**, **retentional** and
**extensional** accounts differ over the duration/content of experiences;
they do not map one-to-one onto temporal ontologies. “The present is brief”
can concern a psychological integration window, not a spacetime hyperplane.
James (`phil-james`) is historical psychology; Husserl (`phil-husserl`) is
phenomenology; Dainton (`phil-dainton`) is a contemporary philosophical
comparator. None alone is a modern experimental consensus.

Experience is evidence requiring explanation, but an experience as of passage
does not immediately identify its metaphysical cause. Nor may a B-theorist
dismiss all inconvenient experience as illusion without explaining its
structure. Compare models that predict the same phenomenal reports using
different underlying accounts; seek discriminating cognitive predictions
without confusing a result about experience with a result about cosmic time.
Callender and Skow supply contrasting explanatory/argumentative resources.

**Agency/value:** relief that pain is past, anticipation, regret, future bias,
planning and diachronic identity can constrain a theory of practical reasoning
without entailing an objectively moving now. Distinguish rational temporal
preferences from psychological prevalence and from metaphysical asymmetry.
The growing-block “am I latest?” challenge is a self-location/knowledge problem,
not merely a complaint about an unavailable clock reading.

## 16. History and alternative philosophical framings

The Aristotle, Augustine, Newton and Leibniz sketches in this paragraph are
historical orientation pending a dedicated original-text/translation audit;
they are not newly audited arguments and carry no inferential weight here.
Use original arguments rather than retrospectively labeling every author an
A- or B-theorist. Aristotle relates time to motion and before/after without
identifying time with motion; Augustine's *Confessions* XI develops problems
of presentness, memory, expectation and measuring time; Newton distinguishes
mathematical time from sensible measures; Leibniz develops an order-of-
successions account and symmetry/indiscernibility challenges. These texts frame
questions; none has already answered relativistic clock physics.

Kant's *Critique* treats time as a form of inner sense/condition of appearances,
not simply a mistaken physical hypothesis or the assertion that clocks are
imaginary (`phil-kant`). Husserl analyzes temporal consciousness; Bergson's
heterogeneous duration challenges spatialized representations, not merely
mathematical competence (`phil-bergson`). Heideggerian/existential temporality,
Whiteheadian process, and pragmatist approaches need dedicated source audits
before they become more than coverage leads.

Non-Western/Indigenous accounts, Buddhist debates over momentariness, Madhyamaka
critiques of temporal self-existence, and classical Indian debates about time
are important missing comparators. Do not homogenize traditions, equate cyclical
ritual/historical time with closed timelike curves, or retrofit quantum physics
as confirmation of a tradition. Select a specific text, translation and
interpretive dispute first. No such primary-text audit is claimed here.

## 17. Time travel, loops and causal possibility

Separate logical/metaphysical consistency, a spacetime metric admitting a CTC,
dynamical realizability, stability, quantum consistency, and actual observation.
Lewis distinguishes personal from external time (`phil-lewis`); the familiar
grandfather puzzle requires exact modal/ability assumptions. A self-consistent
loop differs from changing an already specified past. Branching solutions can
describe a different history rather than alteration of the same past.

Bootstrap loops raise explanation/grounding issues without automatically
creating a logical contradiction. GR solutions permitting unusual causal
structure are not engineering plans; chronology-protection arguments and
energy/quantum-field assumptions require their own audit. Presentism, eternalism
and different persistence theories bear different costs here, but none wins
because a fictional narrative is easy or hard to imagine.

## 18. Argument ledger: where computation can and cannot help

| Argument / pressure | Extra premise or vulnerability | Useful bounded computation |
|---|---|---|
| SR eliminates a global present | Which symmetries must presentness respect? Is presentness a foliation? | Classify structures invariant under a specified automorphism group; construct countermodels when assumptions change. |
| Unique synchrony privileges one ontology | Definability/observer structure → ontological privilege is additional. | Audit exact theorem hypotheses and candidate relations, not an abridged quotation. |
| A complete history entails determinism | Existence/representation is confused with uniqueness under laws. | Compare solution spaces and stochastic path measures with fixed semantics. |
| Temporal experience proves flow | Phenomenology → one metaphysical explanation needs exclusion of rivals. | Specify report-level predictions and causal models; equal reports do not identify ontology. |
| Presentism cannot support past truths | Depends on truthmaking/grounding/reference commitments. | Model explicit tense/domain semantics; formal consistency is not metaphysical adequacy. |
| Growing block is epistemically inaccessible | Past observers' consciousness and knowledge conditions need definition. | Build self-location models and probability assumptions; do not count unweighted observer copies as a measure. |
| A clock construction removes fundamental time | Recovery of some roles → exhaustive reduction is a bridge. | Check preserved observables, common domains, interaction limits and counterexamples. |
| Entropy selects the true temporal ontology | A direction/record account is promoted to an existence claim. | Reproduce statistical models with explicit measures/boundaries; test alternative explanations. |
| A foliation is empirically excluded | Requires a coupling and measurable consequence, not merely extra structure. | Derive actual likelihoods/constraints for a named model and access regime. |
| Growth and a finished history are equivalent | Final-structure bijection omits dynamics, interventions or measures. | Construct measure/observable-preserving maps and identify precisely where they fail. |

These are research routes, not established open problems. Many elementary
independence and equivalence results are prior art. Review nearest predecessors
before elevating a computational exercise into a novelty claim.

## 19. Priors, evidence and philosophical comparison

For packages `M1,M2`, posterior odds equal prior odds times a likelihood ratio,
provided the hypotheses, data and background assumptions are well-defined.
Equal prior-predictive distributions *for a specified data class and access
regime* give a Bayes factor of one wherever the common likelihood is nonzero.
Pointwise observational equivalence at matched parameter values is insufficient
if parameter or auxiliary-model priors differ. This does not prove metaphysical equivalence,
equal priors, equivalence for all future interventions, or no philosophical
reasons to prefer a package. Avoid comparing maximized fit in one package with
marginalized evidence in another, and record auxiliary-model priors.

Do not assign arbitrary weighted scores to simplicity, experience, elegance
and physics and call the result truth. Use an argument/evidence ledger and
partial ordering: inconsistency under named assumptions; empirical exclusion
within scope; compatible but unsupported; empirically equivalent in scope;
explanatorily preferred conditional on contested standards; genuinely unresolved.
If numerical priors are used, publish a defensible family/range and sensitivity
analysis. A conclusion that changes with reasonable bridge choices must say so.

## 20. Source coverage, priorities and article readiness

The current corpus is uneven: strong SR argument audits and relational-clock
leads; thinner direct growing-block, moving-spotlight, phenomenological,
historical and empirical-model comparisons. Added source records deepen the
reading queue, not the number of independently verified arguments. Duplicate
seed/lane records and separate code files must not inflate distinct-work counts.

Priority order: (1) original Malament and original Stein page images; (2) full
Brading and a physics-facing presentist critic/defender pair; (3) complete
growing-block epistemic-objection/reply pair; (4) spotlight variants and Skow's
relativity treatment; (5) truthmaking/tense/branching semantics; (6) specified
preferred-structure model versus actual observations; (7) deeper arrows,
functionalism and temporal-experience comparators. Historical and cross-cultural
expansion remains a separate expert-reading responsibility, not a box checked
by a sentence here.

A working article can begin now around **conditional constraint arguments**:
physics constrains ontology-plus-bridge packages, not labels alone. The
contribution cannot merely be repeating underdetermination. A submission needs
a precise advance: a corrected source reconstruction, an explicit new boundary
classification, an independently checked counterexample, or a defensible
argument showing why a proposed bridge succeeds/fails beyond known critiques.
Separate expository claims, our argument, established mathematics, and the
unmet novelty claim. Start an outline without inventing a result section.

## 21. Required extraction record for every serious argument

Record: `id`; question; exact model/version; source IDs and loci; actual read
depth; semantic definitions; physical premises; mathematical assumptions;
philosophical bridges; conclusion; inferential steps; strongest objection;
best reply; alternative interpretation; observable consequences and access;
countermodel/falsifier; result of changing assumptions; dependencies;
verification route and bounds; closest predecessors; remaining uncertainty.

Tag premises as **assumed / derived / definitional / contested**, not just
“true.” Audit theorem parameters and unfolded definitions as well as declared
axioms. A Lean certificate establishes a formal implication, not the truth of
its physical or philosophical premises. A numerical counterexample needs a
faithful model and domain bounds; a finite search failure is not a general proof.
Subagents supply checkable artifacts and criticism, never philosophical votes.
