## What remains distinct

The project already has two elementary countermodels in
`knowledge/checks/correspondence-cases.jsonl`: universal R satisfies the isolated
four-premise OCR hypothesis, and reflexive chronological past additionally
excludes universality while missing past-null pairs. They refute those isolated
implications, not a theorem including the surrounding causal-past and unsettled
conditions. The earlier audit describes the surrounding argument's causal-past
inclusion and condition (iv), and its orientation, scale and dimension
qualifications. Those remain OCR/context evidence, not a visual transcription.

The transcription's apparent position of the theorem relative to the 149/150
page break cannot establish its printed page. No exact printed locator is
certified here. A later scan should check pp. 148–150 in sequence, not merely
search for a sentence or rely on the old p. 150 label.
