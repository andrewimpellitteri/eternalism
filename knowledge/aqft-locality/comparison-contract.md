# Comparison contract: AQFT locality and temporal claims

These are specific anchor models/versions. “Exists” reports the ontology of the mathematical model only; it does not settle what exists simpliciter. No score or ontology preference is assigned.

## 1. Minkowski Haag–Kastler vacuum net

- **Version/model:** local observable net on Minkowski spacetime as framed by Haag–Kastler (1964), with a vacuum representation and the additional assumptions stated explicitly for each theorem (not a claim that every later axiom is in their 1964 text).
- **What exists / passage / fixed future:** the mathematical model contains a net of algebras and a representation/state. No passage predicate is part of the net axioms. Future fixedness is not determined by the structural axioms alone.
- **State space, dynamics, causal structure, clocks:** states are positive normalized functionals; in a representation they give expectation values. Spacelike separated local algebras commute under Einstein causality. Minkowski causal order is explicit. A clock is a subsystem/observable plus a physical clock model; it is not supplied by local-net axioms.
- **Symmetries, preferred structure, access, boundary:** Poincaré covariance and a vacuum representation are common anchor assumptions; the vacuum is a preferred invariant state in this model, but the net framework does not make such a choice natural across arbitrary curved spacetimes. Bare `A(O)` is not a full observer-access model. Spectrum and global-vacuum boundary conditions matter for Reeh–Schlieder.
- **Predictions/status:** microcausality constrains signaling; Reeh–Schlieder entails vacuum correlations and cyclicity with the stated assumptions. These are structural results, not evidence for a temporal ontology.
- **Bridge premises / alternatives:** an interpretation may treat local algebra elements as possible measurements, but passage, presentness, event occurrence, and existence are further premises. Local becoming remains compatible with no global simultaneity at the level of the net; an eternalist interpretation is also compatible. Neither is selected by these theorems.
- **Equivalences / limits / falsifiers:** representations of the same abstract algebra can be physically equivalent in Haag–Kastler's sense despite unitary inequivalence; that is not ontological equivalence. A contrary theorem under the same axioms, or an operational model showing violation of spacelike no-signaling, would challenge the respective physical claim. An alternate axiomatization with no vacuum/spectrum condition can invalidate direct Reeh–Schlieder transfer.

## 2. Minkowski wedge algebra, vacuum state, modular flow (BW 1975 scalar-field version)

- **Version/model:** Hermitian scalar Wightman field on four-dimensional Minkowski space under the 1975 Bisognano–Wichmann field and representation conditions; a wedge von Neumann algebra must also exist and satisfy the cited cyclicity/invariance conditions.
- **What exists / passage / fixed future:** a vacuum vector, wedge algebra, and modular operators exist under hypotheses. Modular flow is a one-parameter automorphism group. Neither “the universe flows” nor “the future is fixed” is in the theorem.
- **State space, dynamics, causal structure, clocks:** vacuum and Poincaré representation are fixed inputs; wedge-preserving boosts are the geometric group. The proper time of a uniformly accelerated observer is not generally the modular parameter; the BW identification is with boosts after normalization. No clock construction appears in the theorem.
- **Symmetries, preferred structure, access, boundary:** the wedge and Poincaré representation are specified structure; vacuum is distinguished by translation invariance. Local algebra access is idealized. Wedge-duality conclusion needs added algebra generation/density/domain assumptions; the authors note these are not automatic.
- **Predictions/status:** in the specified vacuum wedge, modular automorphisms agree with Lorentz boosts; modular conjugation is geometrically related to reflection/TCP. This is an exact structural result under assumptions, not direct evidence of an experienced time flow.
- **Bridge premises / alternatives:** calling the modular parameter “time” is a naming convention unless connected to a physical clock or operational protocol. Modular flow can be interpreted as state-dependent statistical structure or geometric boost flow; neither interpretation identifies it with objective becoming without added premises.
- **Equivalences / limits / falsifiers:** mathematical modular flow is general for cyclic-separating pairs; geometric boost flow is a special BW result. A non-BW net/state may have nongeometric modular flow. The assertion that all local regions/states have such geometric flow is defeated by this domain restriction alone; no universal claim is made.

## 3. Locally covariant QFT with dynamical locality (Fewster–Verch framework)

- **Version/model:** functor from globally hyperbolic spacetimes with causally convex, orientation/time-orientation preserving isometric embeddings to unital *-algebras; time-slice and Einstein-causality properties; dynamically local theories identify kinematic and relative-Cauchy-evolution-defined local subalgebras.
- **What exists / passage / fixed future:** algebras on spacetime backgrounds, morphisms, local subalgebras, and relative Cauchy evolution. The framework is covariant and does not introduce a global present or passage law. Dynamics are represented through Cauchy evolution and response to metric perturbations, not as a metaphysical becoming primitive.
- **State space, dynamics, causal structure, clocks:** state assignments can be made per spacetime; natural states are compatible with all morphisms. Relative Cauchy evolution models response to compact metric changes. Causal structure is carried by each globally hyperbolic metric. Clock observables are model-specific and not part of the general functor axioms.
- **Symmetries, preferred structure, access, boundary:** no preferred coordinate system is required. A natural state is a highly strong across-spacetimes compatibility requirement. Locality is an algebraic/dynamical condition; observer restrictions require extra operational data.
- **Predictions/status:** Theorem 6.13 rules out a nontrivial theory under a conjunction of dynamical locality, natural state, a suitable faithful Reeh–Schlieder GNS representation on a spacetime with noncompact Cauchy surfaces, and extended locality for the final triviality conclusion. This is conditional mathematical structure, not an empirical measurement of passage.
- **Bridge premises / alternatives:** one could interpret state naturality as a universal covariant vacuum-selection demand; denying that demand avoids the theorem's contradiction without rejecting local covariance. Nothing entails that all local records are impossible.
- **Equivalences / limits / falsifiers:** kinematic/dynamical locality is not automatic. The massless scalar quotient example fails dynamical locality in disconnected 2D `Loc`, though the connected category and dimensions `n≥3` satisfy the theorem. This model and category boundary block overgeneralization. A specific theory must be checked against every hypothesis.

## Theory contrasts relevant to time

| Question | What AQFT supplies | Additional content required |
|---|---|---|
| Local events | Region-indexed observables, inclusion and commutation relations | A rule identifying observable records with events and specifying when they occur |
| Relativity of simultaneity | Covariant spacetime embeddings and local causal order; no preferred foliation required | A claim that coordinate simultaneity tracks metaphysical presentness (or a competing local-becoming account) |
| Localized preparation | Local algebra and, with extra operation structure, local instruments | Preparation energy, success probability, control, finite resources, and disturbance outside the region |
| Signaling versus correlations | Microcausal commutation can forbid controllable spacelike signaling while allowing correlations | A measurement/conditioning account; correlations alone do not establish influence |
| Clock time | A model may include clocks and dynamics | Calibration, resolution, observer coupling, and relation to proper time |
| Arrow/passage/determinism | None follows from locality or modular flow alone | Thermodynamic premises for an arrow; dynamics/initial conditions for determinism; philosophical bridge premises for passage/ontology |

## Nearest predecessors and defeat conditions

The nearest sources are Haag–Kastler (1964) for local observable nets; Reeh–Schlieder (1961), with the precise assumptions represented by Fewster–Rejzner (2020), for vacuum cyclicity; Bisognano–Wichmann (1975, 1976) for wedge modular geometry; Brunetti–Fredenhagen–Verch (2003) and Fewster–Verch (2012) for locally covariant field theory and dynamical locality; and Fewster–Rejzner (2020) for the distinction between cyclicity and operational preparation. The present lane proposes no novelty claim. Any later gap proposal is defeated if it is already a consequence of those source results under the exact same algebra/state/access assumptions, or if it relies on defining “event” or “present” to include the desired ontology.
